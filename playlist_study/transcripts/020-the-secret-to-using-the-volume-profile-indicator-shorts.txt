this is how I use volume in order to make more calculated trading decisions and raise my win percentage once you're on the chart go to the indicators Tab and search volume profile make sure you choose this fixed one right here once you have selected it draw a line from a recent swing low to a recent swing High the volume profile is just a normal volume indicator but flipped on its side so you can see what price levels have the most amount of volume this can be extremely useful as you can literally see where strong points of interest are this red line is called the point of control this is basically just the level where there's the most amount of volume a lot of the time this line almost acts as a magnet where sometimes price will want to head towards it so you can use this line as a support or a pretty good buy Zone then you have these bars on the side the bigger the bar the higher the volume so as you can see at this price there was a lot of volume meaning people are interested in the stock at this level but then as we go up the volume starts to weaken so the price ends up making a double top and falls
