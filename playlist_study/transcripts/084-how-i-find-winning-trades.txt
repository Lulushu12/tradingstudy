I found this one hack that involves liquidity&nbsp; and it will help you win so many more trades. Liquidity is when price makes a low or&nbsp; a high and price breaks that level. Often times price will break this point and&nbsp; reverse and head back to the opposite liquidity&nbsp;&nbsp; level like this. Then repeat the process. But if you have ever traded liquidity like this,&nbsp;&nbsp; you ll quickly figure out&nbsp; it doesn t work every time. So how can we find moves like this, that have&nbsp; a really great chance of being successful? First mark your points of liquidity. Do&nbsp; this by marking the recent lows and highs. Next we want price to break&nbsp; one of these liquidity points. &nbsp; In this instance, it was buy side liquidity. Now we don t want just a normal liquidity break,&nbsp;&nbsp; we want a sharp liquidity break. A sharp liquidity break is when price&nbsp;&nbsp; drops extremely fast past the liquidity and while&nbsp; doing this break, it creates a fair value gap. Once price does this, it needs to create&nbsp; another fair value gap going in the opposite&nbsp;&nbsp; direction right after this move. This is our sign to enter a trade. Enter once price returns to the&nbsp; bullish fair value gap here. Set your take profit at the sell side&nbsp; liquidity and set your stop loss. And just like that, you&nbsp; got an easy winning trade.
