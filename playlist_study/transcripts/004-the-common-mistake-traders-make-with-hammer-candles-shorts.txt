Here s a mistake I see traders make all&nbsp; the time when using the Hammer Candlestick.&nbsp;&nbsp; A bullish hammer has a small body at&nbsp; the top and a long wick at the bottom&nbsp;&nbsp; at least twice the size of the body. A hammer candlestick can be a great&nbsp;&nbsp; indication that the market is about to reverse.&nbsp; As the buyers are eating up all the sellers. The mistake I see lots of traders making is&nbsp; entering right when they see a hammer. Well,&nbsp;&nbsp; what s a hammer without an arm? Nothing. So we need an arm. Which in this case,&nbsp;&nbsp; is going to be a confirmation candle. Wait to&nbsp; see if the next candle breaks the high of the&nbsp;&nbsp; previous candle. If it does, then enter. Even though hammers won t be a win every&nbsp;&nbsp; time. They have an extremely high win rate. If you want to increase that win rate even more.&nbsp;&nbsp; Only trade hammers in an uptrend. You can tell&nbsp; if a market is in an uptrend by adding a 200&nbsp;&nbsp; exponential moving average. If the price&nbsp; is above the 200 ema, there s a hammer,&nbsp;&nbsp; and a confirmation candle. There is a&nbsp; great chance the market will go up.
