Here we have two areas of demand. But which one do we trade? Well, when we first look at this chart, there are a couple things that stand out. First, we see that zone one is the zone that had the strength to break the structure of the previous high, which is a strong sign for zone one. Second, it is above zone one, we have a fair value gap, meaning price will naturally want to come down to this price inefficiency. And the only way it can do that is by breaking through zone 2. Zone 2 is an inducement zone where a lot of new traders will enter when in reality they just become the liquidity and price moves to the real zone zone one and price goes up from there.
