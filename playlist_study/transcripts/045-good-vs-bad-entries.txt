This is how to decipher a&nbsp; good entry vs a bad entry. A very common strategy is to draw a demand&nbsp; zone like this. Where price had a very&nbsp;&nbsp; strong uptrend at this point previously. A lot of traders will just enter as soon&nbsp;&nbsp; as price returns to this demand zone&nbsp; right away. This is a huge mistake. Here you can see we have a demand zone, but had a&nbsp; very big red candle with huge amounts of downwards&nbsp;&nbsp; momentum towards this demand zone. So sure, price hit our demand zone. But when price falls extremely hard like&nbsp; this towards a demand zone, often times&nbsp;&nbsp; it will just break through it. So if you d ended up entering,&nbsp;&nbsp; you would of lost this trade. What we want, is to have price slowly&nbsp;&nbsp; come down to the demand zone. We don t want any&nbsp; sharp movements or big red candles. We just want&nbsp;&nbsp; a gradual decline to the demand zone.
