[00:30] What is the Gartley pattern? The Gartley pattern resembles an M/W shape on the chart, depending on whether it is a bullish or a bearish Gartley and consists of five points on the chart. These points are marked with X, A, B, C, and D. Gartley is a retracement and continuation pattern at the same time, depending on its location in a trend, that is formed when a trend temporarily changes direction before
[01:00] continuing in its original direction. And it provides a low-risk opportunity for traders to enter the market where the pattern finishes and the trend comes back. How to identify the Gartley pattern? The Gartley pattern depends on several movements in price. Every swing has to confirm certain Fibonacci levels. To make it very simple, let’s look at each component of the Gartley structure.
