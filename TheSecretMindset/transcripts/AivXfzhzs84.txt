While Japanese candlesticks require many definitions and flexible rules for most patterns, a heikin-ashi chart works with only the five simple rules. 1. A sequence of green bodies identifies an uptrend, while a sequence of red bodies identifies a downtrend. 2. The uptrend gets stronger with longer green bodies and no lower shadows. The downtrend gets stronger with longer red bodies and no upper shadows.
[02:30] 3. The trend gets weaker with smaller bodies and with the emergence of lower and upper shadows. 4.
