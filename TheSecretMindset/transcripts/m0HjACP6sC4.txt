Triple screen is essentially a trend following system, but the main characteristic, is that it works by analyzing charts at multiple timeframes. The root of elder’s triple screen is that it tries to time trade entries to coincide with market corrections or pullbacks.
[02:00] These can happen in both bull trends and bear trends. The strategy only buys or sells when the signals between different screens at different timeframes are in agreement.
