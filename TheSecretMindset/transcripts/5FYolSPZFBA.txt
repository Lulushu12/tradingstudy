Any stock that is trading under 1 million shares per day can be easily manipulated by market makers (MMs), and they usually trade very slowly. The difference between the bid and the ask is called the spread. Beginner traders usually don’t pay too much attention to how the difference between the bid and the ask of a stock affects their profit. Large stock spreads can damage your profit potential, that’s why you need to choose
