## Summary
This video teaches RSI overbought/oversold as TREND CONTINUATION signals (counter to conventional wisdom). Rather than buying oversold (RSI < 30) or selling overbought (RSI > 70), the strategy does the opposite: enters on pullbacks after RSI extremes, using Fibonacci retracements (23%, 38%, 50%) to identify shallow pullback zones. Entry requires rejection candles (engulfing or pin bar) at Fib levels. Optional 50 EMA filter confirms trend direction. Designed for day trading and scalping on 15-30 minute timeframes with 2:1 to 3:1 risk-reward ratios.

## Strategy 1: RSI Overbought/Oversold Trend Continuation

### Core philosophy [00:00-02:30]

**Rejection of conventional wisdom [00:00-01:30]**
Traditional belief: "Buy oversold, sell overbought" [00:00]
Speaker argues: This doesn't work; do opposite instead [00:00]
RSI values (>70, <30) indicate "past price movements but don't show future direction" [01:00]
"May stay above or below these levels for extended period if trend is strong" [01:00-01:30]

**Revised logic [02:00-02:30]**
- Overbought RSI = extremely strong uptrend moving higher with strong force [02:00]
- Oversold RSI = extremely strong downtrend moving lower with strong force [02:00]
- Strategy profits from "newbies and traders entering wrong way of market" [02:00]
- Market makes shallow pullback to "trap dumb traders" [02:30]

### Indicators and settings
- Relative Strength Index: period 14 (default) [03:00]
- Fibonacci Retracement: 23%, 38%, 50% levels only [03:00]
  - Rationale: "we are interested in shallow pullbacks" [03:00]
- Exponential Moving Average (optional): 50-period [08:30-09:00]

### Context / bias filter

**Without moving average [03:00-04:30]**
Simply identify RSI extreme signals:
- RSI crossing above 70 (overbought) = potential long setup [03:00-03:30]
- RSI crossing below 30 (oversold) = potential short setup [04:00]

**With moving average filter (optional) [08:30-09:30]**
Use 50-period EMA to confirm trend:
- Uptrend: price above 50 EMA, EMA sloping upward [08:30-09:00]
  - Look for overbought RSI signals (>70) for long entries [09:00]
  - Want to trade "direction of moving average" [09:00]
- Downtrend: price below 50 EMA, EMA sloping downward [09:00-09:30]
  - Look for oversold RSI signals (<30) for short entries [09:00-09:30]

### Entry trigger

**Buy setup (Long) [03:30-04:00]**
1. RSI crosses above 70 [03:00-03:30]
2. Wait for pullback/shallow correction [03:30]
3. Apply Fib retracement from most recent swing low to highest point of up move [03:30]
4. Enter at first sign of REJECTION at one of Fib levels (23%, 38%, or 50%) [03:30]

Entry candle pattern: "Engulfing candlesticks rejecting a fib level" or "pin bar" [13:00-13:30]

Example: "Massive engulfing bar rejecting the 38 fib level" [05:00-05:30]
Another example: "Bullish engulfing candle" after price went below buy area [06:00-06:30]

**Sell setup (Short) [04:00-04:30]**
1. RSI crosses below 30 [04:00]
2. Wait for pullback/shallow upward correction [04:00]
3. Apply Fib retracement from most recent swing high to lowest point of down move [04:00]
4. Enter at first sign of REJECTION at one of Fib levels [04:00]

Entry candle pattern: Engulfing candles or pin bars [13:00-13:30]

Example: "Engulfing bar rejecting the 38 fib level" [06:30-07:00]

**Key timing principle [10:00-10:30]**
"Don't enter immediately after spotting overbought RSI. Wait for small pullback and then make move. Allow market to trap novice traders" [10:00-10:30]
This creates "many trapped traders caught on wrong side" [10:30]

### Stop loss

**Above pullback high (short trades) [07:00-07:30]**
