## Summary
This video presents a strategy based on identifying major structure shifts (breakouts of significant swing highs/lows) and then mapping premium/discount zones using the Gann Box tool with 25%, 50%, and 75% levels. Traders buy when price retraces into discount territory (especially the 25% level) with confluence from static support/resistance, and sell when price rallies into premium territory (75% level). The strategy assumes institutions operate within these value zones and that convergence of multiple signals creates high-probability setups.
