## Summary

Teaches a breakout retest strategy that waits for price to retrace back to newly broken resistance or support levels before entering. Emphasizes marking breakout zones as areas rather than rigid lines, validating with volume patterns (high on break, low on pullback, rising again at retest), and using candlestick patterns (engulfing, pin bars) at the retest zone for entry confirmation. Focuses on how smart money repositions during retests, flushing out weak retail traders.

## Strategy 1: Breakout Retest Trading

### Indicators and settings

- Volume (on breakout candle): high volume spikes on candle that clears the zone [07:00]
- Volume (on pullback): lower than breakout volume [07:30]
- Volume (on retest): rising again to confirm buyers defending area [08:00]
- Pin bar pattern: NOT STATED on specific wick-to-body ratio; described only as "small body with long upper or lower wick" [11:30]; "bigger the pin, the more force" [12:00]
- Engulfing candle: NOT STATED on specific parameters [09:30]

### Context / bias filter

- Market needs a "clean breakout" [00:00], NOT STATED what constitutes "clean"
- Look for noticeable gap in close price through level, not just a wick [07:00], NOT STATED threshold for "noticeable"
- Volume must spike on candle that clears the zone [07:00]
- Low volume breaks are suspect [07:00]
- Mark breakout zone as area/cluster instead of single line [05:00]

### Entry trigger

- Price retraces back to the newly established breakout zone [01:30]
- Zone defined as "highlight the whole region rather than a specific price" when cluster of highs/lows exists [05:00-05:30]
- At retest zone, must see confirmation via candlestick pattern: "engulfing candle that swallows up the previous one" OR "pin bar wick rejection off the area" [09:30-10:00]
- Pin bar shows "traders are fighting over that price" [11:30]
- Volume at retest must be above average [12:00]
- When using lower timeframe (e.g., 30min chart) for entry confirmation within H4 bias, spot engulfing candlestick right at support zone on lower timeframe [10:30]

### Stop loss

- Above the engulfing candle at retest [11:00]
- OR a few points above the whole resistance zone [11:00], NOT STATED exact number of points

### Invalidation / skip conditions

- If volume stays high as price pulls back toward breakout point, sellers still in control, likely a trap [07:30]
- Second retest of a breakout zone usually isn't as strong as the initial retest [14:30-15:30]
- If price violates retest zone twice, likelihood of new wave decreases a lot [14:00]
