## Summary
This video teaches Volume Spread Analysis (VSA), a framework for trading using volume and price patterns to identify smart money accumulation and distribution phases. It covers trend continuation patterns (Break of Structure), double top/bottom patterns with liquidity runs, change of character reversals, head and shoulders patterns, double liquidity runs, Wyckoff accumulation/distribution phases, and high-volume breakout strategies. The core idea is that volume disagreement with price signals potential reversals, while volume validation signals continuation.
