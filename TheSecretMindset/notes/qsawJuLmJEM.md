## Summary
This video presents a breakout and retest trading strategy. After price breaks through a key level on high volume with a clear gap, the strategy waits for price to return (retest) to the broken level. Entry occurs when price retests the broken level and shows confirmation via price action patterns (pin bar or engulfing candle) and volume confirmation. The strategy prioritizes the first retest as highest probability.

## Strategy 1: Break and Retest with Volume and Price Action Confirmation

### Indicators and settings
- No indicators required; price, volume, and candlestick patterns only
- Optional: Higher timeframe confirmation

### Context / bias filter
NOT STATED explicitly. Implied: wait for significant breakout levels (support/resistance) to be identified first.

### Entry trigger
1. **Identify Clear Breakout** [06:00]:
   - Price must break support or resistance level [06:00]
   - Must show clear closing gap breach [06:00]
   - Must be followed by strong, rising volume [06:30]
   - NOT just touching the level; must be a decisive break

2. **Wait for Pullback** [06:30-07:30]:
   - Price pulls back to retest the broken level [07:00]
   - Pullback should occur on low or decreasing volume [07:00]
   - This signals weak sellers (on retest) vs. strong buyers (on breakout)

3. **Confirm Entry at Retest** [09:00-11:00]:
   - At retest level, price must show confirmation pattern:
     - **Pin Bar** [11:00-11:30]: Rejection candle with wick sticking out from all others; needs above average volume
     - **Engulfing Candle** [09:00-10:30]: One candle completely engulfs previous candle; shows force
   - OR price action showing market returning to technical level

4. **Volume at Retest** [07:30]:
   - As market pulls away from retest, volume should increase [07:30]
   - Strong volume on the move away from retest = confirmation that the breakout is real

### Stop loss
Place above (for shorts) or below (for longs) the breakout zone, NOT above individual signal candle [09:30-10:00]
- Using signal candle (engulfing bar) for stop can cause premature exits [10:00-10:30]
- Zone-based stop gives market room to develop

### Invalidation / skip conditions
If retest doesn't show price action confirmation (no pin bar, no engulfing, weak volume), skip the trade [11:30]

## Additional Concepts

### Why Retests Occur [01:00-03:00]
Smart money entered early before retail breakout traders. After breakout, smart money takes profits, causing price to return to breakout level. Breakout traders close positions as price approaches their entry, adding sell orders. This retest is market "resetting itself" or "flushing out weak positions" [03:00].

### Zone vs. Line Approach [04:30-05:30]
- Using horizontal line at breakout point: Don't know which high will be retested [05:00]
- Using zone: Covers multiple highs, removes discretion [05:00]
- Zones give breathing room for market messiness [05:30]

### First Retest vs. Subsequent Retests [12:30-14:00]
- **First retest**: Highest probability because breakout traders still in market [13:00]
- **Second retest**: Lower probability because original breakout traders have exited; fewer stops to hunt; less order flow [13:00-13:30]
- Example shown: Market bounces at first retest but breaks zone on second test [13:30]

### Concept: Order Flow [13:00-13:30]
- When technical level hit once, its significance decreases [13:00]
- Fewer stops at second touch = lower probability of reaction [13:00]
