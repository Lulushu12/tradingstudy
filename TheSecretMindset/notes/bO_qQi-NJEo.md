## Summary
This video presents the "Triple Line Setup," a 1-minute scalping strategy using two EMAs (9 and 21) and one recent support/resistance level. The strategy exploits the "pocket" (space between EMAs) as a confluence zone where support/resistance becomes high probability. A "Recovery Rule" allows entries even when price slightly overshoots levels.

## Strategy 1: Triple Line 1-Minute Scalping

### Indicators and settings
- Exponential Moving Average 9: period 9 [00:30]
- Exponential Moving Average 21: period 21 [00:30]
- Relative Strength Index: period NOT STATED, used for 50-line cross only [04:00]
- Support/Resistance: one recent level only, tested within last hour or fresh untouched level [01:00-01:30]

### Context / bias filter

**Session filter [10:00-10:30]**
"Only scalp during London or New York session. Outside these windows, don't even open your charts" [10:30]
Asian session is "dead zone" with no volume; support levels meaningless [08:30-09:00]

**EMA angle filter [09:00-10:00]**
"Always check that the 9 EMA is angled at least 30 degrees in your trade direction. If it's flat, skip the trade" [10:30]
Flat EMAs indicate no trend, no momentum, trading in chop [09:00-10:00]

**The EMA Pocket [00:30-02:00]**
The space between 9 EMA and 21 EMA is the "magnetic zone" or "sweet spot." Price repeatedly pulled back to this pocket like gravity [01:00]
Only trade support/resistance levels when price is WITHIN this pocket [01:30]
Example: "Price hits support and it's right between the EMAs. 10 pips in 5 minutes. No guessing. Just pure confluence" [01:30]
Counterexample: "Price hits similar support level but below both EMAs. I don't touch it" [01:30-02:00]

### Entry trigger

**Primary entry rule [03:00-03:30]**
1. Price touches support/resistance level while sitting between 9 and 21 EMAs (the pocket) [03:00]
2. Wait for price to settle between the 2 lines [03:00]
3. Enter on the close of a new red candle (for shorts) or green candle (for longs) [03:00-03:30]
4. Do NOT enter on the wick or touch; wait for candle close [03:00]

Rationale: "When price races down and hits support, it has momentum behind it (bearish momentum). You're trying to catch a falling knife. But if you wait for price to touch support AND be between the 2 lines, that's double confirmation" [02:30-03:00]

**RSI confirmation filter [04:00-05:00]**
When unsure about a setup (something feels off even though confluence looks good):
- Check RSI position relative to 50-line: "Dead simple rule. RSI above 50 equals only take long trades. RSI below 50 equals only take short trades" [04:30]
- Rationale: RSI shows momentum direction [04:30]
- "Trade price first. When you need a tiebreaker, when you're 50-50 on setup, let RSI be your confirmation" [05:30]

### Recovery Rule for Imperfect Signals [06:30-08:00]

If price pierces through support/resistance level but recovers within 5 candles AND gets back between the 2 EMA lines, the signal is still valid [07:00]

Example 1 [07:30-08:00]: "Price shot through resistance by a few pips. Looks like broken setup. But watch what happens. Few candles later, price back below resistance. Sitting between moving average pocket. That was 20 pips profit scalping trade"

Explanation: "On a 1-minute timeframe, market doesn't draw perfect lines. Neither should you. Support isn't a line. It's a zone. Sometimes price needs to hunt stops below support before reversing" [08:00]

### Take profit / exit

**Two-part exit system [11:00-12:00]**

**Part 1: Partial profit at 10 pips [11:00-11:30]**
- "Take 50% off at 10 pips. Always. No exceptions. At 10 pips, you sell half" [11:00-11:30]
- Rationale: "On 1-minute timeframe, 10 pips is a real move. Covers spread and gives profit. Removes emotion" [11:30]

**Part 2: Remaining position exit [11:30-12:00]**
After taking 50% profit, move stop to breakeven immediately [11:30]
Exit remaining position when either:
- Price hits next resistance level, OR
- Price closes below 21 EMA [11:30-12:00]
Whichever occurs first [12:00]

Result: "Never turn a winner into a loser. Partial profit guarantees money made. Breakeven stop protects from reversal" [12:00]

### Invalidation / skip conditions

**Three deadly traps that MUST be skipped [08:30-10:00]**

1. **Dead zones / Asian session [08:30-09:00]**
   - Perfect setup appears but price doesn't move for 20 minutes then stops out
   - No volume, support levels meaningless, price walks through them [09:00]

2. **Flat EMAs / No trend [09:00-09:30]**
   - EMAs horizontal with no angle
   - Bounces few pips then reverses
   - Like "trying to surf in swimming pool. No waves means no moves" [09:30-10:00]
   - Filter: 9 EMA must be angled at least 30 degrees in trade direction [10:30]

3. **Chasing price after it's moved 10+ pips [09:30-10:00]**
   - FOMO entry far from level
   - "You just bought at the top"
   - Risk-reward garbage (risking 10 pips to make 5 pips) [10:00]
   - Rule: "Never, ever enter if price is more than 5 pips from your level" [10:30]
