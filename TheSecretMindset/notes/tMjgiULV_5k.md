## Notable wave characteristics and rules described

**Three Unbreakable Rules** [02:00-02:30]:
1. Wave two cannot retrace more than 100% of wave one
2. Wave four cannot move into the territory of wave one
3. Wave three cannot be the shortest wave amongst waves one, three, and five

**Wave 1 Characteristics** [03:00-04:00]:
- Begins after long sustained downtrend; market sentiment very bearish
- Viewed by market as "bear market rally"; traders short it [03:00-03:30]
- "Generally the least predictive wave"
- Duration and length unknown in advance
- "No way to know how long the wave will last" [03:30]
- Subdivides into five smaller waves; sometimes appears as leading diagonal structure

**Wave 2 Characteristics** [04:00-04:30]:
- Retraces 50%, 61%, or 78% of wave one [04:00]
- Cannot retrace below wave one start (unbreakable rule)
- Traders believe downtrend continuing; market fails to break wave one low
- Forms as zigzag or flat correction (ABC pattern, three waves) [04:30]

**Wave 3 Characteristics** [05:00-06:30]:
- "Usually the biggest and most powerful wave"
- "Offers the largest potential for profit"
- Psychology: growing optimism; trend shift becomes evident
- Prices accelerate upward as traders recognize bullish sentiment
- Sellers' stops triggered at wave one high, adding momentum
- "Offers very little in the way of pullbacks; when present, tend to be quite shallow" [06:00]
- "Extend 1.618 times the length of wave one" (measured from end of wave two) [06:00-06:30]
- Alternative projection: "2.618 times the length of wave one" [06:30]

**Wave 4 Characteristics** [06:30-08:00]:
- Shallower retracement, often 38% or 50% of wave three [06:30-07:00]
- "Should not overlap into the territory of wave one" (unbreakable rule) [07:00]
- "Profit-taking wave"; traders liquidate positions [07:30]
- "Range bound" with "whipsawing price action"; "false breakouts on either side" [08:00]
- "More likely to lose money during these directionless market conditions" [08:00]
- Forms as triangle, flat formation, zigzag pattern, or combination [07:30-08:00]

**Wave 5 Characteristics** [08:00-10:00]:
- "Final leg within the impulse sequence"
- "Almost everyone is bullish"; "retail wave" [08:30]
- "Momentum less dynamic than wave three"
- "Velocity of price movement more subdued than wave three" [08:30]
- Technical divergences form (RSI, Stochastics, MACD) between price and momentum indicators [09:00]
- "Lower volume compared to wave three" [09:30]
- Terminates at length equivalent to wave one [09:30-10:00]
- Alternative projection: "61% the length of wave one" [09:30-10:00]

**Wave A Characteristics** [10:00-10:30]:
- Starts corrective sequence (ABC pattern follows impulse)
- Traders still bullish; "consider wave A pullback as just another minor retracement"
- "Somewhat similar to wave one in its structure"
- Market participants heavily positioned [10:30]
- "Often a sharp move"; subdivides as five-wave or three-wave pattern [10:30]

**Wave B Characteristics** [11:00-11:30]:
- "Often considered a bull trap"
- Retraces wave A by 50%, 61%, or 78% [11:30]
- "Fairly short-lived"; fails to surpass wave five swing high [11:00]
- "Often create double top formation"; tries to test wave five extreme but fails [11:00-11:30]
- Subdivision often zigzag or triangle [11:30]
- Possible trade: "fade the wave B price move as it nears its terminal point" [12:00]

**Wave C Characteristics** [12:00-12:30]:
- "Final leg within entire corrective structure"
- Often equivalent to length of wave A [12:30]
- Projections: 1.27 times the length of wave A [12:30]
- Alternative projection: 1.61 times the length of wave A [12:30]

**Wave 2 and Wave 4 Alternation** [13:30-14:00]:
- Must display alternation "in as many ways as possible"
- Alternation by price: wave 2 vs. wave 4 obviously smaller/larger
- Alternation by time: one takes much more/less time than the other
- Alternation by pattern: different formation types (ABC vs. complex ABC vs. triangle; flat vs. zigzag)
