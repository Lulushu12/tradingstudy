## Summary
This video teaches a Flag Pattern continuation strategy using 21/55 EMA as a trend filter. The 21 EMA crossing above the 55 EMA signals uptrend (look for bull flags); crossing below signals downtrend (look for bear flags). Entries occur on flag breakouts confirmed by engulfing candles or pin bars at the EMA levels. Confluence elements include Stochastic divergence, volume confirmation, and candlestick patterns forming at dynamic support/resistance created by the EMAs.

- 21 EMA below 55 EMA: market in downtrend, look for Bear Flag Patterns only [03:30]

### Pattern Recognition

**Bull Flag Pattern [01:30-02:00]**
- Forms during bullish trends
- Begins with bullish trending move (flag pole)
- Pauses and turns into minor bearish correction (flag)
- Tops and bottoms of correction are parallel
- Continuation pattern: likely to push price further in direction of pole

**Bear Flag Pattern [01:00-01:30]**
- Forms during bearish trends
- Starts with bearish trend impulse (flag pole)
- Turns into correction directed upwards (flag)
- Tops and bottoms evenly distributed, creating parallel channel
- Continuation pattern: likely to continue downward after breakout

### Entry trigger

**Conservative Entry [06:00-06:30]**
- Wait for price to break the flag boundary (breakout confirmation)
- Bull flag: buy when candle closes above upper boundary [04:30]
- Bear flag: sell when candle closes below lower boundary [04:30]

**Aggressive Entry [06:00-06:30]**
- Enter before breakout confirmation
- Take trade at close of candlestick pattern inside flag
- Or at break of high or low of the candlestick pattern
- Higher risk-to-reward but more whipsaws

**Ideal Confluence Entry [06:30-07:00]**
Price rejecting dynamic area formed by 2 EMAs + candlestick pattern (engulfing or pin bar) + flag breakout
- "The ideal scenario would be a pin bar or an engulfing candle that forms right when the price breaks the flag" [06:30]
- Confluence increases probability [07:00]

**Candlestick Pattern Triggers [05:00-05:30]**
Entry triggered by engulfing candle or pin bar forming at dynamic support/resistance (the EMAs):
- Bullish candlestick forming on 21 EMA suggests EMA has held as dynamic support [05:30]
- Bullish candlestick suggests market has good chance of going up [05:30]

### Stop loss
"Beyond the most extreme swing within the Flag structure" [04:30]
- For bull flag: place stop below the lowest bottom in the flag [04:30]
- For bear flag: place stop above the highest top in the flag [implied]
- Rule: "If price breaches opposite side of breakout, immediately exit, pattern most likely false" [04:30]

### Take profit / exit

Two profit targets based on Flag geometry [02:30-03:00]:

**Target 1: Measured Move**
- Distance equal to size of flag (vertical distance between upper and lower channel within flag)
- Apply this distance starting from breakout point
- First target at end of this distance [02:30]

**Target 2: Pole Size**
- Measure vertical distance between high and low of the flag pole
- Apply this distance starting from breakout point
- Second target at end of this distance [03:00]

**Profit Taking Strategy**
"I would suggest taking profits at each target level to reduce risk and book profits" [05:00]
