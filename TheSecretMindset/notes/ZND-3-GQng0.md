## Summary
A stock screening framework for identifying the best stocks to day trade based on 10 specific filters applied in Finviz screener. The framework prioritizes liquid, volatile stocks trading in established uptrends with strong recent momentum (gaps) and positive price extremes (near 52-week highs). The combination of filters is designed to narrow the universe of tradeable stocks to high-probability candidates rather than to define specific entry/exit rules for trades themselves.

## Instruments and timeframes stated
- Markets: U.S. stocks [01:30]
- Timeframes: day trading timeframe (intraday) [00:00]
- Sessions/hours: pre-market and after-hours activity referenced [08:00]

## Screening Framework: 10-Filter Stock Selection System

This is not a trading strategy but rather an instrument selection methodology. The criteria are filters applied to identify candidates:

### Filter 1: Average Volume
- Metric: Daily average volume over unspecified period
- Parameter: Over 1 million shares [01:00]
- Rationale: Ensures liquidity; allows entry and exit without major price impact [01:00]

### Filter 2: Stock Price
- Parameter: Over $30 [01:30]
- Rationale: Avoids lower-priced stocks with smaller market caps, fewer shares outstanding, and higher susceptibility to price swings and manipulation [01:30]; attracts institutional investors [01:30]

### Filter 3: Relative Volume
- Metric: Current volume compared to 30-day average [02:30]
- Parameters: 
  - Conservative: Over 1 (stock trading at average volume) [02:30]
  - Aggressive: Over 2 (stock trading at more than twice normal volume) [02:30]
- Rationale: Increased volume indicates increased interest and potential for significant price action [02:30]

### Filter 4: Beta
- Metric: Stock volatility relative to S&P 500 [03:30]
- Baseline: Beta = 1 equals market volatility [04:00]
- Parameters:
  - Standard: Over 1 (more volatile than general market) [04:00]
  - Aggressive: Over 1.5 or Over 2 [04:30]
- Example: Beta of 1.5 means stock should move 50% more than market [04:00]
- Rationale: High-beta stocks make larger moves in shorter time periods [03:30]

### Filter 5: Market Capitalization
- Parameter: Over 1 billion dollars [05:00]
- Rationale: Established, actively-traded names; get attention of major Wall Street players; more stable; less prone to manipulation [05:00]; solid fundamentals and track record [05:30]

### Filter 6: Average True Range (ATR)
- Metric: Average price movement range over 14 days [05:30]
- Parameter: Over 1 [06:00]
- Rationale: High ATR indicates significant price swings on regular basis; creates opportunities for day traders [06:00]

### Filter 7: Float
- Metric: Number of shares available for trading on open market [06:30]
- Parameter: Over 10 million [06:30]
- Rationale: Ensures enough shares available for trading; stock less likely to experience sudden, erratic price swings [06:30]

### Filter 8: 50-Day Simple Moving Average (SMA)
- Indicators: 50-day SMA and 20-day SMA [07:30]
- Criteria: Stock trading above both 50-day and 20-day SMAs [07:30]
- Rationale: 
  - 50-day SMA: longer-term trend indicator; less sensitive to short-term fluctuations; when it changes course, signals significant trend change [07:30]
  - 20-day SMA: shorter-term indicator; more sensitive to recent price action; identifies potential near-term trend changes [07:30]
  - Alignment above both = well-established uptrend with bullish momentum [08:00]

### Filter 9: Gap Up
- Metric: Stock opening significantly higher than previous day's close [08:00]
- Parameter: Gap Up Over 1% [08:30]
- Rationale: Indicates strong overnight buying pressure and demand; sign of momentum potentially continuing throughout trading day [08:30]; especially powerful on high volume with wide range [08:30]
- Context: Combined with 50-day SMA and broad market uptrend, increases likelihood of continued momentum [09:00]

### Filter 10: 52-Week High/Low
- Metric: Stock's highest and lowest prices in past 52 weeks [09:30]
- Parameter: 0%-10% below high (stocks trading within 10% of 52-week high) [09:30]
- Rationale: Stocks near 52-week highs show bullish sentiment and momentum; may be on verge of breaking out to new highs [10:00]; identifies stocks close to breakout without having made the break yet [10:00]

### Combining the Filters
When all 10 filters are applied together, they create a system that identifies "the most promising stocks for day trading" [10:30]. The speaker notes that sometimes applying all filters results in very small list or no stocks; in these cases, recommend adjusting (loosening) some filters, particularly the "gap up" filter or "52-Week High/Low" filter to expand candidate pool [11:00].

## Notable claims and caveats
Speaker emphasizes finding "BEST stocks to day trade" with "highest profit potential" [00:00]. States: "The market is always changing, and you'll need to be flexible in your approach" [10:30]. Notes that the framework is not about trading the stocks once selected—it's about selection; actual trading strategies are covered in other videos [11:30].

Key screening priorities: liquidity first, then volatility, then alignment with overall market trend [00:30]. Avoids lower-priced stocks due to manipulation risk [01:30]. Prefers institutional-grade stocks [01:30]. Emphasizes that volume + price movement = profit opportunities [00:30].

This is purely a screening/filtering tool with no trading strategy, entry signals, stop loss, take profit, or mechanizability assessment. The screened stocks then require a separate trading strategy to trade them.
