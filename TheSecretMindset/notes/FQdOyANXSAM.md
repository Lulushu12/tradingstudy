## Strategy 1: Pin Bar Reversal at Support/Resistance

### Indicators and settings

No indicators used. Price action only.

### Context / bias filter

The pin bar must occur at a strong area of support or resistance [06:00]. Preferably, the pin bar should form at "confluent levels of previous price action" [07:00], meaning areas where multiple price levels (previous highs/lows, moving average resistance, horizontal levels) converge. The higher the confluence, the higher the accuracy [07:00-07:30]. For bearish pin bars, price must first move higher before the pin bar forms [04:30]. For bullish pin bars, price must first move lower before the pin bar forms (implied reversal principle).

### Entry trigger

Trade the pin bar as a reversal signal [04:00]. For bearish pin bars: enter after the reversal pin bar completes and price has moved higher first [04:30]. For bullish pin bars: enter after price has moved lower and the reversal pin bar completes (opposite logic). The pin bar itself must meet validity criteria stated below [03:00].

### Invalidation / skip conditions

Do not trade pin bars as continuation signals [04:00-05:00]; treat them only as reversals. Do not trade "less well-defined pin bars and smaller pin bars" [03:30]; only trade "large and obvious candles" [08:30] that "stick out" from the chart. Do not trade pin bars in isolation without support/resistance confirmation [06:00-06:30]; they lack edge without confluence. Do not trade pin bars that are "hidden in the charts" or hard to spot [08:00]. Do not trade if there is insufficient room to move; for example, if the pin bar is right at recent highs with no space to trade higher [06:30].
