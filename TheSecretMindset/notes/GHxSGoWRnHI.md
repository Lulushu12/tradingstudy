## Summary

### Indicators and settings

- Stochastic Oscillator: period = NOT STATED
- Fibonacci Retracement levels: 23.6%, 38.2% mentioned [03:30-04:00]

### Context / bias filter

Trade on weekly or monthly timeframes only [03:00-03:30]. Identify the main trend first [03:00]. Wait for price to retrace to the 23.6% or 38.2% Fibonacci retracement level during an uptrend [03:30-04:00]. A hidden divergence must form on the stochastic oscillator during this retracement phase [03:30].

A hidden divergence in an uptrend: price makes higher highs but the stochastic oscillator makes lower highs, suggesting the uptrend is continuing with strong momentum [03:30-04:00]. (Implied for downtrends: price makes lower lows but stochastic makes higher lows.)

### Entry trigger

Enter a long trade when:
1. Price is in an established uptrend
2. Price retraces to 23.6% or 38.2% Fibonacci level [03:30-04:00]
3. Stochastic oscillator shows a hidden divergence (price higher but stochastic lower) [03:30-04:30]

The speaker shows multiple examples: gold weekly chart, Aussie Dollar weekly chart, Apple weekly chart [03:30-04:30], all confirming uptrend continuation after this setup.

## Strategy 2: Carry Trade (Interest Rate Differential)

### Indicators and settings

- Interest rates of two countries (economic data, not technical indicator)
- Fibonacci Retracement levels: 38.2% mentioned [06:00]

### Context / bias filter

Identify currency pairs with significant interest rate differentials [05:00-05:30]. Buy the currency with the higher interest rate; sell the currency with the lower interest rate [05:00-05:30]. On the technical side, identify the main monthly trend and support/resistance levels [06:00-06:30]. Wait for price to retrace to Fibonacci level (38.2% mentioned [06:00]) within an uptrend [06:00-06:30].

### Entry trigger

Enter a long position when:
1. Interest rate differential favors the higher-yielding currency [05:00-05:30]
2. Technical setup confirms: price retraces to 38.2% Fib level in an uptrend [06:00-06:30]
3. Price rejects the retracement level and begins reversing in the direction of the main trend [06:00-06:30]

The example: AUD/JPY carry trade because Australia has higher interest rates than Japan [06:00]. Enter long after price retraces and shows rejection of the 38.2 level [06:00-06:30].

Do not trade if interest rate differential is small or unfavorable. Do not trade if technical setup does not confirm (price not at Fib level, or not in an uptrend).

## Position Trading Advantages (Educational Context)

The speaker emphasizes several advantages of position trading [01:00-02:30]:
1. Eliminates market noise from short-term price fluctuations and rumors [01:00]
2. Requires less screen time and monitoring [01:00-01:30]
3. Reduces overtrading temptation; fewer trades executed [01:30-02:00]
4. Reduces daily stress; positions have "plenty of room to survive market volatility" [02:00]
5. Signals are more relevant on higher timeframes; divergences and crossovers are more reliable [02:30]
