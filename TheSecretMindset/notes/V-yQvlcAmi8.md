## Summary

The video teaches how to identify and trade hidden divergences as trend continuation signals. Hidden divergences occur when price and an oscillator indicator fail to confirm each other: in an uptrend, price makes a higher low while the oscillator makes a lower low; in a downtrend, price makes a lower high while the oscillator makes a higher high. These are contrasted with regular divergences (which signal reversals) and represent the higher-probability pattern. Entry confirmation can include RSI crossovers, candlestick patterns (pin bars, engulfing), and trendline/resistance breakouts.
