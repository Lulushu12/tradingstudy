## Summary

This video presents a strategy that combines RSI trend lines with price action divergence. The technique involves drawing trend lines on the RSI oscillator itself (connecting swing lows in uptrends or swing highs in downtrends), then entering trades when the RSI trend line breaks while price is still trading below its own price trend line. This creates a divergence between momentum and price, providing a leading signal before price reversal. The strategy emphasizes trading in the direction of the primary trend and using support/resistance levels for confirmation. Stop losses are placed near the breakout candle or above/below recent swings.
