## Summary

This video teaches a trend-following strategy that enters on the first pullback immediately after a trend reversal. The core method identifies when an old trend has lost momentum (lower high, then break of that level), then enters when the new trend begins to pull back before continuing in the new direction. The strategy uses ABCD pattern recognition, trend bar confirmation, and timeframe correlation. The speaker emphasizes that first pullbacks are low-risk, high-reward entries that catch the majority of a trending move without needing to pick exact tops or bottoms.

## Strategy 1: First Pullback Trading

### Indicators and settings

No technical indicators required. Pure price action: identifies ABCD patterns, trend bars, and reversal patterns.

### Context / bias filter

"The first pullback in a new trend direction is also the final test of the extreme in the old trend direction" [01:00-01:30]. The strategy only works after a clear reversal has been identified. Not applicable in sideways/range markets.

**Prior setup:** Market was in an old uptrend (or downtrend). First indication of new pullback is a lower high (Point C) showing weakness [02:00-02:30].

### Entry trigger

Entry occurs after the full ABCD pattern is complete:

**Pattern Definition [02:30-03:00]:**
- Point A: swing extreme in old trend direction (e.g., swing high in uptrend)
- Point B: first lower low (or break below Point A) [04:00]
- Point C: first lower high [02:30]
- Point D: second lower low that breaks below Point B [03:00]

"A pullback must include CD wave where point D moves beyond point B, or the pullback is invalid" [03:00]. "Price must move below Point B for confirmation of the first pullback in the new trend direction" [03:00].

**Confirmation Signs [03:30-05:30]:**

**Sign 1: Break Below Point A [04:00]**
"A break below Point A represents a confirmation that a pullback in the old direction has failed" [03:30-04:00]. This is the first lower low, signaling trend loss [04:00].

**Sign 2: Trend Bar Confirmation [04:30-05:00]**
"A clear break below Point A and one of the best ways to identify seller's conviction is by finding one or more long bodied bearish trend bars breaking through point A" [04:30-05:00]. "Trend bars are great to show strength in the market...especially at the start of a new trend" [05:00]. Trend bars with small tails indicate strong, one-sided momentum [05:00-05:30].

**Sign 3: Timeframe Correlation [05:30-06:30]**
Use higher timeframe to identify the extreme, lower timeframe to pinpoint pullback failure. Example [05:30-06:30]: Daily chart identifies price extreme (far from moving average), 4-hour chart shows double top (final test, lower high), then break below that creates the pullback entry signal. "If you stayed on the daily timeframe, you might not have spotted the pullback and would have missed a good pullback entry" [06:00-06:30].

**Sign 4: Reversal Pattern Context [06:30-09:30]**
First pullback often occurs immediately after reversal patterns. Patterns to recognize [07:00-09:30]:

- **Double Top/Bottom and Neckline:** Double top signals final test of extreme, followed by new lower low (Point B) and new lower high (Point C). Retest of neckline (old support becoming resistance) is an additional confirmation [07:00-07:30].

- **Range Market Breakout:** Range must be formed by 2 touches on either side. Point B is first break outside range (first lower low). Point C is retest of resistance (previous support). Point D completes the pattern [08:00-08:30].

- **Head and Shoulders:** Three-topped pattern (left shoulder, head, right shoulder). Two lows form neckline (horizontal or slope). First pullback corresponds to right shoulder. Critical: Point D must be below Point B, and both must be below neckline [08:30-09:30].

### Stop loss

NOT STATED in general framework. Implied to be above the recent resistance level (e.g., above Point C or neckline in reversal patterns).

### Take profit / exit

NOT STATED in general framework. Implied to be at next resistance level or at the extreme of the old trend (now become support in the new trend).

### Invalidation / skip conditions

1. **Incomplete ABCD Pattern:** If Point D does not form (price does not break below Point B), the pullback setup is invalid [03:00-03:30].

2. **False Breakouts:** Point C can be a false break of a range. Confirmation only comes by watching Point D closely [08:00-08:30]. Range breakouts can turn into double bottoms if price reverses above the neckline [09:00-09:30].

3. **Reversal Pattern Failure:** If Head and Shoulders neckline holds (price doesn't break below it), the pattern fails [09:00-09:30]. Pattern could reverse into a double or triple bottom instead [09:00-09:30].

4. **Non-Trend Markets:** Strategy is not described for range-bound or consolidation markets. Reversal patterns must be confirmed before entering [01:30-02:00].

## Additional Pattern Recognition Details

### Double Top Pattern [07:00-07:30]

Structure:
- Point A: swing high in uptrend
- Point B: pullback low (first lower low)
- Point C: second high testing the first high (creating double top, a new lower high vs. first test)
- Neckline: the low between the two tops

Entry signal: Price breaks below neckline and retest of neckline.

### Range Market Breakout [08:00-08:30]

Structure:
- Two touches on either side define the range
- Point B: first break outside range (first lower low)
- Point C: first retest of resistance (previous support becomes resistance)
- Point D: second lower low, completing pullback pattern

### Head and Shoulders [08:30-09:30]

Structure:
- Left Shoulder: first top
- Head: second (highest) top
- Right Shoulder: third top
- Neckline: connects the two lows between the three tops (can be horizontal or slope)

Critical condition: Point D (the low after right shoulder) must be below neckline, which is below Point B (left shoulder low).
