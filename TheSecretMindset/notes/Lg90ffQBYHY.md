## Summary
A reversal trading strategy based on the AB=CD harmonic pattern, which uses two impulsive waves (AB and CD) of equal length separated by a retracement wave (BC) at Fibonacci ratios. The pattern is identified by measuring Fibonacci levels: BC retraces 61.8% of AB, and CD extends to 127.2% of BC. Entry occurs when price bounces from the D point extension level. Stop loss is placed beyond the CD extreme, and minimum profit target is a move equal to the CD leg size (100% CD retracement).

## Strategy 1: AB=CD Harmonic Pattern Reversal

### Indicators and settings
- Fibonacci ratios (manual measurement or harmonic indicator/scanner)
- BC retracement: 61.8% of AB leg [01:30, 03:00, 05:30, 06:30]
- CD extension: 127.2% (1.272) of BC leg [01:30, 03:00, 06:30]
- AB and CD legs: equal in size [04:30, 05:00, 07:00]
- AB and CD: approximately equal in time to develop [11:00]
- Optional: momentum indicator for divergence detection

### Context / bias filter
**Pattern Recognition** [02:00-03:00]: Identify ABCD points by finding swing moves on the chart. "You find the swing points by looking where the market is making a large move and then reverses back and then makes again a new swing and again reverses. This is the four-step setup, which will make the ABCD pattern on the chart." [02:00-02:30]. Can use a harmonic indicator or scanner to detect patterns [02:30].

**Bullish ABCD (expects upward reversal)** [04:00-05:30]:
- Price has been moving down
- A = start point (top, highest)
- B = end of first downswing (lowest)
- C = retraces back up from B to 61.8% of the AB move [05:00-05:30]
- D = forms below B point at 127.2% extension of BC wave [05:00]
- Write down rules: AB wave top-to-bottom, BC retraces down to 61.8% of AB, D forms at 127.2% extension of BC [05:00-05:30]

**Bearish ABCD (expects downward reversal)** [05:30-07:00]:
- Price has been moving up
- A = start point (bottom, lowest)
- B = end of first upswing (highest)
- C = retraces back down from B to 61.8% of the AB move [06:00-06:30]
- D = forms above B point at 127.2% extension of BC wave [06:00]
- Write down rules: AB wave bottom-to-top, BC retraces up to 61.8% of AB, D forms at 127.2% extension of BC [06:30-07:00]

**Pattern Validity Confirmation** [07:00-07:30]: "If you are able to identify these characteristics on the price chart, then you are probably looking at a valid ABCD pattern." [07:30]:
1. Two parallel price swings equal in size (AB = CD)
2. BC wave = 61.8% retracement of AB
3. CD wave = 127.2% extension of BC
4. AB and CD should take approximately the same time to develop [11:00]

### Entry trigger
**Entry Point** [07:30-08:00]: "After you confirm the pattern, you should enter the market at the moment when the price action bounces from the 1.27 extension of the BC move. You initiate a trade in the direction of the bounce, which is counter to the CD leg." [07:30-08:00]

**Confluence Signals at D Point** [03:30-04:00, 10:30-11:00]:
- Candlestick pattern (engulfing bar or pin bar) [03:30-04:00, 10:30-11:00]
- Trend line breakout [03:30-04:00]

Speaker emphasizes: "The best confluence setup involving this pattern" [03:30] is when support/resistance levels increase the potential reversal probability.

**Example Entry** [11:00-12:00]: Pattern confirmed with valid Fibonacci ratios, false breakout observed (retail stop-loss clusters), then enter a long trade [11:00-11:30].

### Stop loss
[08:00-08:30]: "The proper location of your stop loss would be just beyond the price extreme formed at the end of the CD move." [08:00-08:30]. For bullish: just below the D point low. For bearish: just above the D point high (implied).

### Take profit / exit
**Minimum Target** [08:00-09:00]: "The minimum target, which you should pursue, is a price move which is equal to the CD leg in size. In this manner, the place where the CD move started emerging is your target. Basically, the 100% Fibonacci retracement of the CD wave." [08:00-08:30].

**Partial Exit Strategy** [08:30-09:00]: "After the price completes the minimum target, you could close 50% of the position size and keep 50% open to try to ride the continued momentum." [08:30-09:00]. Monitor price action clues using "support and resistance levels, trend lines, price channels, or candlestick formations" [09:00] to decide on further moves.

**Extended Hold** [12:00-12:30]: Example shows holding beyond minimum target when price creates only higher lows and doesn't test critical support areas. "The best course of action is to hold the trade further until the price breaks an important support level." [12:00-12:30].

### Invalidation / skip conditions
**Extended D Point** [10:30]: "The main drawback is that you don't know when the market will reverse, and sometimes all the condition will meet the criteria, but the point D will extend further from 1.27.2 to 1.61.8." [10:30-11:00]. To filter best signals, look for reversal candlestick patterns at D point [10:30-11:00].

**Stop-hunt Fakeouts** [09:00-10:30]: ABCD at key level or S/R breakout level "will often act as a fakeout or as a false breakout." [09:00]. This is exploitable if you understand stop-hunting: "smart money can stop hunt the liquidity sitting just beyond the support or resistance level, taking advantage of retail traders who don't know how to set up their stop loss properly." [10:00-10:30].
