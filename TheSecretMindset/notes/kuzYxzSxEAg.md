## 3-EMA Market Structure Framework (Analysis tool, not a complete strategy)

### Indicators and settings
- Exponential Moving Average (EMA): 20-period [00:30 - 01:00]
- Exponential Moving Average (EMA): 100-period [00:30 - 01:00]
- Exponential Moving Average (EMA): 200-period [00:30 - 01:00]

Note: Speaker states "The length is not important yet" [00:30] and later: "you can change the period as you wish" [11:30]. However, specific ratios of 20/100/200 are used throughout all examples.

Preference: "I prefer using exponential moving averages because they place more weight on the most recent candles" vs simple moving averages [11:00 - 11:30]. Difference between 20 EMA and 20 SMA is "very small" [11:30].

### Market Structure Identification

**Uptrend definition** [00:30 - 01:00]:
- Price is above or at the 20 EMA
- 20 EMA is above the 100 EMA
- 100 EMA is above the 200 EMA

**Downtrend definition** [01:00]:
- Price is below or at the 20 EMA
- 20 EMA is below the 100 EMA
- 100 EMA is below the 200 EMA

**Range/sideways market** [05:00 - 05:30]:
- Most moving averages have flat angle
- Price moving close to 100 EMA
- 100 EMA and 200 EMA close or touching each other
- Many crossovers between all three moving averages

### Trend Continuation Signals

**Valid uptrend** [01:30 - 02:00]:
- Price pulling away from moving averages
- 20 EMA moves away from 100 EMA
- 100 EMA has upward slope (not flat)
- No crossovers between 100 and 200 EMA
- Increasing space between moving averages

**Shallow pullback in uptrend** [02:30 - 03:00]:
- Price moves back to 100 EMA (not all the way to 200 EMA)
- Price usually bounces at 20 EMA and 100 EMA
- Trend resumes
- Space between 100 and 200 EMA NOT contracting

**Deep retracement in trend** [03:30 - 04:00]:
- Price moves back to long-term 200 EMA
- Price usually bounces at 200 EMA
- Trend resumes
- 100 EMA becomes flat or takes opposite angle
- 200 EMA still trending
- Space between 100 and 200 EMA becomes smaller (contraction)

### Reversal Warning Signals

**Overextended market signals** [04:00 - 04:30]:
1. Price is unable to break away from 20 and 100 EMAs; price "hangs around this area" with no power to move
2. Price breaks the 100 EMA in opposite direction [04:00 - 04:30]

**Continuation of reversal** [04:30 - 05:00]:
- Price continues to push opposite direction
- Break of 200 EMA in opposite direction, OR
- 100 EMA and 200 EMA cross in opposite direction [04:30 - 05:00]

Note: "You don't trade crossovers. You are just observing the evolution of the lines on the chart" [04:30 - 05:00].

### Momentum Analysis

**Price vs 20 EMA** [07:30 - 08:00]:
- When price is not hitting the 20 EMA, price has momentum and shows impulsive price action [07:30 - 08:00]

**20 EMA angle** [08:00]:
- Steep angle of 20 EMA often indicates strong momentum [08:00]

**20 EMA vs 100 EMA spacing** [08:00 - 08:30]:
- If 20 EMA is parallel with 100 EMA, indicates strong momentum [08:00]

**Price vs 100 EMA** [07:30 - 08:00]:
- If price not touching 100 EMA, price is in strong impulsive price action [07:30 - 08:00]

**20 EMA volatility indicator** [08:30 - 09:00]:
- If 20 EMA stays away from 100 EMA, market is very impulsive [08:30]
- Steep 20 EMA angle indicates strong impulse [08:30 - 09:00]

### Dynamic Support and Resistance

**In strong momentum** [09:00 - 09:30]:
- Both 20 and 100 EMA work as bounce areas when price shows strong impulse [09:00 - 09:30]
- 20 and 100 EMA offer dynamic support/resistance when they have angle and are not sideways [09:00 - 09:30]

**Medium-term EMA bounces** [09:30]:
- Medium-term (100 EMA) acts as bounce zone when moving average has steeper angle

**Long-term EMA support** [09:30]:
- 200 EMA is the last bouncing spot within current trend direction [09:30]

### Breakout Signals

**100 EMA break** [10:00]:
- When price breaks the 100 EMA in opposite direction = first warning signal [10:00]

**200 EMA break** [10:00]:
- When price breaks the 200 EMA = another indication of possible reversal [10:00]

### Range/Consolidation Behavior

**False breakouts in ranges** [10:30]:
- When price moving sideways, moving averages pull price back to themselves, creating false breakouts [10:30]
- Price tends to retrace back after breakout away from 100 EMA and move back to it [10:30]
- 200 EMA often involved in middle of range [10:30]

### Moving Average Spacing Analysis

**No space** [11:30 - 12:00]:
- Price in range, not trending
- Price has difficulties breaking away
- Price will not fully respect support/resistance levels of range
- Higher risk of false breakouts [11:30 - 12:00]
- Price could be reversing into new trend [12:00]

**Small space** [12:00 - 12:30]:
- Price might be starting new trend
- New momentum could start soon [12:00 - 12:30]
- After contraction period, expansion must follow [12:30]

**Increasing space** [12:30]:
- Price showing strong or increasing momentum
- Minor pullbacks are good continuation trade setups [12:30]

**Big space but flat/slightly decreasing** [13:00]:
- Correction will follow [13:00]
- Must wait for price to finish consolidation before continuing trend [13:00]

**Contracting space** [13:00]:
- Price could make larger, deeper retracement, OR
- Price might be ready for full reversal in opposite direction [13:00]

### Crossover Analysis

**100 EMA crossing 200 EMA relatively quickly** [12:00 - 12:30]:
- Price might be changing trend direction [12:00 - 12:30]

**General crossover observation** [14:00 - 14:30]:
- Crossover: 20 EMA and 200 EMA at same price means trend not as clear [13:30 - 14:00]
- Crossover represents when average of last 20 prices equals average of last 200 prices [13:30 - 14:00]
- "This equal relationship of the two EMAs is not a reliable indication of a trend" [14:00]

**Consequences of crossovers** [14:30]:
- Price either starting period of larger consolidation, OR
- Price about to confirm larger reversal [14:30]
