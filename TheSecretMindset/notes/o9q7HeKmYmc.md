## Notable trading principles discussed (not a tradeable strategy)

The video presents execution and psychological principles rather than a specific tradeable strategy:

1. **Candle Color Impact** [00:30-03:00]: Change candles to one color (not red/green) to avoid emotional reactions to multi-colored candles. Rationale: visual perception affects pattern recognition and emotional response. Mechanically trivial, psychologically practical.

2. **Price Ranges vs. Exact Prices** [03:00-04:30]: Use price ranges instead of exact entry prices to reduce perfectionism and overthinking. Avoids the trap of waiting for the "perfect entry" that never comes. Range-width not specified.

3. **Limit Orders vs. Market Orders** [05:00-06:30]: Use limit orders instead of market orders to "let the market come to you" [05:30]. This reduces emotional engagement and forcing trades. Reduces stress by making the entry passive.

4. **Higher Timeframes** [06:30-08:30]: Move from one-minute or five-minute charts [07:30] to higher timeframes. Rationale: higher timeframes generate fewer but more reliable signals, reduce need to monitor, and prevent mental burnout. Timeframe target not specified.

5. **Stop Loss Sizing** [08:30-09:30]: Use wider stop losses and smaller position sizes [09:30]. Narrow stops lead to being "chopped to pieces" [09:00]. Wider stop size not specified; position size should be small enough that neither a loss nor a win affects mindset.

6. **Trading with a Plan** [10:00-11:30]: Only trade if you have a strategy, a trading plan, keep a trading journal, and know your process. Use the journal to track mental mistakes, triggers, and patterns related to emotions and market conditions [11:00-11:30].
