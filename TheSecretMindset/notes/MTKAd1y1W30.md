## Strategy 1: Breakout Trading with 7-Test Scoring System

### Indicators and settings
- No indicators used; pure price action and volume
- Volume: visual confirmation of spike, magnitude NOT STATED
- Consolidation compression: 5 to 15 candles [03:00]

### Context / bias filter
**Trend Alignment (Test 1)** [02:00-02:30]: Check the higher timeframe trend first. "Breakout matches the trend, this adds confidence. That is plus one point on our scorecard. But if the breakout fights the trend, zero points. Be extremely suspicious." [02:00-02:30]. Breakouts with trend have momentum; countertrend breakouts face resistance from trend traders.

### Entry trigger
The entry is gated by a 7-point scoring system applied across three phases: setup, trigger, and confirmation.

**Phase 1: Setup (before breakout)**
- Test 1 - Trend Alignment [02:00-02:30]: Higher timeframe trend aligns with breakout direction (+1), or opposes it (0)
- Test 2 - Build-up/Compression [03:00-03:30]: "Five to 15 candles of compression against the level. That's stored energy." [03:00]. Compression before breakout (+1), no build-up (0)

**Phase 2: Trigger (during breakout candle)**
- Test 3 - Close Position [04:00-04:30]: "Candle closes with its body beyond resistance. First test passed. Candle closes inside, test failed, zero points." [04:00-04:30]. "Best breakouts close near the high on bullish moves or near the low on bearish moves. Little or no wick on the breakout side." [04:30-05:00]. Body beyond level (+1), body inside (0)
- Test 4 - Candle Character [05:00-06:30]: "Large body compared to recent candles. Small or no wicks. Clean aggressive move. This screams conviction." [05:30-06:00]. "I compared the breakout candle to the last candles. Is it one of the biggest? Good sign." [06:00]. Big/bold compared to recent candles (+1), small/weak candle (0)
- Test 5 - Volume [07:00-08:00]: "Do you see a volume spike? Plus one point. Quiet volume, zero points." [07:30-08:00]. Volume spike (+1), average or below-average volume (0)

**Phase 3: Confirmation (after breakout)**
- Test 6 - Retest [08:30-09:00]: "Real breakouts often come back to retest the level. Price bounces and continues higher. If the level flips, that is plus one point." [08:30-09:00]. Level bounces as support (+1), price falls back through (0)
- Test 7 - Follow-through [09:30-10:30]: "Watch the next two or three candles. Continuation means confirmation. That's plus one point. Stall or reversal means warning. Zero points." [09:30-10:30]. Next 2-3 candles continue in breakout direction (+1), stall or reversal (0)

**Final Entry Decision [11:00-11:30]:**
- Score 6-7: "high probability setup. Take the trade." [11:00-11:30]
- Score 4-5: "decent, but I'd wait for more confirmation before pulling the trigger." [11:00-11:30]
- Score 3 or below: "that's a trap. Walk away." [11:00-11:30]

Entry timing: Can enter on breakout candle close (with all tests passing), or "Wait for the retest. Watch for a rejection candle at the level. Enter on the retest, not the breakout. Better entry, less risk." [09:00-09:30]
