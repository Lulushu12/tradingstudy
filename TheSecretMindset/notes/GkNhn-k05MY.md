## Summary

This video teaches a psychological and structural framework for understanding trapped traders and profiting from stop hunts. Rather than teaching a specific mechanizable strategy, the speaker identifies six trap patterns where traders typically get stopped out: pin bars, engulfing pattern failures, false breakouts, trendline breaks without momentum, failed pattern reversals (double tops, triangles), and opening range failures. The core concept is: identify where traders entered predictably, anticipate where their stops are clustered, and position to profit when those stops are hit. The video emphasizes asking "Where are the stops?" rather than "Where will price go?"

## Framework: Six Stop Hunt Trap Patterns

### Pattern 1: Pin Bar / Wick Trap [01:30-02:30]

**Setup:**
"Long wick up, small body at the bottom. Classic rejection" [01:30-02:00].

**Where Traders Get Trapped [01:30-02:00]:**
"That long wick, someone bought up there. Actually, a lot of people bought up there. During the formation of this candle, price was at the top. Traders saw breakout and they entered long. Then price reversed and closed at the bottom. Now those traders are holding losing positions" [02:00].

**Stop Placement [02:00]:**
"Where are their stops? Just below this low" [02:00].

**Profiting from the Trap [02:00-02:30]:**
"I don't short immediately on the pin bar. I wait. I want to see price reject away from that wick zone. The moment it does, those trapped longs are confirmed. The move down accelerates because their stops and new shorts are both pushing the same direction" [02:00-02:30].

**Result:**
"That's not just selling. That's trapped longs liquidating. That's fuel" [02:30].

### Pattern 2: Engulfing Pattern Failure [02:30-03:30]

**Setup:**
"Bullish engulfing candle. Price was falling, then this big green candle swallows the previous red one. Textbook reversal signal" [02:30-03:00].

**Where Traders Get Trapped [03:00]:**
"So what do traders do? They buy. Watch the next candle. It doesn't just fail, it engulfs the engulfing. Now every single person who bought that pattern is underwater immediately" [02:30-03:00].

**Profiting from the Trap [03:00-03:30]:**
"When an engulfing pattern fails within one or two candles, you trade the failure direction. Not because the pattern was wrong, but because everyone who traded it just became trapped. Their stops fuel your move" [03:00-03:30].

**Key Insight [03:30]:**
"Those pretty candlestick patterns everyone memorizes, they're not signals. They're traps waiting to happen. And now you know which side of the trap to be on" [03:30].

### Pattern 3: False Breakout from Resistance [03:30-05:00]

**Setup:**
"Here's a range, clear resistance at the top. Price has touched it many times. This time, it breaks. Strong candle closes above resistance. Every breakout trader on the planet is buying this" [04:00-04:30].

**What Happens Next [04:00-04:30]:**
"Now, look at what happens over the next three candles. Price doesn't accelerate. It stalls, then it slips back below the resistance level. Now, it's inside the range again" [04:00-04:30].

**Stop Placement [04:30-05:00]:**
"Where are all those breakout traders' stops? Two places. First, just below the breakout candle, tight stops. Second, just below the range. When price reenters the range, the tight stops get hit immediately. But, the real move, that happens when price pushes toward the bottom of the range and triggers the wider stops" [04:30-05:00].

**Stop Cascade [05:00]:**
"Watch this cascade. That's not selling, that's liquidation. Breakout traders funding the move against them" [05:00].

**Profiting from the Trap [05:00-05:30]:**
"This is your trade. Wait for the false breakout to confirm. Price breaks out, fails, reverses back inside. Now, you trade opposite the breakout. You're trading with the stop cascade, not against it. Those trapped breakout longs are your fuel. They become liquidity, your liquidity" [05:00-05:30].

### Pattern 4: Trendline Break Without Momentum [05:30-06:30]

**Setup:**
"Look at this uptrend. Clean trendline, price has respected it three times. Then it breaks below. Traders see this and think reversal. The uptrend is over, time to short" [05:30-06:00].

**What Happens Next [06:00]:**
"Price dips below, triggers all the short entries, then immediately recovers and continues the uptrend. Those short sellers trapped" [06:00].

**Stop Placement [06:00]:**
"Their stops are sitting right above where they entered" [06:00].

**Key Rule [06:00-06:30]:**
"Trendline breaks without momentum usually fail. Here's what I look for now. When price breaks a trendline, but doesn't accelerate, I don't short it. I wait for the recovery" [06:00-06:30].

**Profiting from the Trap [06:30]:**
"The moment price gets back above the trendline, I go long because I'm trading with the original trend. And I'm getting fueled by all the stops of traders who shorted the break. Look at that push. That's not just buying, that's short covering. That's trapped traders funding my trade" [06:30].

### Pattern 5: Double Top Failure [07:00-08:00]

**Setup:**
"Here's a double top, the most recognized reversal pattern in trading. Price makes a high, pulls back, makes roughly the same high again. Textbook short entry" [07:00-07:30].

**Where Traders Enter [07:30]:**
"Where do traders short this? Right at the second peak, or on the break below the neckline" [07:30].

**What Happens Instead [07:30-08:00]:**
"Now watch. Price doesn't reverse. It breaks above both peaks. Every single trader who shorted the double top is now trapped. And where are their stops? Directly above the double top highs, every one of them" [07:30-08:00].

**Stop Acceleration [08:00]:**
"When price breaks above, those stops trigger. But here's the thing, stop losses on shorts are buy orders. So the market doesn't just break above the double top, it accelerates through it. This is why failed double tops become the most powerful continuation patterns. The trapped shorts don't just exit, they fuel the move against them" [08:00].

**Profiting from the Trap [08:00]:**
"My rule, when a double top gets broken to the upside, I'm not upset I missed the reversal. I'm looking for a pullback to get long. The move has fuel behind it" [08:00].

### Pattern 6: Triangle Breakout Failure [08:30-09:30]

**Setup:**
"Everyone watches them. Symmetrical, ascending, descending, doesn't matter. Traders sit on the edges waiting for the breakout direction. But here's what makes triangles so dangerous. Both sides are loaded with pending orders. Breakout longs above, breakout shorts below" [08:30-09:00].

**The Trap [09:00-09:30]:**
"Watch what happens when price breaks one direction first. It goes up, longs enter. Then it immediately reverses, comes back into the triangle, and breaks the other way. Two sets of traders are now trapped. The longs who bought the initial breakout, and the shorts who got stopped out on that fake move up" [09:00-09:30].

**Maximum Fuel [09:30]:**
"When a triangle fails like this, the move is usually explosive. You've got longs liquidating, and new shorts entering at the same time. Maximum fuel" [09:30].

**Profiting from the Trap [09:30]:**
"I don't trade triangle breakouts. I wait. If the first break fails, and price reverses through the opposite side, that's my entry. By then, I know who's trapped and where their stops are" [09:30].

### Pattern 7: Opening Range Failure [10:00-10:30]

**Setup/Trap [10:00-10:30]:**
"This is the trap that catches momentum traders every single day. New session opens. London, New York, doesn't matter. The first 15 to 30 minutes show a strong move in one direction. Traders see momentum and they enter. The direction is set for the day, they say. But watch what happens over the next hour. Price doesn't continue. It reverses. And it doesn't just reverse, it trends the opposite direction for the rest of the session. Every trader who followed the opening move is now on the wrong side" [10:00-10:30].

**Profiting from the Trap [10:30]:**
"Here's what I do now. I don't trade the first 15 to 30 minutes. I let the opening range establish. I mark the high and low of that range. Then I watch. If price breaks one side of the range and fails, I look for the opposite direction. Opening range failures trap the most aggressive traders. Their stops become fuel for the real move of the session" [10:30].
