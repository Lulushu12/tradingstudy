## Strategy 1: Central Pivot Range (CPR) Day Trading

### Indicators and settings
- Pivot Points: calculated from yesterday's high, low, and close [02:00]; creates three lines
- 50 Exponential Moving Average: period 50 [08:00]
- Trend line: short-term trend direction [08:00]

### Context / bias filter

**Morning Compass [01:30-02:30]**
Compare today's Central Pivot Range to yesterday's [02:00]:
- If higher: short-term uptrend, only look for buys [02:00-02:30]
- If lower: short-term downtrend, only look for sells [02:00-02:30]
- If range higher for three to four days in row: trend confirmed [02:30]

**Range Width Analysis [02:30-03:30]**
The width of the pivot range determines expected price behavior [02:30]:
- Wide range: zone acts as strong support/resistance, price bounces between levels, expect ranging/chopping behavior [03:00-03:30]
- Narrow range: zone offers minimal support, price cuts through easily, expect biggest moves and high momentum [03:00-03:30]

Width determines entry type: "If it's wide range, you trade the bounce. Narrow range, don't trust the zone. Look for breakouts instead." [04:00]

### Entry trigger

**Standard Setup [08:00-09:30]**
1. Short-term trend line must snap (not break gently) in your direction [08:00-08:30]
2. 50 EMA must show momentum: sloping upward with price above = bullish; downward slope with price below = bearish [08:30]
3. Price pulls back toward pivot range zone [09:00]
4. **Confluence condition [08:30-09:00]**: Best setups occur when pivot range and 50 EMA stack at exact same price level
5. Rejection candle forms at the zone: pin bar, engulfing, or other rejection pattern [09:00]
6. Enter on the rejection candle or immediately after [09:00]

Example: "I need to see before I enter a single trade. Trend line broken in my direction, momentum shifted, price pulling back to the pivot zone, the level I've already mapped, and the moving average confirming that the bigger move is still intact. Then I wait for the rejection candle." [09:00-09:30]

**Ghost Level Setup [06:00-07:00]**
When price moves so aggressively during a session that it never touches the pivot range [06:00]:
- Recognize as untested level ("ghost level") [06:00]
- Next session: if price drifts toward this previously untested zone, it acts as strong support/resistance [06:30-07:00]
- Enter on rejection from ghost level [07:00]

### Stop loss
Placed above the pivot range for short entries [05:00]; above the zone for long entries [10:30]

### Take profit / exit
Target the next swing high (for longs) or swing low (for shorts) [00:30] [05:00] [10:30]

### Invalidation / skip conditions
Do not trade when:
- Range is paper thin (narrow) AND 50 EMA is not sitting inside the pivot range zone [11:30]
- No confluence present; only one level acting alone [11:30]
- No rejection candle forms [09:00-09:30]

Speaker explicitly states: "Knowing when the setup isn't there and closing the laptop instead of forcing a trade" [12:00]
