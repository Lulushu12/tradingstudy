## Summary

The video teaches a price action framework combined with On-Balance-Volume (OBV) to assess market phases and trend strength. Nine distinct patterns are identified across uptrends, downtrends, and ranges, categorized by whether price and volume are aligned (strong), divergent (weak/reversal), or sideways (moderate/consolidation). OBV that precedes price action is used to anticipate trend reversals and identify accumulation/distribution phases in consolidating markets.

## Market Phase Definitions (Price Action Foundation)

Before analyzing volume patterns, the speaker defines three market phases:

- Uptrend: "price is making higher highs and higher lows" [01:30]
- Downtrend: "price is making lower highs and lower lows" [02:00]
- Sideways/Range: "price is not making higher highs and higher lows or lower highs lower lows" [02:00]

Reversal signals: "A reversal in the primary trend is signaled when the market is unable to create another consecutive swing in the direction of the primary trend" [02:30]

## Indicator Settings

- On-Balance-Volume (OBV): standard calculation; "increases or decreases during each day in correlation on whether the price closes higher or lower compared to the close during the previous day" [04:30]
- Key assumption: "on balance volume movements precede price changes" [04:30]
