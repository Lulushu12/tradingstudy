## Summary

This video explains range bar charts as an alternative to time-based candlestick charts. Range bars form when price moves a specified amount (e.g., 10 pips) rather than after a fixed time period. The video argues range bars reduce noise and make significant price action more visible for day trading and scalping. It covers how to select appropriate range sizes based on market volatility (using ATR or daily range as reference) and demonstrates supply/demand identification on range bar charts.
