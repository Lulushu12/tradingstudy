## Summary

This video teaches Fibonacci Pivot Points as support and resistance levels. The primary strategy is trend-following: enter long at S1 or central pivot when the market is in an uptrend, targeting R1 or R2; enter short at R1 or central pivot in downtrends, targeting S1 or S2. An alternative strategy combines Fibonacci Pivot Points with Fibonacci Retracements for confluence entries. The speaker emphasizes that pivot points are objective, leading indicators and work best on 1-hour and higher timeframes.

## Strategy 1: Fibonacci Pivot Point Trend Following

### Indicators and settings

- Fibonacci Pivot Points (calculated from previous session's high, low, close using Fibonacci ratios 38.2, 61.8, 100)
- Pivot point levels: Central Pivot Point, S1 (Support 1), S2 (Support 2), S3 (Support 3), R1 (Resistance 1), R2 (Resistance 2), R3 (Resistance 3)
- Optional: Moving Average 100-200 period for trend confirmation [06:30]

### Context / bias filter

Identify the main trend first [05:00]. For uptrend: price making "higher highs and higher lows" [05:00]. For downtrend: price making "lower lows and lower highs" [05:30]. Additionally, compare the current day's central pivot point with the previous day's: if current pivot is higher than previous, consider only long entries; if lower, consider only short entries [06:30-07:00]. Price should "hold above" pivot point for uptrend or "hold below" for downtrend [02:30-03:00].

### Entry trigger

For long (uptrend): Buy at S1 or at the central Pivot Point [05:00-05:30]. For short (downtrend): Sell at R1 or at the central Pivot Point [05:00-05:30]. Entries can be made using price action or indicators; the speaker mentions candlestick patterns, trend line breakouts, or indicator-based entry signals are optional [07:30].

### Take profit / exit

For long entries: Target R1 or R2 [05:00-05:30]. For short entries: Target S1 or S2 [05:00-05:30]. Do not focus on S2, S3, R2, or R3 levels during strong trending moves, as they are "not often tested during a strong bullish or bearish advance" [05:30-06:00].

### Invalidation / skip conditions

Do not trade S2, S3, R2, R3 in strong trending markets [05:30-06:00]. Do not trade if the trend has changed (e.g., if the market was uptrending but currently makes lower highs/lows, switch to short bias). Do not trade if price is not at a significant pivot level; wait for price interaction at S1, central pivot, or R1.

## Strategy 2: Fibonacci Pivot Points with Fibonacci Retracement Confluence

### Indicators and settings

- Fibonacci Pivot Points: central pivot point, S1, R1 levels (minimum levels used)
- Fibonacci Retracement levels: 38.2% and 50% retracement levels recommended [08:00]

### Context / bias filter

Identify the main trend and the relevant swing high and low for drawing Fibonacci retracement. Look for confluence areas where Fibonacci retracement levels (38.2%, 50%) align with Fibonacci Pivot Point levels (central pivot, S1, or R1) [08:00-08:30].

### Entry trigger

When price retraces to the identified confluence zone (where retracement level meets a pivot level), either "enter at the market or wait for a candle signal or any other trigger at the confluence zone" [09:00-09:30]. The speaker acknowledges that entering before confirmation is riskier but offers higher risk/reward [09:00].
