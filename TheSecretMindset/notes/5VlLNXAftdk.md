## Summary
A day trading strategy combining previous day's high/low levels with VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price) bands to identify high-probability reversal zones. The strategy looks for confluence where previous day levels overlap with VWAP bands, then enters trades when price tests these confluences. Bullish entries at previous day lows (supported by lower VWAP), bearish entries at previous day highs (resisted by upper VWAP). Stop loss is placed near the confluence zone, but take profit targets are not specified.

## Instruments and timeframes stated
- markets: Forex (EUR/USD implied) and stocks (Apple, Tesla examples)
- timeframes: Daily chart for marking previous day levels [00:00-01:00]; intraday trading implied (same-day entry/exit)
- sessions/hours: Previous day's session high/low; testing within current trading day
