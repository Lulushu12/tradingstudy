## Summary
A comprehensive Bollinger Bands system using three bands (period 200, std dev 1, 2, 3) to classify trend strength, identify confluence entries, and spot reversal divergences. Includes three distinct strategies: (1) confluence continuation trades in strong trends, (2) confluence trades in weak trend areas, and (3) divergence reversals using MACD. Optional tools include Heikin Ashi candles for filtering market noise and MACD histogram for divergence detection.

## Strategy 1: Confluence Continuation in Strong Trends

### Indicators and settings
- Bollinger Bands 1: period 200, standard deviation 1
- Bollinger Bands 2: period 200, standard deviation 2
- Bollinger Bands 3: period 200, standard deviation 3
- Middle Bollinger Band: increase thickness and change color for visibility [00:30 - 01:00]
- Optional: Heikin Ashi candles [09:30 - 11:30]

### Context / bias filter

**Trend identification based on price location:**

Weak uptrend [02:00 - 02:30]: Price between middle line and first band. "There is no strong momentum, and the price is likely to fluctuate within a range, and may even go below the middle line" [02:30]. Entry possible "only if you have confluence with other factors" [02:30].

Strong uptrend [02:30 - 03:00]: Price between first and second band. "There is a higher chance that the price will continue upward. As long as the candles continue to close in this buy zone, the odds favour maintaining current long positions or even opening new ones" [02:30 - 03:00].

Very strong uptrend [03:00 - 03:30]: Price between second and third band. "Price is overextended and I would avoid buying if price goes outside the bands" [03:00 - 03:30].

Weak downtrend [03:30 - 04:00]: Price below middle line between middle and first band. "There is no strong momentum, yet" [04:00].

Strong downtrend [04:00]: Price between first and second band below middle line. "There is a higher chance that the price will continue downward" [04:00].

Very strong downtrend [04:00 - 04:30]: Price between second and third band. [04:00 - 04:30].

### Entry trigger

**Confluence requirement** [04:30 - 06:00]: "Confluence refers to the overlap of two or more key levels or areas of value. These can be trend lines, horizontal levels, and even other channels. Looking for overlaps of key levels with Bollinger Band is an excellent way to find high probability setups" [04:30 - 05:00].

Uptrend confluence: "Find a confluence between a recent support or resistance level and one of the standard deviation bands, you can trade a bounce of that area" [05:00 - 05:30].

Downtrend confluence: "A recent support or resistance aligns with one of the lower volatility bands, a decent pullback trade may be on the cards" [05:30 - 06:00].

Entry point: Price touches the band [06:30 - 07:00]. Alternative: "You can wait for a rejection signal, maybe an engulfing candle or a pin bar. It's up to you" [08:00].

Example: "Price broke above the middle band and also broke this resistance level. Which now will potentially act as a support. Here's our value area. The first band above the middle line and this level are aligning on the same point. So a buy trade, in the strong trend area, is a decent trend continuation opportunity" [06:00 - 06:30].

## Strategy 3: Divergence Reversal Trade

### Indicators and settings
- Bollinger Bands: same 3-band setup (period 200, std dev 1, 2, 3)
- MACD histogram: "I've added the MACD histogram, to easily spot points of inconsistency between price and indicator" [12:00]
- Trendline: "always wait for a trendline breakout before entering into a divergence trade" [13:30]

### Context / bias filter

"After price spends a lot of time in the very strong trend territory, between the second and the third band of the setup" [11:30 - 12:00]. This is when to look for reversals.

### Entry trigger

**Divergence identification** [12:00 - 12:30]:
- Bearish divergence: Market makes higher high, but MACD shows lower high (weakness) [12:00 - 12:30]
- Bullish divergence: Market makes lower low, but MACD shows higher low (strength) [12:30]

**Key timing requirement** [13:00 - 13:30]: "When you find a divergence, you don't enter at the third band. You enter at the second one" [13:00].

**Trendline filter** [13:30]: "Always wait for a trendline breakout before entering into a divergence trade. This will filter a lot of fake divergences. You want to enter after a trendline breakout, somewhere around the second Bollinger band" [13:30].

Entry: "Enter after a trendline breakout, somewhere around the second Bollinger band" [13:30].

### Stop loss
"Stop loss above the third band" [13:30 - 14:00].

### Take profit / exit

"You target the first band, and the middle band as the final target. But most of the times, you'll have a decent win rate if you just target the second band" [13:00 - 13:00].

**Targets:**
- First target: Second band (riskier trade often stops here) [13:00]
- Second target: First band [13:00]
- Final target: Middle band [13:00]

"This is a riskier trade, so if it's not your style, stick to trend continuation setups" [14:00].

### Invalidation / skip conditions
"This is a riskier trade, against the trend" [12:30 - 13:00]. Skip if no trendline breakout (this filters fake divergences) [13:30].

### Claimed performance
"You'll have a decent win rate if you just target the second band" [13:00]. "Price respect the bands, almost religiously. Even when price was below the middle band, it found support at the lower bands. This alone makes this system a very powerful one" [06:00 - 06:30].
