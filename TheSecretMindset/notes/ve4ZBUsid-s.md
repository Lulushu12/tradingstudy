## Summary
A scalping strategy that capitalizes on price reversals following news announcements by identifying and exploiting stop-hunts. The strategy involves: marking liquidity zones on the 5-minute chart before a major news event, waiting for the initial price spike when news is released, identifying rejection signals on the 1-minute chart when the spike reverses, and entering a counter-trend trade in the opposite direction of the initial spike. Stop loss is placed just above the rejection candle high. Designed for high-impact economic news events.

### Entry trigger
**Step 1: Identify Liquidity Zones (Before News)** [03:00-04:00]: "Before the news announcement, you analyze the lower charts to find the most obvious and nearby liquidity zones. You want to mark those price levels where there is a significant concentration of pending orders. These zones are often found at major supply or demand areas, round numbers, or at previous swing highs and lows." [03:00-03:30]. Use 5-minute chart for this analysis [00:30].

**Step 2: Wait for News Release and Initial Move** [03:30-04:00]: "When the news is released, you wait for the sudden surge in volatility as market participants react to the information. This volatility will most likely trigger a stop hunt where the price rapidly moves in one direction triggering stop loss orders placed by other traders." [03:30-04:00].

**Step 3: Wait for Rejection at Liquidity Zone** [04:00-04:30]: "Once your zone is hit and the stop hunt occurs and you see signs of rejection on the 1-minute chart, you aim to enter a trade in the opposite direction of the initial price spike." [04:00]. "This rejection candle on the 1-minute chart is our signal that the stop hunt might be over and that the real market sentiment is likely bearish." [10:30-11:00].

**Entry Direction** [04:00-04:30, 08:00-10:00]: 
- If initial spike is upward (triggering buy stops), enter SHORT [08:00-08:30]
- If initial spike is downward (triggering sell stops), enter LONG [09:00-09:30]
- "By going long after the strong rejection of the downward move, we are aligning ourselves with the underlying bullish sentiment." [09:30]
- "Once the buy stops were triggered and the liquidity was absorbed, we saw signs of rejection. This is where we look to enter our short position." [10:30]

**Timing Requirement** [11:30-12:00]: "This scalping method requires quick reflexes and decisive action. You need to be ready to pull the trigger as soon as you see the rejection at your key level. Hesitation can be costly and you will miss your entry if you're not prepared." [11:30-12:00].

**Examples:**
1. **EUR/USD, unemployment/GDP data** [08:00-08:30]: Price spikes up, reverses down to liquidity zone → enter short [08:00-08:30]
2. **Gold, Federal Reserve statement** [09:00-09:30]: Price spikes down, reverses up past pre-announcement levels → enter long [09:00-09:30]
3. **Dow Jones, core price index** [10:00-11:00]: Price spikes up, shows rejection → enter short [10:00-11:00]

### Stop loss
[10:30-11:00]: "A logical stop-loss placement could be just above the high of the rejection candle. This allows the trade some room to breathe while protecting us in case the market suddenly reverses higher." [10:30-11:00].
