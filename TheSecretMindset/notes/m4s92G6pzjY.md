## Divergence Concept and Types

### Definition and Basic Concept
- "A divergence occurs when price and the indicators provide conflicting signals by moving in opposite directions" [00:00]
- Purpose: "If you spot such a situation, it could be worthwhile taking a closer look at what is really going on" [00:00]

### Regular Divergences (Reversal Signals)
**Bullish Regular Divergence:**
- Price creates a lower low
- Indicator (typically MACD) makes a higher low
- Psychology: "MACD is not following the price down, suggesting the move lower is weakening and losing momentum, resulting in a possible move higher" [01:30-02:00]
- Used to trade: Direction change from downward to upward [01:30]
- Best indicators: MACD histogram, Money Flow Index [01:30]

**Bearish Regular Divergence:**
- Price creates a higher high
- Indicator makes a lower high
- Psychology: "MACD is not following the price up, suggesting the move higher is weakening and losing momentum, resulting in a possible move lower" [02:00]
- Used to trade: Direction change from upward to downward [02:00]
- Best indicators: MACD histogram, Money Flow Index [01:30]

### Hidden Divergences (Continuation Signals)
**Bullish Hidden Divergence:**
- Stochastic makes a lower low
- Price makes a higher low
- Psychology: "While the price is higher than it was before, the indicator is lower, suggesting the market is much more oversold" [01:00]
- Used to trade: Continuation of uptrend [01:00]
- Best indicators: Stochastic, RSI [00:30]
- Also called: "Continuation divergences" [01:00]

**Bearish Hidden Divergence:**
- Stochastic makes a higher high
- Price makes a lower high
- Used to trade: Continuation of downtrend [01:00]
- Best indicators: Stochastic, RSI [00:30]

## 10 Divergence Trading Rules

### Rule 1: Use the Right Indicators to Spot Divergences
- Regular divergences: "MACD histogram and Money flow index are very effective" [01:30]
- Hidden divergences: "Stochastic indicator and RSI are perfect to find hidden divergences" [00:30]
- Reason: Oscillators that determine overbought/oversold are "more effective for spotting hidden divergences" [00:30]
- Focus: Most technical indicators used are "momentum oscillators" [00:30]

### Rule 2: Adjust the Settings of Indicators to Identify Divergences
- Different markets/timeframes need different settings [02:30]
- Example: "The first 2 RSI settings were able to spot the hidden divergence, while the smoother RSI, with the longer period, was unable to find it" [02:30]
- Key insight: "Higher lookback periods on indicators will not suit hidden divergences. The same for regular divergences" [02:30]
- Lag concern: "Larger settings will produce a higher lag" [02:30-03:00]
- Flexibility: "You may need to slightly shorten or increase the lookback period to find the optimal divergences" as "market volatility and momentum cycles will change" [03:00]

### Rule 3: Only Take Divergences in the Direction of the Main Trend
- Regular divergences: "The oscillators indicate momentum shift and thus, point toward a reversal of the trend" [03:00-03:30]
- Hidden divergences: "Signal momentum coming into the main trend, suggesting a possible continuation in the main direction" [03:30]
- Problem: "Many traders fail when taking divergence signals because they take counter-trend signals" [03:30]
- Better approach: "Identify the main trend and find divergences that go along with the trend" [03:30]
- Uptrend action: "Ignore bearish regular divergences and search for bullish regular divergences. Also concentrate on bullish hidden divergences" [04:00]
- Downtrend action: "Ignore bullish regular divergences and search for bearish regular divergences and bearish hidden divergences" [04:00]
- Benefit: "You have a higher probability of a successful divergence trade" [04:00]

### Rule 4: Always Wait for Price Action Confirmation (Trend Line Breakout)
- Problem: "Might enter too early because you did not wait for more confirmation" [04:30]
- Critical: "A divergence signal always needs a confirmation" [04:30]
- Best confirmation: "Trend line breakout" [04:30-05:00]
- Example: "Bullish MACD divergence with a confirmed trend line breakout" shows "higher probability the price is going to reverse" [05:00]
- Alternative confirmation: "Support or resistance breakout" [05:00]
- Psychology: "When you see price action breaking a major level, it could signal a shift in power from buyers to sellers (or vice versa) and that a price reversal might occurring" [05:00]

### Rule 5: Take Divergence Signals from Support/Resistance Levels
- Importance: "The location of the divergence is very important" [05:30]
- Purpose: "Adding the filter of location can usually enhance the quality of your divergence signals and trades" [05:30]
- Approach: "Wait for the price to move into a previous support/resistance zone and only then look for divergences and trend shifts, to time your entries" [05:30-06:00]
- Example: "First divergence completely failed and the second one resulted in a massive winner. What was the difference? The first divergence happened in the middle of nowhere and the second divergence formed at a very important resistance level" [06:00]
- Trader mindset: "First identify your support/resistance zones and then let price come to you. Such an approach will impact your performance in a big way" [06:30]

### Rule 6: Confirm Divergence Signals Using Volume
- Neglected tool: "Volume is one piece of information that is often neglected by many market players, especially the beginners" [06:30]
- Read volume correctly: "Must be seen in the light of what the price is doing. The implication of a trading volume depends on the price movement" [06:30-07:00]
- Rising uptrend with divergence + reducing volume: "May show that there's little interest in the asset, and the price may reverse" [07:00]
- Declining with divergence + low volume: "Could mean that institutional traders are not interested in the price direction. So the price might soon reverse to the upside" [07:00]
- High volume after divergence: "Usually indicates more interest in the asset and the presence of institutional traders" [07:30]
- Best signal: "A divergence confirmed by a rising volume represents one of the safest trades" [07:30]

### Rule 7: Find Confluence with Common Moving Averages
- Principle: "Don't take the trade without confirming the signal with some other type of analysis" [07:30-08:00]
- Method: Use confluence - "more than one indication of where price will go" [08:00]
- Example 1: "Confluence occurs where price reaches a commonly used moving average. If in that point, you see a divergence forming, you confirm your direction with the moving average, and you will have a stronger signal" [08:00]
- Example 2: "Hidden bearish divergence on the RSI and the RSI reached overbought area" when price touched moving average [08:00-08:30]
- Benefit: "Higher probability of price going your way" [08:00]

### Rule 8: Use Higher Time Frames to Trade Divergences
- Accuracy: "Divergence signals tend to be more accurate on the longer time frames because you get less false signals" [08:30]
- Trade-off: "Fewer trades but if you structure your trade well, then your profit potential can be huge" [08:30]
- Short timeframe problem: "Divergences on shorter time frames will occur more frequently but are less reliable" [08:30]
- Recommendation: "Look for divergences on 1-hour charts or longer" [08:30]
- Reason: "On those [shorter] timeframes, there's just too much noise and the probability of a divergence to fail is higher" [09:00]

### Rule 9: Use Ultimate Oscillator to Trade Multiple Timeframe Divergences
- Advantage: "Combines short-term, intermediate-term, and long-term price action into one oscillator" [09:00-09:30]
- Design: "Measures momentum for three distinct timeframes. The second timeframe is double compared to the first, and the third timeframe is double compared to the second" [09:30]
- Weighting: "Even though the shortest timeframe carries the most weight, the longest timeframe is not ignored, which reduces the number of false divergences" [09:30]
- Noise reduction: "Generates fewer divergence signals than the other oscillators due to its multi-timeframe construction, so the market noise is greatly reduced" [09:30]
- Trade-off: "While the multiple timeframe trading method for the indicator may help eliminate some poor trades, it also eliminates many good ones. Therefore, it is sometimes necessary to change its settings to reduce sensitivity and increase the number of signals" [09:30-10:00]

### Rule 10: Don't Force Divergence Trades
- Critical: "When trading divergences, you must be careful and precise with your entry point" [10:00]
- When to skip: "If you spot a divergence but the price has already reversed and moved in one direction for some time, the divergence should be considered played out" [10:00-10:30]
- Mindset: "You missed the boat this time and it's better to stay aside and don't force a late trade" [10:00-10:30]
- Action: "Wait for another swing to form and start over your divergence search" [10:30]
- Reason: "If you enter late, you alter your risk:reward ratio for the trade" [10:30]
- Example consequence: "If you enter after the price already moved a lot, you will have to increase your stop loss, and if the trade doesn't have much profit potential, you will have a hard time managing your trade" [10:30-11:00]
- Wisdom: "There are always going to be other divergence opportunities. The key is to be patient" [11:00]
