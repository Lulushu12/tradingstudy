## Strategy 1: Confluence Scalping with Stochastic Divergence

### Indicators and settings

- Static Support/Resistance: identified from recent market swings; at least 3 swing points touch [01:30-02:00]
- 200-period EMA: dynamic support/resistance level [02:00-03:00]
- Stochastic Oscillator: period = NOT STATED (default assumed), used for divergence detection [03:00-03:30]

### Context / bias filter

Identify a confluence area where:
1. Static support or resistance exists at a recent swing level (at least 3 swing points touching the level) [01:30-02:00]
2. The 200 EMA is at or near the same level, providing dynamic support/resistance [02:00-03:00]

The confluence must be "obvious" and clear [01:30]. The speaker requires at least 2 support/resistance levels (1 static, 1 dynamic) for a valid confluence area [01:30].

### Entry trigger

Enter when:
1. Confluence area is identified (static + dynamic levels aligned) [01:30-03:00]
2. Stochastic shows a divergence at the confluence level:
   - Bullish divergence: price makes lower low, Stochastic makes higher low [03:30]
   - Bearish divergence: price makes higher high, Stochastic makes lower high [03:30]
3. Stochastic lines cross to confirm the divergence reversal [05:00]

Examples from charts:
- Long entry: confluence area with 200 EMA + static resistance level, regular and hidden divergence on Stochastic, Stochastic lines cross above [04:30-05:00]
- Short entry: resistance level + 200 EMA confluence, hidden divergence on Stochastic, Stochastic lines cross below [06:00-06:30]

The speaker notes that adding price action techniques (e.g., flag pattern) "will become even more powerful" [05:30].

### Stop loss

Place "below the confluence area" for long entries; place above for short entries [05:00]. Exact price distance NOT STATED. The speaker advises using "tighter stop-losses" due to the scalping nature [07:00].

### Take profit / exit

Depends on trader's risk aversion and trading style [05:00]. Options mentioned:
- Aim for 2:1 risk/reward ratio [05:00]
- Move stop loss to breakeven once in profit, ride a risk-free trade [05:00]

Specific profit target price or method NOT STATED.

### Invalidation / skip conditions

Do not enter if confluence area is not clearly defined. Do not trade instruments with spreads greater than 2 points; "risk/reward ratio is not worth it" [07:30]. Do not enter without Stochastic divergence confirmation; do not trade on confluence alone [04:30].
