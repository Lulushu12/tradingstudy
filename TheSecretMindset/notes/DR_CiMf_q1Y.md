## Strategy 1: Slingshot (Squeeze Breakout Pullback)

### Indicators and settings

- Bollinger Bands: 20-period SMA and standard deviation bands

### Context / bias filter

Identify a squeeze: price chopping sideways with tiny candles and bands getting progressively tighter [00:30]. This indicates "potential energy building" [00:30-01:00]. The tighter the squeeze, "the bigger the snap" [01:30].

### Entry trigger

Do NOT enter on the initial breakout candle, even though volume spikes and bands blast open [00:30-01:00]. Instead, wait for price to pull back and "kiss the exact level it broke from" [01:00-01:30]. Enter on this pullback (the "slingshot"), not on the breakout [01:00-01:30]. This allows amateurs to take heat on the breakout, and then the real move begins [01:00-01:30].

## Strategy 2: Rubber Band (Reversal at Band Extreme)

### Indicators and settings

- Bollinger Bands: 20-period SMA
- Historical support/resistance levels (price action)
- Trigger: candle close back inside band after being outside

### Context / bias filter

Identify a drop with many red candles and price outside the lower band [02:00]. This indicates potential exhaustion [02:00-02:30]. CRITICAL: Look left at the chart [02:30]. Check if there is historical support in that area; did price respect this level before? [02:30]. Without historical support confirmation, do not trade [02:30-03:00].

### Entry trigger

Enter a reversal long trade when:
1. Price drops below lower Bollinger Band [02:00-02:30]
2. The drop reaches a historical support zone [02:30]

### Indicators and settings

In an uptrend, the lower band is NOT a reversal zone; it's a "trampoline" for entry on dips [04:00]. In a downtrend (opposite direction): upper band is a "ceiling" for short entries on bounces [04:30].

Middle line FILTER: If the 20 SMA is angled up AND price is above it = strong uptrend confirmed [04:00-04:30]. If the middle line is flat and price is chopping through it = no trend, no trampoline [04:00-04:30]. This filter improved win rate from 30% to over 50% [04:00].

## Filter 1: RSI Divergence (Lie Detector)

### Concept

RSI divergence acts as a confirmation filter for band extreme setups (Rubber Band strategy) [05:00-06:00]. When price and RSI make opposite extremes, this signals the crowd is wrong [06:00-06:30].

### Rules

- Bullish divergence for reversals: price makes lower low, but RSI makes higher low [05:00-05:30]
- Bearish divergence: price makes higher high, but RSI makes lower high [06:30]
- Combined with band extreme: "Band extreme plus divergence equals high conviction" [06:30]

### Example

Gold breakdown: price crashes through support and lower Bollinger Band (clear short signal), but RSI makes higher low while price makes lower low (divergence). Speaker took long trade at lower band, gained 200 pips in 2 days [06:00].

## Filter 2: Three-Light System (Final Confirmation)

### Rule

Before entering ANY trade, check three conditions. All three must be green lights; one red light and the trade is skipped [08:00-08:30].

### Light 1: Location

"Am I at the real level or a random one?" [07:00]. If price respected this zone before (swing high/low, previous touches), it's a green light [07:00-08:00]. If it's "just empty space on the chart," it's a red light and the trade is skipped [07:00-08:00].

### Light 2: Trend

"Am I fighting the current?" [07:30]. If momentum is behind the setup (trading with trend), it's a green light [07:30]. If the signal opposes the main trend (e.g., buying in downtrend), it's a red light [07:30].

### Light 3: Candle Close

"Is this candle finished or am I jumping in while it's still forming?" [08:00]. If the candle has closed and confirmed the setup, green light [08:00-08:30]. If candle is still forming/moving, red light; wait for close [08:00-08:30].

### Example

Setup at major resistance, trend down, bands angled down = green lights. But candle still forming = red light. Speaker waited; candle closed bullish and invalidated the setup [08:00-08:30].

### Rule

"Three green lights or I walk away. I don't care if it's the perfect slingshot. I don't care if RSI is screaming divergence. One red light and I'm out" [08:00-08:30].
