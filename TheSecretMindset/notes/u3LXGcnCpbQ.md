## Summary
This video presents a 3-wave Fibonacci extension swing trading strategy designed for part-time traders with limited time (30-60 minutes daily). The strategy identifies 3-wave Elliott Wave patterns (points A, B, C) and trades the third impulsive wave using Fibonacci retracements and extensions. Entries occur at the 50% Fibonacci level with targets at the 1.618 extension level, offering 3:1 to 4:1 risk-to-reward ratios.

## Strategy 1: 3-Wave Fibonacci Extension Trading

### Context / bias filter

**Elliott Wave concept simplified [01:00-01:30]**
Full Elliott Wave is 5-wave pattern; this strategy focuses only on first 3 waves (1, 3, and 5 in full Elliott terms, but treated as AB-wave pair and BC-wave pair).
Reasons: "Elliott waves are subjective. Can't recognize until already passed" [01:30]

**Trend context [02:00-02:30]**
Strategy trades continuation patterns in trending markets:
- Uptrend: pattern of higher highs and higher lows [02:00]
- Each successive impulse swing must take out prior high [02:00]
- When price surpasses swing high (#2), confirms 3-wave pattern present [02:00]

### Pattern identification: The 3-Wave Structure

**Uptrend 3-wave pattern [02:30-03:00]**
- Point A: lowest low, forms support [02:30]
- Point B: highest point, forms potential resistance [02:30]
- Point C: second low, forms support (MUST be higher than Point A) [02:30]
- Confirmation: breakout above Point B signals uptrend continuation [02:30]

**Downtrend 3-wave pattern [02:30-03:00]**
- Point A: highest point, finds resistance, then moves down [03:00]
- Point B: lowest low, forms potential support [03:00]
- Point C: second resistance level [03:00]
- Confirmation: break below Point B signals downtrend continuation [03:00]

### Indicators and settings
- Fibonacci Tool: retracement and extension ratios used
- Trendline: rising in uptrends, falling in downtrends
- Minimum swing size: user-defined (first AB wave) [03:30]

### Entry trigger

**Four required conditions for valid setup [04:30-06:00]**

**Condition 1: Trendline Breakout [04:30-05:00]**
- AB wave must break the trendline [05:00]
- Rising trendline (support in uptrend) broken = bullish signal [04:30-05:00]
- Falling trendline (resistance in downtrend) broken = bearish signal [05:00]

**Condition 2: Point C retracement [05:00-05:30]**
- Point C must be higher than Point A for uptrend [05:00]
- Point C must be lower than Point A for downtrend [05:00]
- Second wave (BC) must NOT fall below start of wave one (Point A) [05:30]
- **Critical rule [05:30]**: BC wave must retrace to 38.2 Fib level AT MOST
- Speaker ignores setup if retraces only to 23.6 Fib level [05:30]

**Condition 3: Retracement to 50% Fib Level [05:30-06:00]**
- Price must retrace to 50% Fib level (not less, not to 61.8)
- If price fails to retrace to 50%, ignore setup [06:00]
- "We want price to retrace to 50% level. If doesn't, find other opportunities" [06:00]

**Entry execution [07:00-07:30]**
- Enter at 50% Fibonacci level [07:00]
- Can also switch to lower timeframe to time exact entry [07:00]
- Preference: "enter trade at 50% FIB level" [07:00]
- Example notes: "at moment of breakout of point B, would already be in profit" [07:30]

### Stop loss
**Below 23.6 Fib level [07:00-07:30]**
- For uptrend: "stop loss below the 23.6 level" [07:00]
- For downtrend: "stop loss above the 23.6 level" [08:30]
- Can be moved to break-even after price breaks point B [07:30-08:00]

### Take profit / exit

**Target level: 1.618 Fib extension [04:00, 06:00]**
- Apply Fibonacci extension (reverse application from Point B to Point A)
- Target the 1.618 extension ratio [04:00-04:30, 06:00]
- Examples show price reaching 1.618 target and bouncing hard [08:30-09:00]

**Stop-to-breakeven rule [07:30-08:00]**
After price breaks Point B: "When price breaks point B, move your stop to break even. I personally do it every time" [07:30]
Rationale: "Sometime prices retraces to entry point, leaving breakeven trade. Sometimes goes right to target" [07:30-08:00]
