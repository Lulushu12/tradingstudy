## Summary

This video simplifies Wyckoff's Method into three boxes: (1) Build—price consolidates in a range, (2) Fake—price sweeps past the range edge to trigger stop orders (a spring below or an upthrust above), then reclaims back inside the range, (3) Leave—price breaks out in the opposite direction of the fake and runs. The key insight is trading the reclaim candle (the close back inside the range) rather than the breakout itself, which signals a stop hunt. Entry occurs on the reclaim or the first higher/lower low after reclaim confirmation. Stop is placed below the sweep low, and target is at the opposite range edge. The critical filter is the "failure rule": if price does not reclaim (close back inside the range), skip the trade as it indicates a genuine breakout, not a trap.

## Strategy 1: Wyckoff Three-Box Method (Stop Hunt Fade + Reclaim Entry)

### Indicators and settings

**No technical indicators required.** Pure price action:
- Draw a rectangular box connecting range highs and lows [01:00-01:30]
- Identify sweep (wick below/above box edge), reclaim (close back inside), and leave (breakout)

### Context / bias filter

#### Box 1: The Build (Range Formation) [03:00-04:00]

"Price stops trending and starts ranging. Big players are quietly buying or selling inside that range" [03:00-03:30].

**Accumulation:** "If big buyers are loading up, we call that accumulation, which is just a fancy word for the big money is buying inside this box before pushing it up" [03:30].

**Distribution:** "If big sellers are dumping, that's distribution. Meaning, big money is selling inside this box before pushing it down" [03:30-04:00].

**Entry Logic:**
The range acts as a battle zone where institutional players are building positions. The box is identified by drawing horizontally from the highest high and lowest low of the consolidation period.

#### Composite Man Concept [05:30-07:00]

"The composite man is the market using the crowd's obvious behavior against it. When the crowd puts stops under the range, price often travels there first. When the crowd buys the breakout above the range, price can trap them first" [05:30-06:00].

"The market isn't picking on you personally. You're too little to matter. The market is hunting fuel, and your stop loss happens to be the fuel sitting closest to the surface" [06:30-07:00].

**Reframe:**
Instead of asking "Why did they hunt my stop?", ask "Where is the obvious liquidity sitting before the real move?" [06:00-06:30].

### Entry trigger

#### Box 2: The Fake (Sweep + Reclaim) [02:00-04:30]

**Sweep (Stop Hunt) [02:00-02:30]:**
"Price breaks below the range low, grabs sell side liquidity where sell orders and long stop losses are stacked. Then closes back above the same level" [02:00-02:30]. This is called a spring.

Alternatively: "Price breaks above the range high, breakout buyers jump in. And then price closes back inside the box. That is the mirror trap, called an up thrust" [02:30].

**Reclaim (The Real Entry Signal) [02:00-02:30]:**
"The sweep is not your signal. Everyone watches the breakout, but few watch what happens next. And what happens next is the only thing that matters, the reclaim. Price has to close back inside the box" [02:00].

"That reclaim candle, the one that closes back inside the range after the sweep, is where the real information lives. It's the chart admitting the move was a lie" [00:30].

**Terminology [02:00-02:30]:**
- **Spring:** Price breaks below range low and closes back above it [02:00-02:30]
- **Upthrust:** Price breaks above range high and closes back inside [02:30]

**Entry After Reclaim [09:30-10:00, 10:00-10:30]:**
"I placed the entry above the bullish candle that moves away from the level" [10:00-10:30] (for a long after a spring).

Wait for:
- First higher low after reclaim (for long entries) [08:30-09:00]
- First lower high or strong bearish candle after reclaim (for short entries) [09:00]

**Example: Pound Dollar 15-Minute [09:00-10:00]:**
"Price comes down into the low, and I am not buying yet because the candle has not proved anything...The candle closes back above the low and that is the reclaim. The next candle pulls back and I want to see if buyers defend the same level from inside the range. Price holds, prints a small higher low and now the long idea is valid" [09:30-10:00].

### Stop loss

**Placement [10:00-10:30]:**
"The stop goes below the sweep low" [10:00-10:30].

For a long trade after a spring: Stop is placed below the low of the candle that broke below and reclaimed above the range low.

For a short trade after an upthrust: Stop is placed above the high of the candle that broke above and reclaimed inside.

### Take profit / exit

**Target [10:00-10:30]:**
"The target goes at the range high because that is where buy side liquidity, where buy orders are stacked, is waiting" [10:00-10:30] (for a long after spring).

Conversely, for a short after upthrust, target would be at the range low (sell-side liquidity).

**General Rule:**
Target is at the opposite edge of the box from which the fake occurred.

### Invalidation / skip conditions

#### The Failure Rule (Critical) [08:00-09:00]

"If price breaks below support and closes back above support, I wait for the first higher low or a bullish candle away from the level. If price does not close back above support, I skip the long" [08:30-09:00].

**Failed Reclaim Example [07:30-08:00]:**
"Price breaks below the low. The next candle tries to climb back into the range just like before. But here's the thing. It only gets halfway. The candle closes below the range, not above it" [07:30-08:00].

"That's not a spring. That's a failed reclaim, and it's the single most expensive setup in the Wyckoff playbook" [08:00].

"Anyone who entered long on the wick is sitting on a 5% loss" [08:00].

**Inverse for Upthrust [09:00]:**
"If price breaks above resistance and closes back inside the range, I wait for the first lower low or a bearish candle away from the level, then I can look for a short. If price closes above the range and holds above it, I do not fight the breakout" [09:00].

#### Level Flip Trap [11:00-12:00]

"The next candle opens, drops slightly, then pushes back up and holds above the old resistance line. That tells me the level is flipping into support...If I force the short here, my entry sits right into support" [11:30-12:00].

This indicates the breakout is real, not a fake. Skip the short and consider a long instead.

### Mechanizability

PARTIAL. Core mechanics are computable:
- Draw box (identify highs/lows of consolidation): computable with swing detection
- Sweep (price breaks box edge): computable
- Reclaim (close back inside box): computable
- First higher/lower low after reclaim: computable with swing detection

However, several discretionary elements prevent full automation:
- Identifying "the build" (when consolidation begins/ends) requires visual judgement
- Determining if a reclaim is "strong" or "weak" requires candlestick pattern analysis (body size, etc.)
- Detecting level flips (support becoming resistance) requires context beyond the three-box model
- Entry timing (exact price and order type) not specified

With defined rules (e.g., higher low = new low higher than previous swing low, close above previous higher low level), the strategy becomes mostly mechanizable.

## Advanced Concepts

### Spring (Buy-Side Liquidity Grab) [02:00-02:30]

Price breaks below range low (stops are triggered below the obvious level), then closes back above. This indicates institutional buying stepped in to absorb the selling and stop orders.

### Upthrust (Sell-Side Liquidity Grab) [02:30]

Price breaks above range high (breakout buyers enter), then closes back inside. This indicates institutional selling stepped in to trap the breakout buyers.

### Level Flip [11:00-12:00]

After a sweep and weak reclaim attempt, the price level that originally acted as support/resistance "flips" to act as the opposite (resistance becomes support). This signals the real breakout is happening in the opposite direction, and traders should not fade it.
