## Volume indicators explained

### 1. Average Volume
- Calculation: Most commonly 50-day average for stocks [01:30]
- Recommendation: Use same period as price MA being tracked [01:30]
- Threshold: Anything less than 50 days is not smooth [01:30]
- Use: Baseline for comparing current volume to normal range [implied]

### 2. Volume Momentum
- Concept: "Treats volume as it would be price" [02:00]
- Calculation: Change in volume over specific time interval [02:00]
- Measurements: Size of volume change relative to starting value [02:00]
- Sorting: Based on negative or positive closes [02:00]
- Application: "Quite strong in the smaller ones to notice buying or selling power taking place" [02:00]

### 3. Force Index
- Components: Direction of price change + extent of price change + trading volume [02:30]
- Calculation: Change in price multiplied by daily volume [02:30]
- Interpretation: Positive (above zero) = higher market, Negative (below zero) = lower market, Unchanged = zero line [03:00]
- Best use with Moving Average: 2-day EMA for timing entries/exits, 13-day EMA for longer-term analysis [03:30]
- Purpose: "Measures the force, or power, of bulls behind particular market rallies and of bears behind every decline" [02:30]

### 4. Volume Oscillator
- Components: Two moving averages of volume (fast and slow) [04:00]
- Default settings: Fast MA 14-period, Slow MA 34-period [04:00]
- Calculation: Fast volume MA subtracted from slow MA [04:00]
- Above zero line: Fast MA above slow MA, confirming price direction [04:30]
- Below zero line: Fast MA below slow MA, warning of weakness [04:30]
- Interpretation: Price change + volume increase = trend strength; Price change + volume decrease = weakness [04:00-05:00]

### 5. On Balance Volume (OBV)
- Concept: "Combines price and volume in an attempt to determine whether price movements are strong or are weak" [05:00]
- Calculation: Up day = add volume to previous OBV; Down day = subtract volume from previous OBV [05:30]
- Interpretation: Price change + volume increase = trend confirmation; Price change + volume decrease = weakness [05:30]

### 6. Money Flow Index (MFI)
- Classification: Momentum oscillator [06:00]
- Basis: "Measures the strength of money flowing in and out of a security/stock" [06:00]
- Relation to RSI: "Related to the relative strength index, but with a twist. While RSI only incorporates prices, the money flow index also incorporates the volume." [06:00]
- Overbought: MFI above 90, potential reversal or pullback [06:30]
- Oversold: MFI below 10, potential reversal or pullback [06:30]
- Divergence detection: Bearish divergence when prices rise but volume on up days less than down days; Bullish when prices fall but volume on down days less than up days [06:30-07:00]

### 7. Accumulation/Distribution (A/D) Line
- Also called: "Money flow" in some references [07:30]
- Calculation: Compares strength of close to open divided by trading range [07:30]
- Purpose: "Uses volume to confirm price trends or warn of weak movements that could result in price reversals" [08:00]
- Accumulation: Volume accumulated when close higher than previous close ("accumulation day") [08:00]
- Distribution: Volume distributed when close lower than previous close ("distribution day") [08:00]
- Main use: "Detect divergences between the price movement and volume movement" [08:30]

### 8. Price Volume Trend (PVT)
- Concept: "Combines percentage price change and volume in an attempt to confirm the strength of price trends or through divergences, warn of weak price movements" [08:30]
- Difference from others: Takes into consideration percentage price change, not just up/down direction [08:30]
- Calculation: 
  - Up day: Volume × percentage price increase, added to previous PVT value
  - Down day: Volume × percentage price decrease, added to previous PVT value [08:30]

### 9. Chaikin Money Flow
- Purpose: "Determines if an instrument is under accumulation or distribution" [09:00]
- Mechanism: "Compares the closing price to the high-low range of the trading session" [09:00]
- Measurement: "Buying and selling pressure over a set period of time" [09:00]
- Interpretation: 
  - Close near high + increased volume = CMF increases
  - Close near low + increased volume = CMF decreases [09:00-09:30]
