## Notable concepts discussed

**Volume candle width interpretation [00:30-07:00]:**
- Wide candles indicate heavy trading volume, strong agreement on price direction
- Narrow candles indicate light volume, weak conviction or indecision
- No wicks on wide candle = strong buying/selling pressure that held
- Long wicks on wide candle = intense fighting between buyers and sellers

**Truth-telling principles [09:30-14:00]:**
- High volume with price rise = genuine move
- Low volume with price rise = fake move or weak conviction (likely to reverse)
- Rising price with falling volume (thinner candles) = weakness, possible trend exhaustion [12:00]
- Falling price with rising volume (wider candles) = real selling pressure [12:30]

**Key level analysis [17:30-22:30]:**
- Wide candles at support/resistance = strong interest, high probability of level holding or breaking decisively
- Thin candles at key levels = hesitation, often precedes false break or reversal
- A break through key level needs wide candles to confirm real conviction
- Thin candles at major levels often precede "violent moves" [20:30]

**Entry signal mentions (incomplete):**

- Pin bar with higher volume than surrounding candles shows strong rejection [26:30, 27:00]
- Engulfing pattern with volume spike shows institutional action [28:00]
