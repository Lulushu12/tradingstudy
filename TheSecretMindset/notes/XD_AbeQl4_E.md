## Summary

This video presents a Smart Money Concept (SMC) price action strategy with four conditions: (1) Identify a Break of Structure (BOS) where price exceeds a recent swing high/low, (2) Identify a Change of Character (ChOC) where price reverses direction, (3) Draw Fibonacci levels from the BOS extreme to the ChOC extreme and look for pullbacks to the 50%-61.8% "golden zone," (4) Confirm entry with a wick rejection off that zone. The stop loss is placed just beyond the 78% Fibonacci level, and the target is set for 2x the risk (1:2 risk-reward ratio). The strategy applies to any timeframe through fractal price patterns.

## Strategy 1: SMC Price Action Trading

### Indicators and settings

**No technical indicators used.** Pure price action: Break of Structure, Change of Character, Fibonacci retracements (50%, 61.8%, 78% levels).

**Fibonacci Levels:**
- 50% retracement (first area of pullback interest)
- 61.8% retracement (extension of pullback zone, "golden zone")
- 78% retracement (stop loss placement level)

**For Downtrends [06:00-06:30]:**
Market making lower highs and lower lows. "The Crude Oil market, which was in a clear downtrend on the 4-hour time frame, with lower highs and lower lows, showing strong selling pressure overall" [06:00].

### Entry trigger

#### Condition 1: Break of Structure (BOS) [00:30-01:00]

"Break of Structure means the price has moved significantly past a point where it used to stop before, going in the same direction as before" [00:30].

**Uptrend BOS [00:30-01:00]:**
"In an uptrend, it's when the price goes higher than before...When the market keeps making higher highs and higher lows, in an uptrend, you pick the latest high point. If the price keeps going up and closes above this point for a while, that's a Break of Structure" [00:30-01:00].

**Downtrend BOS [01:00-01:30]:**
"In a downtrend, it's when the price goes lower than before...When the market's making lower lows and lower highs in a downtrend, you pick the most recent low. If the price keeps dropping and closes below this low, that's another Break of Structure" [01:00-01:30].

#### Condition 2: Change of Character (ChOC) [01:30-02:30]

"A Change of Character happens when the market starts to go in the opposite direction, after breaking its previous highs or lows. It's like a sudden and big change in how the price moves in the market" [01:30].

**Downtrend to Uptrend ChOC [01:30-02:00]:**
"For instance, we have this market that's been going down, making lower highs and lower lows. If suddenly the price breaks above its last major high point, that's a Change of Character. It suggests the market might be turning around, with buyers taking over" [01:30-02:00].

**Uptrend to Downtrend ChOC [02:00-02:30]:**
"Here, the price has been going up, making higher highs and higher lows...But then, after one of these breaks of structure, the price turns around and makes a new lower low. This suggests the trend might be changing" [02:00-02:30].

#### Condition 3: Fibonacci Pullback to 50%-61.8% Zone [02:30-04:00]

"Take the Fibonacci tool, and apply it from highest point of the break of structure, to the lowest point of the change of character. We're interested in pullbacks to the 50% - 61.8% Fibonacci area" [02:30-03:00].

"Price tends to go back about 50% of the distance after a big move, and even after smaller moves too. This happens a lot and you can see it on any chart. So, we'll keep an eye out for pullbacks to this Fibonacci golden zone, because they often show a high chance for the price to bounce back in the direction it was going before" [03:00-03:30].

**Goldzone Characteristics [03:30-04:00]:**
"The Fibonacci Golden Zone is a go-to gem for finding great trades...Big money traders watch these levels too. It's common knowledge that major players like banks use these levels in their trading" [03:30-04:00].

"It's a memory zone. If prices bounced there before, they'll likely bounce again. It's a good area to take trades if you missed it the first time" [04:00].

#### Condition 4: Wick Rejection [04:00-05:00]

"It's not enough for the price to just touch the zone. We need to see it reject off the level" [04:00-04:30].

"The fourth condition is a wick, rejecting the zone, a long thin line above or below the candle body. Wicks show fierce rejection of that level. When a wick spikes down, for example, but closes back up, it demonstrates the buyers have stepped in with force. Wicks reveal the price 'tested the waters' at the zone before rapidly bouncing back" [04:30-05:00].

"A long wick into the zone combined with the other factors screams rejection" [04:30-05:00].

### Stop loss

"Set your stop-loss just above or below the 78% Fibonacci level" [05:00-05:15].

**Important Note [07:30-08:00]:**
"Never put the stop loss so close to the 78% level. Leave the market some room for stop hunts, market noise and others" [07:30-08:00]. "Set your stop-loss just above the 78% Fibonacci level, to be safe" [08:30-09:00] (emphasis on "just above" rather than at exactly 78%).

### Take profit / exit

"Targeting double the amount we risked" [05:30-06:00], stated again at [06:30] and [09:30-10:00].

Formally: 1:2 risk-reward ratio (if risking X points, targeting 2X).

### Invalidation / skip conditions

1. **Price Breaks Through 78% Level:** If price closes below/above the 78% Fibonacci level (depending on direction), the stop loss is hit. Example: "The price reached 78% level, broke slightly below it, stopped me from the trade" [07:00-07:30].

2. **No ChOC After BOS:** If a break of structure doesn't lead to a change of character (price continues in original direction), the strategy is not triggered.

3. **Price Does Not Reach Fibonacci Zone:** If price does not retrace to the 50%-61.8% zone, the setup is not valid for entry.

4. **No Wick Rejection:** If price touches the Fibonacci zone but does not produce a wick showing rejection, entry is NOT taken [04:00-04:30].

### Mechanizability

PARTIAL. Break of Structure (price exceeding recent swing high/low) is mechanically computable. Change of Character (reversal after BOS) is mechanically computable. Fibonacci levels are mechanically computable from highest/lowest points. Wick rejection detection is mechanically computable (wick extends beyond zone; close is back inside). However, several gaps prevent full automation:
- Defining "which" swing high/low for BOS (in a complex chart, multiple options exist)
- Defining "big" or "sudden" ChOC (threshold not specified)
- Stop loss placement "just above/below" 78% (distance not quantified)
- Entry timing (on wick formation? close? exactly when?)
With reasonable assumptions (most recent swing, standard Fibonacci calculation, entry on wick formation at zone), the strategy becomes mostly mechanizable but requires discretionary decisions about which extremes to use.
