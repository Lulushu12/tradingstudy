## Summary

This video presents a 1-hour swing trading system built around Central Pivot Range (CPR), a daily-calculated support/resistance framework derived from pivot point formulas. The core framework uses CPR width and position changes to identify trending days and market bias. Three distinct entry approaches are layered on top: (1) trendline breakouts with CPR support/resistance, (2) candlestick formations (pin bars) at CPR zones, and (3) confluence between CPR and Ichimoku's Conversion Line. All strategies emphasize entering in the direction of the daily CPR trend and avoiding trades against it.

### Entry trigger

The video presents three distinct entry mechanisms, all operating within the CPR framework:

**Strategy 1: Trendline Breakout + CPR Confluence** [08:30-11:00]

Buy signal [09:00]:
1. Price breaks a downward trendline (upside breakout)
2. CPR is higher than previous day (uptrend condition)
3. Price tests the CPR from the upper side (price drops back to CPR level)

5. Entry: "long above the CPR" [11:00]
