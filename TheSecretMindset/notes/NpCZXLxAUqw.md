## Summary

A price action trading framework distinguishing between trending and ranging markets, with specific entry strategies for each regime. The core principle is reading market rhythm to identify whether a given day is trending (pullbacks are tradable) or ranging (only trade extremes with confirmation). Uses the 20-period EMA as dynamic support/resistance. ## Strategy 1: First Deep Pullback to EMA (Trending Day Entry)

### Indicators and settings

- 20-period EMA (Exponential Moving Average) [06:00]; speaker states "20-day EMA reacts to price quickly and follows price action closely"
- No other indicators used; price action alone

### Context / bias filter

- Strong uptrend condition [02:00-04:30]:
  - More green bars than red bars; consecutive green bars [03:30-04:00]
  - Green bodies larger than red bodies [04:00]
  - Green bars closing near their highs [04:00]
  - Long wicks on bottom of bars (selling rejection) [04:00]
  - Bars moving slowly upward creating parabolic curve [02:00]
  - Price stays "consistently above EMA, not touching for hours until a deeper correction" [06:30]
  - "One side is dominating the other one" [02:00]

- Strong downtrend condition [03:00-04:30]:
  - More red bars than green bars [03:30]
  - Red bars with average body size and wicks on top [03:30]
  - Green bar pullbacks small and short-lived [03:30]
  - Close of each candle lower than previous close [03:30]
  - Price moves in parabolic curve, not boxed-in [03:30]

### Entry trigger

- "First Deep Pullback" entry [07:30]: Price has moved far from EMA (not touching for hours), then touches EMA for first time as pullback to resume trend [07:30-08:00]
- Uptrend entry: Long when price touches 20 EMA during pullback in strong uptrend, expecting rubber-band effect back to original trend [07:30-08:00]
- Downtrend entry: Short when price touches 20 EMA during pullback in strong downtrend [07:00]
- As long as price stays above EMA, "you will only buy, never sell" [07:30]
- Look for "probability of 1st pullback of a strong trend to turn into reversal is quite rare" [08:00]

## Strategy 2: Trading Range Extremes (Range Day Entry)

### Indicators and settings

- 20-period EMA (in ranging markets cuts through middle of range) [13:00]
- No other indicators

### Context / bias filter

- Range day identification [08:30-12:30]:
  - Filled with wicks, especially at top and bottom boundaries [09:00]
  - Dojis and overlapping bars indicate indecisiveness [12:00]
  - Alternating bullish and red bars of equal quantity [10:30]
  - Red and green bars "somewhat equal in quantity" [10:30]
  - "No consistency in strength from either side"; "strength is weak, shines and dies very quickly" [10:30-11:00]
  - Price oscillates between upper and lower boundary without breakout [08:30]
  - EMA cuts through middle of range; price interacts with EMA very closely [13:00]
  - "Both sides very active, creates balance between buyers and sellers" [11:00]

- Identified by multiple failed breakout attempts before real breakout [09:30]
- "Dojis are the early signs for Range formation" [12:00]

### Entry trigger

- Enter "at the extremes of the range, wait for clear confirmation and stay out of the middle" [00:30]
- "Long at the lower boundary: buy when price reaches lower edge of range with multiple wicks showing seller failure [09:00-09:30]
- Short at upper boundary: sell when price reaches upper edge of range with multiple wicks showing buyer failure [09:00-09:30]
- Entering at extremes "offers great risk-reward ratio for traders" [09:30]
- "Wicks are failures"; bottom wicks mean sellers failed [09:30]
- Wait for "confirmation and proof one side is actually failing" before entering at extremes [11:30]
- Look for "two or more bars have the same High or Low"; mark that level and "watch for reaction when price revisit that level" [12:30]
