## Summary
This video teaches volume-price analysis (VSA) principles and presents five distinct candlestick/volume patterns that signal reversals or continuations. The core concept is that volume and price movement must align (effort = result); when they diverge, the speaker identifies it as a setup opportunity. The video builds toward a Wyckoff method framework (mentioned at the end but not fully explained) and emphasizes reading the relationship between volume and candle size rather than colors alone.
