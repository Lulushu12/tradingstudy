## Summary
This video critiques the popular moving average crossover strategy taught by "most market gurus" and offers a more refined approach based on 10+ years of trading experience. The speaker argues that MA crossovers fail on lower timeframes but can work on daily and higher timeframes when combined with proper price action analysis and stock fundamentals. The video teaches both why MA crossovers fail and how to use moving averages correctly as trend confirmation and support/resistance tools.

## Notable claims and caveats

- Moving average crossovers advertised as easy (like "printing money") but actual results are poor: "10%-15% of trading days, if you're lucky" [00:00]
- "Most of market gurus" teach oversimplified MA crossover strategies that don't work [00:00]
- Speaker's hard truth: "Moving averages crossovers don't work on lower time frames. Period." [01:30]
- Tested "hundreds, thousands of combinations" with poor results on lower timeframes [01:30]
- Moving averages are lagging indicators: "Price moves first and the moving average moves second" [02:00]
- "You are always behind the price" with MAs [02:00]
- Cannot forecast new trends with MAs [02:00]
- Noise/false signals are a major problem on lower timeframes [03:00]
- Sideways/range-bound markets are "frustrating" for crossover strategies [03:30]
- "If you found a magic crossover that works on lower timeframes, go ahead and share it with me" [01:30]
- On higher timeframes, "you might be able to profit from crossovers" [02:00]
- Standard "stop and reverse" crossover system keeps trader in market at all times, which is risky [06:30]
- Speaker takes only long signals and "completely ignore[s] short signals" [07:00]
- "Wait for the candle to close and confirm the crossover, it will save you a lot of bad entries" [07:30]
- "Extremely late exits" from crossover reversal signals "can severely cut into your profits" [08:00]
- "No universal moving average crossover that works on any market" - requires continuous backtesting and adjustment [09:00]
- Stock screeners recommended to efficiently search for stocks matching crossover criteria [09:30]
- Fundamentals filter is crucial: "it's good to have fundamentals on your side" [09:30]
- No discussion of transaction costs, spreads, slippage, or commissions
