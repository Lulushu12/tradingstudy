## Summary
A stock screening framework for identifying tradeable stocks in the day trading and swing trading universe. The video presents 10 objective scanning criteria designed to filter out illiquid, low-volatility, and high-risk stocks, leaving a pre-qualified pool of candidates suitable for intraday trading. Criteria focus on volume, price level, volatility (ATR and daily range), spreads, news stability, fundamentals (EPS growth), trend confirmation (200 MA), and regulatory risk. This is a stock selection tool, not a trading strategy with entry/exit rules.

### Scanning Criteria (10 Filters)

**Criterion 1: Average Daily Volume** [00:30-01:30]
- Minimum: 1 million shares traded per day (50-day average) [00:30]
- Rationale: Liquidity, prevent market-maker manipulation, ensure sufficient intraday trading volume [00:30-01:30]
- Claim: "Any stock that is trading under 1 million shares per day can be easily manipulated by market makers" [01:00]
- Warning: Low-volume stocks are "extremely speculative and unpredictable" [01:30]

**Criterion 2: Stock Price Level** [02:00-03:00]
- Minimum: $50 per share [02:00]
- Preference: Triple-digit stocks ($100+) [02:00]
- Rationale: These price ranges have "most highly traded stocks, but also have the largest volatility" [02:00]. Avoid penny stocks (<$5) [02:30]
- Warning: Penny stocks may have no buyers/sellers for days despite large % moves; a position bought at $1, sold at $2, may not execute if no buyers exist [02:30-03:00]

**Criterion 3: ATR (Average True Range) Volatility Expansion** [03:30-04:00]
- Measure: Attach 100-day EMA to ATR indicator [04:00]
- Signal: Trade when ATR breaks above its 100-day EMA, indicating significant expansion in daily trading range [03:30-04:00]
- Rationale: "The ideal periods to enter the market is when the ATR shows expansion periods in the daily range" [03:30]
- Implication: If ATR is low, wait for another day with more favorable conditions [03:30-04:00]

**Criterion 4: Average Daily Range** [04:30-05:00]
- Target: High daily price range; example: $5 daily range preferred over $0.50 range [04:30-05:00]
- Rationale: "Traders want to find stocks that have a high price range, making for more trading opportunities" [04:30-05:00]. Each market has its own personality in daily range [04:30]
- Warning: A stock stuck in $0.50 range makes profitable trading difficult without large position sizes [05:00]

**Criterion 5: Narrow Bid-Ask Spreads** [05:30-06:30]
- Principle: Bid = highest price buyer willing to pay; Ask = lowest price seller willing to accept [05:30]
- Target: Narrow spread (small difference between bid and ask) [05:30-06:00]
- Relationship to volume: "Spread is a byproduct of the daily trading volume. Naturally, stocks with large volume will positively influence the spread. More liquid stocks... will have a narrower spread." [06:00-06:30]
- Warning: "Large stock spreads can damage your profit potential" [06:00]; low-volume stocks exhibit wide spreads that "wipe out the profit potential" [06:30]

**Criterion 6: No Major News Headlines** [06:30-07:00]
- Rule: "If your stock is constantly in the news headlines due to a major economic issue taking place, then don't trade it. Wait it out." [06:30-07:00]
- Rationale: "Stocks that are constantly affected by the news tend to be highly volatile, and they tend to have very unpredictable charting patterns" [07:00]
- Advice for beginners: "Don't try to anticipate the market reaction to big events... the market often does exactly the opposite of what you'd think it SHOULD do. As a beginner, you're supposed to be avoiding the noise." [07:00]

**Criterion 7: Earnings Per Share (EPS) Growth** [07:30-08:00]
- Target: EPS growth rate >= 20% compared with previous year [08:00]
- Applicability: Optional for day traders; important for swing traders going long [07:30]
- Calculation: "Simply divide a company's net income by its number of shares outstanding" [07:30-08:00]
- Claim: "Stocks with earnings-per-share growth rates of at least 20% compared with the previous year suggest a company has products or services in strong demand" [08:00]. "Strong earnings growth... has the greatest impact on its future price performance" [08:00]
- Plus: "Even better if the earnings-per-share growth rate has been accelerating in recent quarters and years" [08:00]

**Criterion 8: Day Highs and Lows / Breakout Identification** [08:00-09:00]
- Concept: Breakouts = major price moves following consolidation periods [08:30]
- Filters by timeframe:
  - Short-term breakouts: Trading above 30-day highs [08:30-09:00]
  - Medium-term breakouts: Trading above 90-day highs [09:00]
  - Long-term breakouts: Trading at 52-week highs [09:00]
- Rationale: "Breakouts are prime opportunities for traders because a stock has the potential not only to make a big move in a short period, but also to continue that directional move by gaining momentum" [08:30-09:00]

**Criterion 9: 200-Day Moving Average for Trend Confirmation** [09:00-09:30]
- Rule: "If price is consistently trading above the 200 moving average, this can be viewed as an upward trending market. Markets trading below the 200 moving average are seen to be in a downtrend." [09:00-09:30]
- Application: "Traders commonly do this to analyze the general market trend and then look to only place trades in the direction of the long-term trend" [09:30]
- Rationale: "The 200 moving average is widely used by traders because it is seen as a good indicator of the long term trend in the market" [09:00]

**Criterion 10: Avoid Government Regulation-Affected Stocks** [09:30-10:00]
- Rule: "If the stock is affected by government regulations or approvals—I do not trade it." [09:30-10:00]
- Examples: Biotech stocks, military defense stocks [10:00]
- Warning: These stocks "tend to move even 50 percent in one single day when the news hits the wire (good or bad news), whether it be the FDA approving or declining a new product, for example" [10:00]
