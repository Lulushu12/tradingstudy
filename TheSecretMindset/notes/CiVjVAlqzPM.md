## Summary

The video teaches a day trading strategy using V-bottom and V-top chart patterns filtered with Anchored VWAP (calculated from the low/high of the V-pattern). The strategy waits for a breakout of the neckline (previous swing high/low), then enters on a retest when the neckline aligns with the Anchored VWAP, creating a confluence zone. Optional filters include central pivot points and candlestick confirmation patterns (pin bars, long-wicked candles).

## Strategy 1: V-Bottom Reversal with Anchored VWAP (LONG)

### Indicators and settings

- V-bottom pattern: "price momentum switches from aggressive selling to aggressive buying. This is a powerful bullish reversal pattern" [00:30]
- Neckline: "the previous swing high before the bottom" [01:00-01:30]
- Anchored VWAP: "We'll apply it on the lowest low of a V-bottom formation" [03:00-03:30]

### Context / bias filter

- "The V-bottom should be at a new low point in the market. Meaning you can't trade the formation if there are recent lows below it" [03:30-04:00]
- Market shows clear reversal from downtrend: price "traded lower into a bottom and immediately reversed higher with a massive increase in momentum" [01:00]

### Entry trigger

1. "Market forms a V-bottom formation" [04:00]
2. "Wait for price to break the neckline of the pattern... This is basically an interruption in the previous downtrend" [01:00-01:30]
3. "we plan to enter a long position once price retests the neckline. But with several conformations" [01:30]
4. "If the neckline, the static support, aligns with the Anchored Vwap, acting a dynamic support, you buy at the re-test of the neckline" [04:00]

Optional confirmation: "I like to see either a pin bar, or a candlestick with an evident wick, or an engulfing bar, forming at the VWAP – pivot point area" [12:00]

### Stop loss

- "Stop loss goes below VWAP" [06:00]
- When using central pivot filter: "Stop loss placement, below VWAP and below the central pivot point" [10:00]
- Management: "Once price breaks [swing high], we remove the risk from the trade, by moving our stop to breakeven" [07:00]

### Take profit / exit

- Primary target: "my target is the next swing high in the price structure" [06:00]
- Alternative target: "set above the neckline, equal to the distance measured from the low of the pattern to the neckline high" [06:00-06:30]
- Risk-reward requirement: "minimum risk to reward ratio should be at least 2" [06:00]
- Move stop to breakeven once certain swing levels broken [07:00, 10:30]

### Invalidation / skip conditions

- If there are recent lows below the V-bottom low, do not trade [03:30-04:00]

## Strategy 2: V-Top Reversal with Anchored VWAP (SHORT)

### Indicators and settings

- V-top pattern: "price momentum switches from aggressive buying to aggressive selling. This is a bearish reversal pattern" [02:00]
- Neckline: previous swing low before the top
- Anchored VWAP: "on the highest high of a V-top formation" [03:00-03:30]

### Context / bias filter

- "The V-top should be at a new high point in the market. So you can't trade the formation if there are recent highs above it" [04:00-04:30]
- "It is nearly impossible to predict V-tops, and will wait for price to break the neckline of the pattern" [02:00-02:30]
- Conservative entry: "wait for a break and close below the neckline and to attempt a short once price pulls back to the neckline and gets rejected" [02:00]

### Entry trigger

1. "Market forms a V-top formation" [04:30]
2. Break and close below the neckline [02:00]
3. "If the re-test of the neckline, the static resistance, aligns with the Anchored Vwap, as dynamic resistance, you look to sell the re-test of the neckline" [04:30-05:00]

Confirmation: "We have a pin bar, rejecting the VWAP, at the re-test of the breakout" [08:00]

### Stop loss

- "Stop loss above the VWAP and above the high of the pin bar" [08:30]
- When using pivot filter: "Stop loss above the VWAP line and above the pivot level" [12:00]
- Move to breakeven once swing low broken [12:30]

### Take profit / exit

- Target swing low: "Our target is this major swing low" [08:30]
- "we'll take our profit at the next major swing low in the price structure" [12:00]
- Risk-reward examples shown: 4:1 [07:30], 3:1 [10:30], 6:1 [12:30]

### Invalidation / skip conditions

- If there are recent highs above the V-top high, do not trade [04:00-04:30]

## Mechanizability

PARTIAL

V-pattern detection (new high/low with reversal momentum) and Anchored VWAP are mechanically computable from OHLCV data. Swing high/low identification can be automated using local extrema. However, critical gaps remain: (1) "aggressive momentum" lacks quantitative thresholds (no ATR multiples or % move criteria); (2) confluence definition vague ("aligns" not quantified in terms of distance tolerance); (3) retest entry exact point not specified (wick vs. close); (4) swing selection for profit target has no objective rule; (5) pin bar and engulfing bar patterns are not mechanically defined; (6) central pivot integration is discretionary. The core setup framework is mechanizable, but entry confirmation timing and exit precision require manual judgment.
