## Summary

This video teaches a confluence-based price action strategy focused on trendline breaks combined with support/resistance flips and retests. The core principle applies to both reversal and continuation patterns: identify when a trendline breaks, wait for a retest to the broken line, confirm via support/resistance role reversal, and enter on the retest if price bounces. The strategy emphasizes multiple timeframe alignment, volume confirmation, and candlestick pattern validation. Examples apply to XAUUSD (gold) but the speaker states it works on crude oil and blue-chip stocks.

## Strategy 1: Bearish Reversal - Broken Uptrend with Retest

- support_level: a horizontal support level that acts as key resistance after flip [02:00]
- no_indicator_settings: Strategy is based on price action, not technical indicators

### Context / bias filter
Price must be in an established uptrend with an upward trendline clearly visible showing "consistent buying pressure" [00:30-01:00]. A horizontal support level must exist that will flip to resistance [02:00].

### Entry trigger

### Confluence concept (applies to all setups)
All strategies rely on confluence of multiple factors:
- A diagonal break (trendline) [01:00, 06:30, 13:30, 18:30, 23:30]
- A horizontal break or level flip (support/resistance) [02:00, 07:00, 13:30, 19:00, 24:00]

### Volume confirmation (applies to all setups)
