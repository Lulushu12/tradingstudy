## Indicator 2: On Balance Volume (OBV)

### Mechanics
OBV rises if current close is higher than previous close; OBV falls if current close is lower [02:30]. Operated on assumption that volume movements precede price changes [03:00].

### Usage Rules
- If OBV rising above zero: suggests accumulation and buying pressure [03:30]
- If OBV falling below zero: suggests distribution and selling pressure [03:30]
- Use to confirm trends when OBV and price move together [04:30]
- Identify imbalances: price lower low but OBV higher low suggests reversal potential [05:00-05:30]

## Indicator 3: Hull Moving Average

### Settings
- Three Hull variations available [06:00]
- Visualization: as bands or single line [06:00]
- Can display higher timeframe Hull MA on current chart [06:30]

### Usage Rules
- Price above Hull line sloping upward: bullish trend, consider long [07:00-07:30]
- Price below Hull line sloping downward: bearish trend, consider short [07:30]
- Price crosses above Hull: shift from bearish to bullish [08:00]
- Price crosses below Hull: shift from bullish to bearish [08:00]
- Prices touching or bouncing off line: potential entry/exit points [08:00-08:30]

### Features
- Reduced lag compared to traditional moving averages [07:00]
- Can paint candlesticks according to trend [06:00-06:30]
- Multi-timeframe capability for filtering noise [08:30]
