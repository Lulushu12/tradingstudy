## Strategy 1: Supply and Demand Zone Trading with Heiken Ashi

### Indicators and settings
- Heiken Ashi: chart type (no parameters)
- No additional indicators used

### Context / bias filter
- In an uptrend: demand areas are being respected, supply areas are being taken out [12:00]
- In a downtrend: supply areas are being respected, demand areas are being taken out [12:30]
- First-time retrace to the base is the strongest to enter [10:00]
- Focus on fresh zones (price has not pulled back yet to the zone) [04:30]
- Prefer zones where price made a strong move away from them [06:30]

### Entry trigger
- Buy: "when the price action approaches a demand level and bounces upwards forming a green Heiken Ashi candle, ideally with no lower wick" [11:00]
- Sell: "when the price reaches a supply level and bounces downwards, forming a red Heiken Ashi candle, ideally with no upper shadows" [11:00]

### Stop loss
- For long trades: "put a stop loss order right below the demand area" [11:30]
- For short trades: "put your stop loss order right above the supply area" [11:30]

### Take profit / exit
- "hold your trades until the price action reaches an opposite level on the chart. So, if you are trading long a demand level, you should hold your trade until the price action reaches the next supply zone on the chart" [11:30]
- Alternative: "close a part of your position after a Heiken Ashi color change" [11:30]

### Invalidation / skip conditions
- Skip zones that are "used up": "Price has pulled back to level several times, and these are not good for trading full swings" [04:30]
- Avoid weak zones where "price moving down in an unconvincing manner" [07:00]
- Zones with "several Heiken Ashi inside the zone" are weaker than zones with single rejection candles [08:00]
