## Summary

This video teaches the Choppiness Index indicator as a tool to identify choppy/consolidating markets versus trending markets, and presents three swing trading techniques using it. The core principle is to avoid trading in the 80% of the time when markets are choppy [01:00], and only trade when the Choppiness Index confirms a trending market. The video emphasizes price action and support/resistance as primary tools, with Choppiness Index as confirmation.

## Choppiness Index Indicator Levels

- Range: 0 to 100 (directionless indicator)
- Choppy/Consolidating: above 61.8 [02:00-02:30]
- Trending: below 38.2 [02:30-03:00]
- End of Trend Warning: below 25 [03:00-03:30]
- Sideways trend continuation: Choppiness Index above 50 [02:00]
- Strong trending zone: Choppiness Index below 45 (used in Technique 2) [05:30]

## Strategy 1: Breakout Trade After High Choppiness Readings

### Context / bias filter

Wait for a prolonged period of consolidation (Choppiness Index above 61.8) [04:00-04:30]. The market is "practically flat" during this time [04:30]. Do not trade during consolidation; wait for the exit signal.

### Entry trigger

Enter a trade when the Choppiness Index falls below the 61.8 level after a prolonged period of high readings [04:30]. This signals the "trend inception" [04:30]. Coincide this with a "valid breakout" in price action [04:30-05:00]. The direction of the trade (long or short) follows the direction of the price breakout [04:30-05:30].

## Strategy 2: Ride the Trend When Choppiness is Below 45

### Context / bias filter

Determine the main trend first using price action, support and resistance, and a moving average [05:30-06:00]. Once the main trend is identified, look for swing trading opportunities when the Choppiness Index stays below 45 for "a long period" [05:30].

### Entry trigger

Look for swing opportunities in the direction of the main trend when Choppiness Index is below 45 [05:30-06:00]. The speaker shows examples: entering long when price is above a moving average and Choppiness is below 45 [06:00]; entering short when price is making consecutive lows, Choppiness is below 45, and price is below a moving average [06:30].

## Strategy 3: Choppiness Index with Volume Confirmation

### Context / bias filter

Identify a consolidation range (high Choppiness readings, low volume, sideways price action) [07:30-08:00]. Wait for volume to start increasing while price is still in the range [07:30].

### Entry trigger

1. Choppiness Index falls below 61.8 after prolonged high readings [08:00]
2. Price breaks out of the consolidation range [08:00]
3. Volume increases during and after the breakout [08:00-08:30]
