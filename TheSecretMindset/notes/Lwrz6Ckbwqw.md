## Summary
This video explains Heikin-Ashi charting methodology, a Japanese technique for trend identification. Heikin-Ashi candlesticks are calculated differently from standard candlesticks, smoothing price action to filter noise and make trends easier to spot. The video covers how to read Heikin-Ashi patterns, their advantages for trend trading, disadvantages for short-term trading, and how to combine them with price action analysis.

## Strategy 1: Heikin-Ashi Trend Following with Doji Reversal Signals

### Indicators and settings
- Heikin-Ashi charting method applied to H4 or D1 timeframes [09:30]
- No additional technical indicators required; described as combining with price action rules [09:30]

### Context / bias filter
A persistent trend must be established. "If your goal is to catch longer and persistent trends, then using a Heikin-Ashi chart will help you toward that end." [09:30]
Uptrend context: Heikin-Ashi bars have big bodies, long upper shadows, no lower shadow [04:30]
Downtrend context: Heikin-Ashi bars have big bodies, long lower shadows, no upper shadow [05:00]

### Entry trigger
1. Enter in direction of trend continuation when trend is clearly established with no lower shadows (uptrend) or no upper shadows (downtrend) [06:00]
2. Reversal trigger: "Reversal candlesticks in the Heikin-Ashi charts look like regular doji candlesticks. They have no or very small bodies but long upper and lower shadows. The doji, when it appears after a directional move, has a reversal potential and indicates that the price action is stalling and might be poised to start a counter trend move." [05:30]

## Notable claims and caveats

- Heikin-Ashi candlesticks do not show true prices; they are derived from true prices and should be treated "more of an indicator than a price chart itself" [06:30]
- Signals are delayed/lagging compared to standard candlesticks. "So a Heikin-Ashi chart is slower than a candlestick chart and its signals are delayed. They work somehow like a moving average." [03:00]
- Heikin-Ashi eliminates "unnecessary false signals" and "choppy movements" [07:00]
- Drawback: "Heikin-Ashi smoothed signals don't let you to notice the reversal in time" [08:00]
- Specifically NOT recommended for short-term trading and scalping (1-min and 5-min charts): "short term trading could be inefficient when using Heikin-Ashi" [09:00]
- Entry can be late, but this can help traders "stay in the trade without being nervous or making any unnecessary moves" [07:30]
