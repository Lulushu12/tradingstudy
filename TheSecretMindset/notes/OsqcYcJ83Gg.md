## Summary
This video explains the Break of Structure (BoS) pattern, which signals a potential trend reversal or weakening. The pattern is identified when market structure changes (e.g., from higher highs/lows to lower highs/lows). Three variations are presented: basic entry near the pattern, entry via supply/demand zone confirmation, and entry via divergence confirmation with oscillators.

## Strategy 1: Basic Break of Structure Entry

### Indicators and settings
None required; price action only

### Context / bias filter
**Identify prevailing trend first**:
- For SHORT (bearish BoS): Series of higher highs followed by series of higher lows visible [06:30]
- For LONG (bullish BoS): Series of lower lows followed by series of lower highs visible [07:00]

Then identify the break: price makes lower low (in uptrend) or higher high (in downtrend) [01:00-02:00]

### Entry trigger
**Bearish Break of Structure** (after uptrend with HH/HL):
- Price creates lower low (LL)
- Entry: At zone between the right wave and the highest high [06:30]

**Bullish Break of Structure** (after downtrend with LL/LH):
- Price creates higher high (HH)
- Entry: At zone between the right wave and the lowest low [07:00]

### Stop loss
- For SHORT: Above the last higher high (HH) [06:30]
- For LONG: Below the last lower low (LL) [07:00]

### Take profit / exit
- First target (TP1): Near the first valley/peak, at the low of the first down leg [06:30] or high of first up leg [07:00]
- Second target (TP2): At the area of the lower low (for shorts) or higher high (for longs) [06:30-07:00]

## Strategy 2: Break of Structure + Supply/Demand Zone

### Indicators and settings
None required; price action only

### Context / bias filter
Same as Strategy 1 - identify break of structure first

### Entry trigger
1. Identify the break of structure pattern
2. Wait for price to retrace back to the first wave [08:00]
3. Find supply or demand zone for more accurate entry [08:00]

For **bullish BoS** (buying):
- Look at left side of previous low where strong rally started [08:30]
- Find origin/start point of that rally
- Demand zone = drawn from last BEARISH candle before the up move [09:00]
- Enter when price returns to this demand zone [09:00]

For **bearish BoS** (shorting):
- Look at left side of previous high where strong down move started [10:00]
- Find origin of strong down move
- Supply zone = drawn from last BULLISH candle before down move [10:00]
- Entry: When price returns to supply area [10:00]

### Stop loss
- For bullish: Below the lowest low [09:00]
- For bearish: Above the higher high (or highest high reached) [10:30]

### Take profit / exit
- TP1: First valley/peak level
- TP2: Second valley/peak level
- Can leave portion open for bigger move [09:30]

### Invalidation / skip conditions
If supply/demand zone is not found, back off from trade [09:00]

### Claimed performance
None stated

### Vagueness log
1. "Strong rally" and "strong down move" are SUBJECTIVE
2. "Last BEARISH candle before up move" requires visual identification - VISUAL-ONLY
3. Zone width/size is NOT STATED
4. "Recent swings" reference for trailing is SUBJECTIVE

### Mechanizability
PARTIAL - Break of structure identification is mostly mechanical, but supply/demand zone placement requires VISUAL-ONLY judgment.

## Strategy 3: Break of Structure + Divergence Confirmation

### Indicators and settings
- MACD, RSI, or Stochastic for divergence detection

### Context / bias filter
Identify break of structure pattern first [13:00]

### Entry trigger
1. Identify BoS pattern
2. Wait for price to retrace/return
3. At the third wave of the structure, confirm with divergence:
   - **Bearish divergence**: Price makes new high but oscillator makes lower high [13:00]
   - **Bullish divergence**: Price makes new low but oscillator makes higher high [NOT STATED but implied symmetric]

4. Example: Bearish BoS with Stochastic divergence - enter short when price rallies to initial high but Stochastic refuses new level [13:00-13:30]

### Stop loss
Above the last higher high (HH) [13:30]

### Take profit / exit
- TP1: Near first valley
- TP2: At lower low area [13:30]

### Invalidation / skip conditions
If divergence does not appear at retest, skip the trade
