## Strategy 2: Support and Resistance Level Trading

### Indicators and settings

- Support/resistance: visually obvious price levels where the market tends to pause or reverse [09:30]
- Ideal characteristics: sharp, big reversals [07:30-08:00], multiple bounces off the level [08:00-08:30], swing highs/lows [08:30], ability to act as both support and resistance (role reversal) [09:00], visually obvious on chart without squinting [09:30-10:00], recently respected levels [10:00-10:30], round numbers ending in zero or double zeros [06:30], zones rather than exact lines [11:30]

### Context / bias filter

In strong trends, support and resistance levels help catch big moves [20:00]. In ranging markets, price bounces between support and resistance without clear direction [21:00]. Check higher timeframes to ensure the setup fits the bigger picture [19:30].

### Entry trigger

- **In uptrend:** Look for entry when price dips to old resistance levels which now act as support; watch for breakouts when price breaks above consolidation high [20:30]
- **In downtrend:** Look for bounces from resistance levels, consolidations, and breakdowns below support [20:30-21:00]
- **In range:** Enter when price bounces up from support with force or drops hard from resistance [21:00-21:30]
- **Retest method:** When support/resistance level breaks, wait for a retest of that level before entering [13:30]

### Stop loss

Place stops just beyond key support/resistance levels to catch liquidity sweeps, but NOT at obvious levels (that's where inducement targets) [81:30]

### Take profit / exit

- Use the opposite end of the range as exit target in ranging markets [80:30]
- Exit at new established resistance/support after a flip [12:00-13:00]

### Invalidation / skip conditions

False breakouts occur: price breaks through S/R level only to reverse [11:30]. Be cautious in choppy or ranging markets where flipped levels may not hold [14:00-14:30]. If a former support level fails to hold as new resistance, a strong move in opposite direction may occur [14:00].

## Strategy 4: Supply and Demand Zones (Fresh Unmitigated Zones)

### Indicators and settings

- Pivot Zones: form at key turning points, marked by one or two candlesticks with distinct characteristics [26:00-26:30]
- Demand zone signal: strong bearish candle where next candle closes back above its high [26:30]
- Supply zone signal: strong bullish candle where next candle closes below its low [26:30-27:00]
- Continuation structures: Rally-Base-Rally (uptrend) or Drop-Base-Drop (downtrend) [27:00-27:30]
- Reversal structures: Drop-Base-Rally or Rally-Base-Drop [27:30-28:00]

### Context / bias filter

Identify fresh unmitigated supply and demand zones, not ones tested multiple times [30:00-30:30]. Zones that form after explosive price movements are most relevant [31:00]. The less time price spends in a zone, the more out of balance supply/demand are and the higher the probability of successful trade on retest [29:30-30:00].

### Entry trigger

Enter when price revisits a fresh zone. Validate with a clear Break of Structure (BOS) - price must make a decisive move away from the zone and take out nearby swing highs or lows [31:00-31:30]. Use confluence: if zone has liquidity in front of it (swing low, consolidation pattern) suggesting traders are waiting at that level, probability is higher [32:30-33:00].

Conservative approach: wait for candlestick pattern at the level (bullish reversal pattern like pin bar or engulfing at demand zone) [36:00-36:30]. Advanced: look for reversal pattern on lower timeframe (e.g., double bottom or CHoCH on 15-min at 1-hour zone) [36:30-37:00].

### Stop loss

### Invalidation / skip conditions

A zone becomes mitigated (weaker) after being tested multiple times; fresh zones are prioritized [30:00-30:30]. If there's no clear Break of Structure and price just hovers around the zone, the zone might not be as strong [31:30-32:00]. If a flip zone (former supply becoming demand) fails to hold, a strong move in opposite direction occurs [32:00].

## Strategy 5: Candlestick Pattern Reading (Price Action Method)

### Indicators and settings

**Trend Bar:** Body of candle takes up at least half of entire bar range [41:00-41:30]. Represents strong market sentiment and likely continuation in direction of candle [41:00].

**Trend-Based Long Wick Candle:** Wick sticks out in same direction as moving trend; smaller body and longer wick indicates better quality [42:00]. Upper wick acts as resistance, lower wick acts as support [42:00-42:30].

**Momentum Candle:** Larger spread compared to previous candles, showing clear directional movement [42:30-43:00]. In uptrend, each candle gets larger, moving greater distance per candle (gain in bullish momentum) [43:00]. In downtrend, each candle gets larger (gain in bearish momentum) [43:00].

**Inside Bar:** Neither takes out high nor low of predecessor [43:30-44:00]. Small spread, signifies loss of momentum and uncertainty [44:00].

**Multiple Candle Rejection:** Several candles in a row failing to break through specific price level, each forming long wicks in same direction [44:30-45:00].

**Decreasing Candles:** Successive candles shrinking in size as prices approach critical point [45:30-46:00]. Indicates loss of momentum [46:00].

### Context / bias filter

Analyze candlesticks in context of overall price move: beginning, middle, or end of trend? At support/resistance or middle of consolidation? [47:00]. Never analyze candlesticks in isolation [47:00].

### Entry trigger

- Look for consecutive trend bars closing in same direction as prevailing trend [41:30]
- For reversal: long wick candle at key support/resistance level indicates rejection; in uptrend, lower wick at support means sellers failed to close with momentum [42:00-42:30]
- For continuation: in uptrend, momentum candles with each getting larger signal buyers in full control [43:00-43:30]
- For indecision reversal: inside bar at crucial spot (major support/resistance) - break of inside bar can trigger traders [44:00-44:30]
- For rejection zones: multiple long wicks in same direction at key level = strong selling or buying presence [45:00-45:30]
- For trend exhaustion: decreasing candles finishing with multiple long wick candles at key level = loss of momentum [46:00-46:30]

NOT STATED

### Invalidation / skip conditions

Absence of trend bars over past 10 or so candles suggests market consolidating [41:30]. Inside bar is just uncertainty; confirms potential for breakout but does not guarantee direction [44:00-44:30].

## Strategy 12: Wyckoff Method - Four Phases

### Indicators and settings

**Phase 1 - Accumulation:** Prices move sideways at market bottoms; big players buy quietly [90:30-91:00]. Signs: prices stop falling and move sideways, low volume but picks up on price dips, price drops don't last long, strong buying when prices fall a bit [91:00].

**Phase 2 - Markup (Uptrend):** Prices start to climb; big players let prices rise [91:00-91:30]. Signs: higher highs and higher lows, volume often increases as prices rise, pullbacks are short and shallow, each new high met with strong buying [91:30-92:00].

**Phase 3 - Distribution:** Market tops; big players sell slowly [92:00-92:30]. Signs: prices stop rising and move sideways, volume might increase but price rises don't last, more volatility with sharp ups and downs, each new high quickly sold into [92:30].

**Phase 4 - Markdown (Downtrend):** Prices start falling; big players finished selling [92:30-93:00]. Signs: lower lows and lower highs, volume often increases as prices fall, bounces are short and weak, each new low met with more selling [93:00-93:30].

### Context / bias filter

Most money made in phase 2 (uptrend) and phase 4 (downtrend) [93:30]. Transition phases (Accumulation and Distribution) trickier to trade; more about preparing for next trend [93:30].

### Entry trigger

Entry opportunities at end of Accumulation phase (after successful Spring pattern) [98:30]. Entry opportunities at end of Distribution phase [98:30]. Use Wyckoff events as clues for phase identification.

### Stop loss

NOT STATED

### Take profit / exit

NOT STATED

### Invalidation / skip conditions
