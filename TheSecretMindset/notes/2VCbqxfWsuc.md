## Notable techniques mentioned

**Free Position / Trailing Stop Method** [07:30-08:30]:
- When a trade is "going in your favor," move stop loss following the price upward
- "Even if the price suddenly drops, you won't lose any money" once stop is moved
- Described as "setting a safety net that moves up with the price"
- Allows squeezing "every bit of profit from the trade"
- Contrast with "setting a fixed target and calling it a day"—trailing stop allows capture of additional profits
- Example: frustration of closing trade at target, then seeing price continue higher
- Discipline required: "resist making any changes" to chart setup for at least one month [09:00-09:30]
