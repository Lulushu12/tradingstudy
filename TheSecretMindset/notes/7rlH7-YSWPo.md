## Summary
This video teaches how to trade with Parabolic SAR (Stop and Reverse) indicator, emphasizing its use as an exit tool rather than an entry trigger. The strategy combines Parabolic SAR with Ichimoku Cloud to create a high-probability trading system. The speaker demonstrates the approach on both forex and stock examples (EUR/JPY and Tesla), emphasizing the use of higher timeframes (4-hour or daily) to avoid false signals from market noise.

## Strategy 1: Parabolic SAR with Ichimoku Cloud (PSAR + Kumo)

### Indicators and settings
Parabolic SAR:
- Step: 0.02 (default) [02:30]
- Maximum step: 0.2 (default) [02:30]
- Dot placement: below price in uptrend, above price in downtrend [03:30]

Ichimoku Cloud:
- Kumo Cloud (Ichimoku cloud itself) - used as dynamic support/resistance [06:30]
- No specific parameters stated; speaker references existing Ichimoku knowledge [06:00]

### Context / bias filter
1. Price must be clearly positioned relative to Ichimoku Cloud: [07:00]
   - For long: "price to be above the Kumo Cloud"
   - For short: "price must be below the Kumo Cloud"
2. Never trade inside the cloud [06:30]
3. Clear trend must be visible to avoid choppy markets [04:30]

### Entry trigger
Two conditions must align:
1. Parabolic SAR dot forms on the correct side of price [07:00]
2. Price is positioned correctly relative to Kumo Cloud [07:00]

Long entry: "We want the price to be above the Kumo Cloud and a dot must be formed below the price." [07:00]
Short entry: "For short opportunities, the price must be below the Kumo Cloud and the dot must be above the price." [07:00]

Additional filter: "pay attention to price action for obvious patterns, static support and resistance levels, and breakouts" [06:30]

### Stop loss
Parabolic SAR acts as a trailing stop: "The rule is that you should close buy positions when the price moves below the SAR and close sell positions when the price moves above the SAR." [04:30]
Alternative: Stop placement follows SAR distance from price [04:30]

### Take profit / exit
Price closes above/below SAR depending on direction [04:30]
Exit when SAR flips to the other side of price [03:30]
Speaker emphasizes: "from my experience, it's better to use the Parabolic SAR to help you determine whether you should close your trade or not" [05:00]

### Invalidation / skip conditions
1. Do not trade when price is inside the Kumo Cloud [06:30]
2. Do not trade in choppy or sideways markets [04:30]
3. Avoid lower timeframes where signals are noisy [05:30]
4. Avoid range-bound or low-volatility markets [10:30]

### Claimed performance
Examples show mixed results: "during this period, we recorded five or six profitable trades and two losing ones" [08:00] on EUR/JPY
Tesla example shows several winning trades with some losers [09:00-09:30]
General caution: "don't expect 10 winning trades out of 10" [08:00]
"Keep in mind that these signals occurred in the past 6 months because we trade the higher time frames" [10:00]

## Notable claims and caveats

- Parabolic SAR is described as "mainly used to identify the market direction and the beginning of reversals" [01:00]
- "Best used in markets that are trending and that have long rallies and small corrections" [04:00]
- "Don't want to use this indicator in a choppy market where the price movement is sideways" [04:30]
- "Better to use it as an exit indicator" rather than entry [04:30]
- "Almost impossible to stick consistent trading just on the basis of the Parabolic SAR" [06:00]
- Parabolic SAR is a lagging indicator: "follows the price" [03:30]
- During strong trends, gap between price and SAR dots widens [01:30]
- In flat/choppy markets, dots and price interact frequently [01:30]
- Default SAR settings (0.02 step, 0.2 max step) provided, but adjustment guidance: "By increasing the values... increase sensitivity but sacrifice precision. By lowering... become less sensitive but more accurate" [02:30-03:00]
- Lower timeframes generate excessive false signals: "everything is faster with a lot of noise" [05:30]
- Higher timeframes (4H+) recommended to avoid emotion and false signals [05:30]
- Strategy requires patience: speaker notes "signals occurred in the past 6 months" on higher timeframes [10:00]
- "Don't be discouraged if you get one or two losing trades in a row" [10:00]
- Avoid "ranging or with low volatility" markets [10:30]
