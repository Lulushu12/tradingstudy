## Summary

This video covers the TRIX indicator (Triple Exponential Average), a momentum oscillator that uses triple exponential smoothing to filter market noise and identify trends and reversals. TRIX is calculated by applying three successive 15-period EMAs to closing prices, then measuring the percentage change. Two main trading approaches are presented: (1) entering when TRIX crosses the 0.0 baseline with price action confirmation (trend line breaks, swing patterns), and (2) using divergences between price and TRIX to anticipate reversals. Stop losses must be placed manually at recent swings; exit signals should not rely on TRIX crossing back through zero. The speaker emphasizes that TRIX works best in the direction of the primary trend and should be combined with price action analysis.

## Strategy 1: TRIX Zero-Line Crossover with Price Action Confirmation

### Indicators and settings
- TRIX: period: 15 (default) [01:30]; can be adjusted to 40 (long-term) or 10 (short-term) [02:00]
- TRIX calculation: Three successive 15-period EMAs of closing prices [01:30-02:00], then 1-period percentage change [01:30]
- Price action filters: trend lines, support/resistance levels, swing patterns
- Optional: moving average applied to TRIX for crossovers (speaker not recommended) [03:30]

### Context / bias filter
Trend confirmation: [04:00-04:30] "A popular TRIX strategy is to watch for price reversal signals, such a trend line or support or resistance breakout, which is confirmed by a TRIX zero line crossover."

[07:00-07:30] Momentum direction: "Upward momentum may be less positive, but it is still positive as long as it stays above 0 baseline."

[10:00-10:30] Recommended: Only take buy signals in overall uptrends, sell signals in overall downtrends.

### Entry trigger
Long entry [04:00-04:30]:
"For long trades, you buy when the TRIX crosses above 0.0 if there is some evidence that the price has started to reverse higher (you need to search for trend line breakout, higher swing high or higher swing low)."

Entry requires TWO conditions:
1. TRIX crosses above 0.0
2. Price action confirmation: "trend line breakout, higher swing high or higher swing low"

Short entry [04:30]:
"For short trades, you go short when the TRIX crosses below 0.0 if there is some evidence that the price has started to reverse lower (so you need confirmation, namely a trend line or support or resistance breakout, lower swing high or lower swing low)."

Entry requires TWO conditions:
1. TRIX crosses below 0.0
2. Price action confirmation: "trend line or support or resistance breakout, lower swing high or lower swing low"

### Stop loss
[05:00] "To control risk, a stop loss must be implemented, preferably just above a recent high if taking a short position, or just below a recent low if taking a long position."

Manual placement: Above recent high (for shorts), below recent low (for longs).

[10:30] "A stop loss must be placed manually above a recent high (shorts) or below a recent low (longs). If there is no respective high or low near the entry point, maybe you should consider avoiding the trade."

### Take profit / exit
[05:00-05:30] "Waiting for the price to move back across the zero line to exit the trade can mean giving back a lot of profit. For this reason, you shouldn't rely in TRIX indicator to offer an exit."

Exit at logical support/resistance levels, not based on TRIX signal.

### Invalidation / skip conditions
[10:00-10:30] Only trade in direction of primary trend: "Ideally, only take buy signals when an overall uptrend is underway, and sell signals when an overall downtrend is underway."

[09:30-10:00] False crossovers are common: "A false crossover is when the TRIX crosses above or below the zero line, only to snap back the other way, resulting in a losing trade."

Skip if no clear support/resistance or swing levels near entry [10:30]: "If there is no respective high or low near the entry point, maybe you should consider avoiding the trade."

## Strategy 2: TRIX Divergence Trading

### Indicators and settings
- TRIX: period: 15 (default) [01:30]; adjustable [02:00]
- Price action: identify highs and lows for divergence analysis

### Context / bias filter
Divergence rules [02:30-03:00]:
- Bullish divergence: "price makes a lower low but the TRIX doesn't. It indicates selling momentum is not as great as it was on prior price drop. Weak selling momentum could lead to an eventual reversal."
- Bearish divergence: "price makes a higher high, but the TRIX doesn't. It signals weakening buying momentum, which could result in an eventual reversal."

Limitations [07:00-07:30]:
- "Bearish divergences do not work well in strong uptrends. Even though momentum seems to be weakening because the indicator is producing lower highs, momentum still has a bullish bias as long as the indicator is above its centerline."
- "The opposite is true for bullish divergences. These do not work well in strong downtrends."

### Entry trigger
[06:00-07:00] Bullish divergence entry: Price makes lower low, TRIX makes higher low (or stays above centerline). "This ultimately leads to a massive run higher, where the indicator makes fresh new intraday highs on each push higher."

Bearish divergence entry: Price makes higher high, TRIX makes lower high. "the second high had a lower reading on the indicator, which is a precursor for a likely pullback."

Entry timing not mechanically specified; implied on divergence confirmation + price action.

### Stop loss
[05:00] "a stop loss must be implemented, preferably just above a recent high if taking a short position, or just below a recent low if taking a long position."

Manual placement above/below recent swings.
