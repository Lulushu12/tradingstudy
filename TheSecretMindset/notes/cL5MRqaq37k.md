## Summary

This video teaches order block trading methodology, focusing on how to identify high-probability order blocks that combine liquidity runs with breaks in market structure. The key differentiator is prioritizing recently created order blocks over old ones, and using price action confirmation signals (engulfing candles or pin bars) on lower timeframes to enter trades. The video emphasizes trading order blocks that form in the direction of the most recent market move, rather than predicting reversals against the trend.

## Strategy 1: Order Block Trading with Price Action Confirmation

### Indicators and settings

- Liquidity run: visual identification of stops being taken and new trades triggered [07:30]
- Break of structure (BOS): break of most recent swing high or low [13:00]
- Change of character: market breaks previous swing high or low, forming new higher high (bullish) or lower low (bearish) [08:00-09:00]
- Order block age: prefer recently created zones; older zones have lower success rate [05:00-05:30]
- Price action entries: engulfing candles or pin bars on lower timeframe [22:00]

### Context / bias filter

Entry is valid only when the order block is preceded by [07:00-10:30]:
1. A liquidity run that "takes stops" and triggers new positions in the direction of the intended move [07:30]
2. A subsequent change of character, defined as breaking the most recent swing high (for bullish) or low (for bearish) [08:00]
3. The order block must have been created recently, not "a long time ago" [05:00-05:30]
4. The order block must form in the direction of the current trend or most recent impulse move [16:00-17:00]; trading against the most recent high/low puts you at unnecessary risk [16:30]

### Entry trigger

**Option A: Engulfing Candle on Lower Timeframe [22:00-23:00]**

After identifying the order block on the higher timeframe, switch to a lower timeframe (minimum 5-minute, not lower than 1-minute to avoid false signals [22:30]). Enter when:
1. A small bullish candle appears in the zone [24:30]
2. Followed immediately by a large bearish engulfing candle that completely covers the small candle [24:30-25:00]
3. The bearish candle must be significantly larger to create pressure on buyers to close positions [25:00-25:30]
4. Enter a sell trade using market order when the engulfing candle closes [not explicitly stated but implied from context]

**Option B: Pin Bar on Lower Timeframe [26:00-27:00]**

After identifying the order block on the higher timeframe, switch to a lower timeframe to find a pin bar. Enter when:
1. Price returns to the order block zone
2. A pin bar forms (long wick showing rejection, short body) [26:00]
3. Wait for the pin bar to close [26:30]
4. Place the trade using a market order once the pin bar closes [26:30]

### Invalidation / skip conditions

1. If an old order block (created "a long time ago") is used instead of recently created zones [05:30]
2. If the order block is traded against the most recent swing high/low or current trend momentum [16:00-17:00]
3. If the move away from the order block lacked significant strength (though the speaker challenges this conventional wisdom) [06:00-06:30]
4. If using pending orders to enter at the order block itself rather than waiting for price action confirmation [21:00]
