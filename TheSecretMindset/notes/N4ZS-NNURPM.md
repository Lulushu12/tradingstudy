## Summary
The "True Zone System" for trading support and resistance levels using three filters: zone quality (sharp reversals, multiple touches, visual obviousness, recency), liquidity awareness (wait for stop hunts to be completed before entering), and volume confirmation (verify real participation). The core strategy waits for price to sweep beyond a support/resistance level (liquidity grab), then enters on the reversal candle that follows the sweep with a volume spike. This exploits the pattern of institutional traders hunting retail stop losses before moving price in the intended direction.

## Strategy 1: Support/Resistance Sweep + Volume Confirmation Entry

### Indicators and settings
- Volume Profile: used to identify point of control (thickest volume area) [12:00 - 12:30]

### Context / bias filter

**Filter 1: Zone Quality** [02:00 - 04:30]

A level is worth trading if it meets all four criteria:

1. **Sharp reversals**: "When price got to this zone, what happened? It didn't chop around. It rejected hard. Fast, impulsive move away from the level" [03:00]. Contrast to "weak candles and no conviction" which signals noise [03:00].

2. **Multiple touches**: "I want to see price respect the zone more than once. Touch one, bounce. Touch two, another bounce. Now I'm interested. A level with one touch is just a swing point. A level with multiple touches is a zone the market remembers" [03:30].

3. **Visual obviousness**: "If you have to squint to see it, the market doesn't see it either. Strong zones jump off the chart. They're obvious" [03:30 - 04:00]. "If it's obvious to you, it's obvious to thousands of other traders. That consensus is what gives the level power" [04:00].

4. **Recency**: "A level from three years ago doesn't carry the same weight as one from last week. Recent levels are fresh in traders' minds. Recent orders are still sitting there. I weigh new zones more heavily than old ones" [04:00 - 04:30].

Example of strong zone: "Sharp reversal in the past. Multiple touches. Obvious. Recent. Price approaches and bounces hard" [04:30]. Example of weak zone: "Single touch. Old. Weak reversal. Price approaches and cuts through like it doesn't exist" [04:30].

**Filter 2: Liquidity Awareness** [06:00 - 08:30]

Entry only occurs AFTER a liquidity sweep (stop hunt) is complete, not at the initial level touch. "The strongest levels are exactly where you'll get stopped out the most" [06:00]. 

Classic stop placement: "Where does everyone put their stop when they buy support? Below the level. Just under the zone. It's textbook. It's logical. And it's exactly why you keep getting stopped out" [06:00 - 06:30].

Stop hunt mechanism: "Price spikes through support, right into all those stops, and immediately reverses. That wasn't the market breaking down. That was a stop hunt. Price needed the liquidity sitting below support to fill large orders. It grabbed those stops and reversed" [06:30 - 07:00].

High-probability sweep locations [08:30 - 09:00]:
- Previous day's high and low
- Round numbers (ending in double zero or fifty)
- Obvious swing points (higher highs, double bottoms)

Entry condition: "Instead of buying support and putting your stop where everyone else's is, you wait. Let price sweep the level first. Let it grab those stops. Then, and only then, you look for your entry" [07:30 - 08:00].

### Entry trigger

1. Price approaches the support/resistance zone [12:30 - 13:00]
2. Zone quality filter passes (sharp reversals, multiple touches, obvious, recent) [12:30 - 13:00]
3. Price sweeps beyond the level (below support for long, above resistance for short) [13:00]
4. Liquidity sweep is confirmed (stop hunt completed) [13:00]
5. "A reversal candle forms" [13:00]
6. Volume spike confirmation on reversal candle [13:00]
7. Enter after the reversal candle closes [08:00]

Specific example: "Price breaks below and sweeps the stops. And look at this candle. Immediate reversal. Strong rejection wick. That sweep just became my entry signal. I enter here, right after the sweep confirms" [08:00].

### Stop loss

"My stop goes below the wick, under the liquidity grab. Tight stop. Clear invalidation" [08:00 - 08:30].

Stop is placed below the wick of the reversal candle that forms after the sweep [08:00 - 08:30].

### Invalidation / skip conditions

**Skip if zone quality filters fail**:
- Single touch only (not multiple touches) [03:30]
- Weak reversal (chop, no conviction) [03:00]
- Old level (from years ago) [04:00 - 04:30]
- Not visually obvious [03:30 - 04:00]

**Skip if no volume spike on reversal candle**:
- "A bounce without volume is a suggestion. A bounce with volume is a statement" [09:30 - 10:00]
- "Price enters the zone. Then a reversal candle forms. Now I check: did volume spike? If yes, buyers showed up. The level is defended. If no, it's a weak reaction. Price might be pausing before breaking through" [10:30 - 11:00]

**Skip low-volume breaks**:
- "When you see a low-volume break, that's most likely a trap" [11:30 - 12:00]
- "Check the volume. Dead. Low participation. Nobody is committing to this break" [11:30]
