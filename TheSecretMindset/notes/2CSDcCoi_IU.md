## Summary
This video teaches how to trade MACD divergences reliably by filtering false signals and improving accuracy. The speaker explains why basic MACD divergence signals fail (false signals after sharp price moves, divergences between highs/lows that don't predict reversals), and presents a refined 3-step approach combining price action analysis, double divergence confirmation, and trend line breakouts. The strategy emphasizes that price action must lead decision-making, with MACD serving as confirmation.

## Strategy 1: Double MACD Divergence with Trend Line Breakout

### Indicators and settings
- MACD: Period NOT STATED (presumably default 12, 26, 9)

### Context / bias filter
Price must be in an established trend (either uptrend or downtrend) to be vulnerable to reversal. "For a downtrend to reverse, the price must make a higher swing high and/or a higher swing low. To reverse an uptrend, the price must make a lower swing high and/or a lower swing low." [07:00]

### Entry trigger
Three sequential conditions required:

1. Price action signal (primary): 
   - For downtrend reversal (bullish): price makes higher swing high and/or higher swing low
   - For uptrend reversal (bearish): price makes lower swing high and/or lower swing low [07:00]

2. Double divergence on MACD: "I look for double divergence patterns, meaning i want to see a divergence on both the histogram and on the signal and MACD line." [07:30]
   - Divergence definition: Price moving in one direction while MACD moves in the opposite direction [00:30]
   - Alternative: Price reaches new high/low but MACD doesn't [01:00]

3. Trend line breakout: "With the creation of a double divergence on the charts, i prefer to draw a trend line on the charts and enter after a breakout." [07:30]

Volume confirmation: "When the second divergence is underway... volumes will decrease." [08:00]

### Stop loss
"Trend line marking the uptrend was broken" or equivalent for downtrend [08:30]
"My stop loss is optimized below or above the breakout" [07:30]
"Your stops should be in place at all times to act as safety net against any potential misreading or miscalculation of risks" [08:00]

2. Sharp price moves cause MACD divergences that don't predict reversals [02:30]
3. "A trend might continue over an extended period even after a double divergence" [08:00]

## Notable claims and caveats

- "MACD divergence is my favorite and the most reliable leading signals I could get" [00:00]
- "This type of signal is supposed to warn of a price-direction reversal, but the signal can be misleading and inaccurate" [01:00]
- MACD divergence is "unreliable trading signal" by itself [01:30]
- Main problem 1: "MACD divergence after a sharp move" [02:00]
  - "Divergence pattern between the two MACD lines will almost always occur right after a sharp price move" [02:30]
  - "Most big moves aren't immediately reversed right after, if you assume that divergence... means a reversal is coming, you could get yourself into a lot of losing trades" [03:30]
- Main problem 2: "Problems with divergence between MACD highs (or lows)" [04:00]
  - "A lower MACD high-price level shows the price didn't have the same velocity... but that doesn't necessarily indicate a reversal" [04:30]
  - "Price can move higher or lower, slowly, for very long periods of time" after a sharper move [05:00]
- "MACD divergence often isn't present when an actual price reversal occurs" [06:30]
  - "Indicator which provides many false signals (divergence occurs, but price doesn't reverse), but also fails to provide signals on many actual price reversals" [06:30]
- "Must focus on the price action first, instead of divergence" [07:00]
- "Traders make money off price movements, not MACD movements" [07:00]
- Volume decrease during double divergence represents "momentum and the volume seem to arrive at common grounds" [08:00]
- "Price action needs to be followed after you're in a trade" [10:00]
- "Never lose sight of the primary levels and how they need to be capitalized in conjunction with the new trend" [10:00]
- Strategy can be used for both "day trading" and "swing trading" [10:30]
