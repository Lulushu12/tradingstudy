## Summary
The TTM Squeeze indicator helps traders identify when markets transition from consolidation (squeeze) to breakout (expansion), and then determines the direction of the breakout using momentum. The indicator visualizes the relationship between Bollinger Bands (inside the Keltner Channel indicates squeeze/consolidation). Trades are entered on the first green dot (expansion signal) after red dots (squeeze), with direction determined by the momentum histogram. The strategy includes an alternative divergence approach and requires multi-timeframe confirmation and alignment with the main trend.

## TTM Squeeze Indicator Components

**Main Components:**
- Bollinger Bands (inner volatility indicator)
- Keltner Channels (outer volatility indicator)
- Momentum histogram (vertical bars, color-coded)
- Squeeze dots on horizontal zero line (red/green)

**Squeeze Condition:** Bollinger Bands completely enclosed within Keltner Channel [01:30] = very low volatility

**Expansion/Breakout:** Bollinger Bands expand outside Keltner Channel [01:30] = volatility increasing, prices likely to break out

## Strategy 1: Momentum Breakout from Squeeze

### Indicators and settings
- TTM Squeeze indicator with standard settings [02:00]
- Momentum histogram: green bars = positive/bullish momentum above zero line [02:00]; red bars = negative/bearish momentum below zero line [02:00]
- Squeeze dots: red = squeeze/consolidation [02:30]; green = expansion/breakout ready [02:30]
- Color intensity: lighter = increasing momentum [04:30]; darker = decreasing momentum [04:30]
- Main trend: identified via price action, moving average, Ichimoku Cloud, or Heikin-Ashi [10:30]

### Context / bias filter
Market must be consolidating (red dots on squeeze line) before expansion signal [04:00]. Look for squeeze in direction of main trend [07:00]. Multi-timeframe confirmation strengthens signal [10:30].

**Trend alignment:** "My preference is to look for squeeze trades in the direction of the main trend" [07:00]. If daily and hourly squeeze firing simultaneously, stronger signal than single timeframe [10:30].

### Entry trigger
**Entry Point:** Trade on first green dot after one or more red dots [04:00]

**Direction Determination:**
1. Look at momentum histogram position and direction [04:30]
2. If histogram above zero line and rising: long trade [04:30]
3. If histogram below zero line and falling lower: short trade [04:30]
4. Consider previous trend direction as additional bias [07:00]

**Timing:** Do not expect immediate move after color change [07:30]. Market may take time to start new move [07:30]. Entry can be taken on "first bar, others after one or two bars have been confirmed" [06:00].

**Entry with momentum shift:** "As the momentum bars near the zero line, this is the signal or condition that we should be looking for which indicates that momentum is shifting into positive or negative territory. Once these conditions are met, an entry can be considered" [05:30].

### Stop loss
NOT STATED. Speaker mentions "your risk and money management is on point" [08:00] but doesn't specify stop placement.

### Take profit / exit
**Exit signal 1:** "When the histogram changes direction and starts moving back towards the zero line, that's a signal to sell" [05:00]

**Exit signal 2:** Sell when you see two bars in a row in the new color with decreasing height [05:00]. Example: "if squeeze fires and bars above zero and increasing, sell when you see two bars in a row above zero and decreasing" [05:00]

### Invalidation / skip conditions
1. Squeeze is occurring (red dots) - not ready for trade yet [04:00]
2. Histogram momentum doesn't match expected direction [04:30]
3. Single timeframe signal without multi-timeframe confirmation [10:30]
4. Against main trend direction [07:00]
5. Market may reverse even with clear squeeze signal; occurred in one example where histogram indicated bearish but market moved bullish [06:30]

### Claimed performance

However, notes: "The second squeeze direction is not so clear...ended being a bullish squeeze" despite histogram suggesting bearish [06:30], emphasizing importance of stops.

## Strategy 2: Divergence Trading with TTM

### Indicators and settings
- TTM Squeeze momentum histogram [09:30]
- Price action on upper chart [10:00]

### Context / bias filter
Divergence occurs when histogram momentum bars slope differently from price movement [09:30].

### Entry trigger
**Bearish Divergence - Short entry:**
- Price making higher highs [10:00]
- TTM histogram making lower highs on positive side of zero line [10:00]
- Indicates momentum weakening even as price rises [10:00]

**Bullish Divergence - Long entry:**
- Price making lower lows [10:00]

## General Strategy Guidance

**Multi-timeframe approach:** Speaker recommends looking at same market across multiple timeframes [10:30]. "If squeeze is firing on both daily and hourly chart at the same time, that's a stronger signal than squeeze that is only firing in one time frame" [10:30].

**Integration with other tools:** "TTM is no magic bullet and it would be best used with other indicators as confirmation" [10:30]. Suggestions:
- Moving averages [10:30]
- Ichimoku Cloud [10:30]
- Heikin-Ashi chart for trend visualization [10:30]

**Core process summary [12:30]:**
1. Find areas of consolidation with red dots
2. Look for momentum to break out in direction of main trend
3. Confirm the movement with higher timeframe
