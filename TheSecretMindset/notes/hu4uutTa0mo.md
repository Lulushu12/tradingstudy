## Summary
The video teaches how to identify and trade the Gartley harmonic pattern, a five-point price formation (marked X, A, B, C, D) that resembles an M or W shape. The pattern is defined by precise Fibonacci retracement levels at each swing, and the speaker claims higher success rates than classic patterns like triangles and wedges. Trades are entered at point D (the Potential Reversal Zone) and scaled out at predetermined Fibonacci targets.

## Strategy 1: Gartley Harmonic Pattern Trading

### Indicators and settings
- Pattern: Gartley (M/W shape with five points: X, A, B, C, D) [00:30]
- Fibonacci ratios applied:
  - XA swing: no specific requirements [01:30]
  - AB swing: approximately 61.8% of XA [01:30]; must not retrace past point X [01:30]
  - BC swing: either 38.2% OR 88.6% of AB [02:00]; must not retrace above point A [02:00]
  - CD swing: if BC = 38.2% of AB, then CD = 127.2% extension of BC; if BC = 88.6% of AB, then CD = 161.8% extension of BC [02:00]
  - AD swing: 78.6% retracement of XA [02:30]
- Ideal Gartley:
  - Point D completes at 0.786 of XA, should not exceed point X [03:00]
  - AB should reverse very close to 0.618 retracement of XA [03:30]

### Context / bias filter
The pattern can form as either a retracement or continuation pattern depending on location in trend [00:30]. Pattern forms when trend temporarily changes direction before continuing in original direction [00:30]. Most powerful when formed after a strong price move or strong rally [08:00], [11:00].

### Entry trigger
**Bullish Gartley:** Enter long if CD leg finds support at 127.2% or 161.8% Fibonacci level of BC move [06:00]. Price action bounces upwards from this zone [09:30].

**Bearish Gartley:** Enter short if CD leg finds resistance at 127.2% or 161.8% Fibonacci extension of BC move [05:00], [11:30], when price creates bearish bounce from 161.8% extension of BC [11:30].

### Stop loss
**Bullish:** Placed below point X [06:00], [09:30]
**Bearish:** Placed above point X [06:30], [11:30]

### Take profit / exit
Scale out at four minimum targets [06:30]:

4. Target 4: 161.8% Fibonacci extension of AD [06:30]

Alternative exits: Use trailing stop or additional price action rules if price momentum shows strength; continue holding portion of trade [07:00].
