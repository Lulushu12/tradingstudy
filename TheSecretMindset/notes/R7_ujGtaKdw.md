## Strategy 1: Institutional Candle + Inside Bars Breakout

### Indicators and settings

- Institutional candle: a larger price bar that sets the initial range (size NOT STATED quantitatively)
- Inside bars: 8 or more small candles forming within the high and low of the institutional candle; "ideally 10" [00:30]
- No external indicators used

### Context / bias filter

The pattern should form at a clear support or resistance level (or demand/supply zone) [01:30]. Trade must be in line with the overall market trend (uptrend for buys, downtrend for shorts) [02:00]. The context is viewed as a contraction-expansion cycle: big players pause to build positions (contraction), then expand [03:00].

### Entry trigger

For buy: Price breaks above the top of the channel (institutional bar's high) [01:00]. Ideally, this happens after a strong upward move followed by contraction at a demand level [02:30]. 

For sell: Price breaks below the bottom of the channel [01:00]. Should follow a downtrend and occur at a supply level [05:30].

You can enter on the breakout close itself or wait for a slight pullback after the breakout [05:00].

### Stop loss

For buy: Stop loss goes below the low of the contraction phase (below the institutional bar's low) [05:00].

For sell: Stop loss goes above the high of the contraction phase [05:00-06:00].

### Take profit / exit

Target is based on the size of the move before the contraction: "Breakouts often move about the same distance as the prior trend wave" [05:15]. Use minor levels where price has paused or reversed before as exit levels [02:00].

### Invalidation / skip conditions

If price breaks below the stop loss level, "it might mean the move has failed" [05:00]. Consolidation that is too short (fewer than 8 inside bars) may not be valid.

## Strategy 2: False Breakout / Liquidity Sweep Setup

### Indicators and settings

- Pin bar: a candle with a wick outside the channel and a small body inside the channel [10:30]
- The wick must be at least 50% of the bar's total range for a "real pin bar" [20:00]
- Inside bar: following candle with high and low completely within the pin bar's range

### Context / bias filter

This is identified as a reversal of a failed breakout. The market makes a quick move outside the channel (against the prevailing trend or falsely), pulling stop losses and orders, then retraces back [10:30]. The pattern signals that "big players use false breakouts to trap traders and create liquidity" [10:30].

### Entry trigger

When price retraces back into the channel after the pin bar (the liquidity sweep), enter in the opposite direction of the false breakout [12:30]. For an upward false breakout, sell when price retraces back inside. For a downward false breakout, buy when price retraces back inside [11:00-12:30].

Entry is on the next bar that "closes firmly inside the trading range" after the sweep [15:30].

### Stop loss

Stop loss goes just above the wick of the sweep candle (for short) or just below it (for long) [18:00].

### Take profit / exit

NOT STATED explicitly; referenced generally as "the next move" or confluence with next level.

### Invalidation / skip conditions

If price continues in the direction of the false breakout (does not retrace back), then it was not a false breakout but a real move, and the setup is invalid.

## Strategy 3: Pin Bar + Inside Bar Reversal Setup

### Indicators and settings

- Pin bar: a candle with a long wick (tail) and a small body, rejecting a level [19:30]
- Wick must be at least 50% of the bar's total range [20:00]
- Inside bar: next candle's high and low completely within the pin bar's range [20:30]
- Location at key support or resistance, or "unmitigated demand/supply" [22:00]

### Context / bias filter

For bullish setup: Pin bar forms at demand zone (support) showing "strong buying pressure" [21:00]. For bearish setup: Pin bar forms at supply zone (resistance) with "strong selling pressure" [21:30].

The inside bar represents a pause where "buyers and sellers are sizing each other up" [21:00]. Trade should align with the overall trend direction (uptrend for longs, downtrend for shorts) [23:00].

### Entry trigger

For buy: Enter a buy order just above the high of the pin bar when price breaks above [22:30]. 

For sell: Enter a sell order just below the low of the pin bar when price breaks below [22:30].

Breakout should show "momentum move up, right away after the breakout" [24:30].

### Stop loss

For buy: Stop loss just below the low of the pin bar [22:30].

For sell: Stop loss just above the high of the pin bar [22:30].

### Take profit / exit

Target at "the next resistance level" for buys or "the next support level" for sells [22:30]. For examples, target is placed at the next unmitigated supply or demand zone [23:00].

### Invalidation / skip conditions

If "it hangs around near the breakout point" without momentum, "the setup has failed" [24:30]. Setup must be at a location where market has turned before or at unmitigated levels [22:00].
