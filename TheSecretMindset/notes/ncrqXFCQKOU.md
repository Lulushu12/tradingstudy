## Summary

Comprehensive teaching of Volume Spread Analysis (VSA) based on Wyckoff methodology. Explains that VSA tracks smart money activity through the relationship between price spread (OHLC range) and transaction volume, identifying imbalances that reveal when demand or supply is exhausted. Teaches Wyckoff's four market phases (Accumulation, Mark Up, Distribution, Mark Down) and 18 distinct VSA candle patterns: 9 Signs of Strength (bullish) and 9 Signs of Weakness (bearish), each designed to anticipate the next candle's directional move.

## Mechanizability

PARTIAL. The core framework (spread = OHLC, volume measurement, comparative volume analysis across candles) is mechanically computable from OHLCV data. Pattern recognition thresholds for "pin bar," "doji," "low spread," "high volume" can be defined but are not specified in the video. All 18 patterns lack explicit stop loss and take profit rules, making them incomplete as tradeable strategies. A trader can visually recognize these patterns and use VSA logic to anticipate direction, but coding a mechanical backtest requires inventing all quantitative thresholds (e.g., "pin bar = wick > 1.5x body," "low spread = body < 25% of average body," etc.). The patterns show where smart money has acted, but entry timing, position sizing, and risk management rules are not stated.
