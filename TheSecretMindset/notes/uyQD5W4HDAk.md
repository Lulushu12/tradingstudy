## Summary

This video presents a 1-minute scalping strategy based on identifying and fading ultra-high volume bars (stopping volume in Volume Spread Analysis terminology). The strategy identifies when price has been declining and suddenly experiences an ultra-high volume bar, which often signals institutional money stepping in to arrest the downmove. A channel is drawn from the high and low of this ultra-high volume candle, and the trade is taken when price breaks above the channel (fade the downmove). Stop loss is placed below the ultra-high volume candle, and targets are set for a 2:1+ risk-reward ratio. The strategy emphasizes filter conditions: narrow spread on the ultra-high volume candle, location at support/resistance/Fibonacci levels, and trading during active market hours.

## Strategy 1: Ultra-High Volume Bar Scalping (Fade Entry)

### Indicators and settings

- **Volume Spread for VSA Indicator:** Color-coded volume intensity
  - Red: Ultra high volume [02:30-03:00]
  - Yellow: High volume [02:30]
  - Green: Average volume [02:30]
  - Blue: Low volume [02:30]
- No other technical indicators required
- **Channel:** Drawn from high and low of the ultra-high volume candle [03:30]

### Context / bias filter

**Primary Setup [03:00-03:30]:**
"The market is decreasing. Then, it experiences a significant surge in volume. This is when an ultra-high volume bar appears" [03:00-03:30].

**Scenario A: Best Case - Stopping Volume (Reversal) [04:00-04:30]:**
"In the best-case scenario, it spots the end of a short term bear trend. In this case, the signal will potentially lead you into the start of a bull trend. And at the same time, a lot of trend traders will be trapped on the down side" [04:00-04:30].

**Scenario B: Trading Range [04:30]:**
"At times, instead of preceding a bullish reversal, it pushes the market into a trading range" [04:30-05:00].

**Scenario C: Continuation (Avoid) [04:30]:**
"Of course, there's also the possibility that the market will continue to decrease despite the signal, implying powerful bearish momentum" [04:30-05:00].

**Filter: Narrow Spread Ultra-High Volume [05:00-06:00]:**
"A narrow spread candle should have low volume. However, the ones that are of high or ultra high volume are special. This should instantly alert you" [05:00-05:30].

"If you have a down candle with a narrow spread and ultra high volume, then the market is showing some signs of strength...The sellers are starting to struggle at this price level" [05:00-05:30].

This reflects Wyckoff's effort vs. result principle: "High volume should result in a wide spread candle, not a narrow spread" [05:30].

### Entry trigger

**Two-Part Entry Condition [03:30-04:00, 05:00-06:00]:**

1. **Ultra-High Volume Bar Appears After Downmove [01:00-01:30]:**
   "Stopping volume is a well-known concept in volume spread analysis (VSA). It refers to a dramatic increase in volume that stops the market from falling further. This is your ultra high volume bar, which appears after a downward move" [01:00-01:30].

2. **Draw Channel from Candle Extremes [03:30]:**
   "Find the candlestick associated with the surge in volume. Then you simply draw a channel, from the candlestick's high and low price" [03:30].

3. **Price Breaks Above Channel (Long Entry) [03:30-04:00]:**
   "In theory, the ultra high volume should stop the price decrease. So, the plan is to fade the downward move, and buy if and when the price goes on the other side of the channel" [03:30-04:00].

**Confirmation Signal (to Filter Bad Trades) [04:30-05:00]:**
"To filter bad trades, it's better to draw the channel on the ultra high volume candlestick, and trade when there's some sort of activity to the upside" [04:30-05:00].

**Bonus Filter - Narrow Spread [05:00-05:30]:**
Highest probability when ultra-high volume appears on a narrow spread candle, indicating "signs of strength" and seller struggle.

**Location Filter [13:30-14:00]:**
"Location of the signal is very important. Look for volume spikes around support and resistance levels, supply/demand areas, Fibonacci levels or near VWAP or common moving averages. These are the high probability signals which will bring you profits" [13:30-14:00].

### Stop loss

"Stop loss goes below the ultra high volume candle" [03:30-04:00].

**Example Specifications [06:30-07:00, 07:30, 08:30, 10:00]:**
"Stop loss goes below support" [07:00], "Stop loss below this swing low, or below the low of the day" [12:00-12:30].

Multiple variations: typically a few pips below the ultra-high volume candle low, or below recent swing lows.

### Take profit / exit

**Minimum Target [03:30-04:00]:**
"The minimum target is a risk to reward ratio of 2. But the profit potential is much higher, as this might be the beginning of a new trend" [03:30-04:00].

**Examples Shown [06:30-12:30]:**
- "Target the next major swing in price structure. That's a risk to reward ratio of around 2.2" [07:00]
- "Targeting the previous swing low offers a decent risk to reward ratio, of around 2" [08:00]
- "Take profit at the next major swing. That's a risk to reward ratio of around 2.5" [08:30]
- "Take profit a few points above this support level. A risk to reward ratio of around 2" [10:00]
- "Take profit at the next swing low in the price structure. Again, a risk to reward of around 2" [11:00]
- "We target this swing high" [12:30]

**General Target Rule:**
Next major swing in price structure, with 2:1 minimum risk-reward (can be higher).

### Invalidation / skip conditions

1. **No Narrow Spread on Ultra-High Volume [05:30-06:00]:**
   If ultra-high volume appears on a wide-spread candle ("high effort, high result"), it suggests continuation rather than reversal and is avoided or treated with caution [10:00-10:30].

2. **Price Doesn't Move Above Channel [10:00-10:30]:**
   "If price breaks below the channel, we'll still risk a trade...Price indeed went higher, and consolidated above the channel. So this was a no trade in the end" [10:00-10:30].

3. **Low Activity Hours (Forex) [12:30-13:00]:**
   "To reduce it and to reduce false signals, I advise you to trade ultra high volume candles during active market hours. For example, if you're trading Forex, most pairs are traded the most between the time the London trading session begins and when the US sessions ends" [12:30-13:00].

4. **Low Activity Sessions [13:00]:**
   "Volume patterns you see form during low activity periods, during Tokyo session for example, have a lower probability of working out successfully" [13:00].

5. **First 30 Minutes of Market Open (Stocks) [13:00-13:30]:**
   "If you trade stocks, you'll usually see several ultra high volume candles, right at the New York open. I would advise you to avoid trading during the first 30 minutes, after the open, if you're not experienced" [13:00-13:30].

6. **High Noise, Many Ultra-High Volume Bars [11:30]:**
   "On the 1-min time frame, there will be lot of noise. And many ultra high volumes, which might confuse you" [11:30]. Alternative: "If you're using Tradingview, the 3-minute time frame is a decent alternative to filter signals" [11:30].

## Key Concepts

### Wyckoff's Effort vs. Result [02:00-06:00]

"One of the main principles involving volume is related to Wyckoff's law of effort versus result. This provides an early warning of a possible change in momentum. Divergences between volume and price often signal a change in price direction" [02:00-02:30].

Example: "When there are several high-volume but narrow-range price bars after a substantial rally, with the price failing to make a new high, this suggests that we might have a change in trend" [02:00-02:30].

Narrow-spread ultra-high volume bar signals struggle by sellers/buyers at that price level.

### Volume as Leading Indicator [01:30-02:00]

"One of the main benefits of volume is that it's a leading indicator, giving you early signals when the price movement is going to continue or reverse" [01:30-02:00].
