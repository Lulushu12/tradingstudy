This video teaches foundational price action concepts for reading market sentiment and identifying trends, rather than presenting a specific tradeable strategy. ## No strategy content

This video does not present a mechanically tradeable strategy with defined entry triggers, stop loss levels, or exit rules. Instead, it teaches foundational price action principles and indicators:

1. **Trend bars** [00:30-01:30]: Identifying candles with body taking up at least 50% of the entire bar range [01:00]
2. **Long shadows/wicks** [02:30-03:30]: Interpreting rejection areas and market pressure
3. **Consecutive bars closing in same direction** [04:00-04:30]: Sign of momentum
4. **Overlap with previous bar** [05:00-06:00]: Minimal overlap indicates urgency [05:30]
5. **Wicks below bullish bars and above bearish bars** [07:00]: Sign of directional control
6. **Reaction after test of support/resistance** [07:30-08:00]: Observing breakout behavior
7. **No retest after strong breakouts** [08:30-09:00]: Sign of strong momentum and urgency
8. **Top-down analysis** [09:00-10:00]: Using multiple timeframes
9. **Volume validation** [12:00-15:30]: Volume confirming or questioning price moves

All of these are teaching tools for reading price action, not a discrete strategy with computable triggers.
