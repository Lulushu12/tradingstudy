## Summary

This video teaches the "Double Liquidity Clear-out" trade: a pattern where price breaks a swing high (first trap), reverses and breaks a swing low (second trap), then continues in the direction of the initial breakout. The video frames this as smart money accumulating positions while trapping retail traders. It emphasizes trading in line with the dominant trend, identifying the accumulation, manipulation, and trend phases, and using pin bars or price action as entry signals after the second clear-out.
