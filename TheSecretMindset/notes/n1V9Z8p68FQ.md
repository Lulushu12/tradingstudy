## Summary
The video teaches a moving average-based strategy for trading false breakouts and liquidity runs. The speaker emphasizes that only 15-20% of moving average breakouts play out as textbook examples, and most are traps set by institutional traders. The strategy involves identifying strong trends, spotting when price briefly crosses a moving average on low volume, recognizing liquidity runs (stop-loss runs), and entering positions against the fake breakout in the direction of the original trend. The goal is to profit from the inevitable reversal after trapped traders' stops are hit.
