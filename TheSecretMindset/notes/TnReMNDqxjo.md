## Summary
This video teaches how to trade ascending triangle patterns, a bullish continuation pattern that signals potential breakouts. The video covers the formation of ascending triangles (flat resistance top with rising lower trend line), explains why false breakouts are common, and presents three entry approaches: breakout above 3+ resistance touches, pivot point confirmation entry, and early entry inside the triangle. Profit targets and stop loss placement rules are provided based on triangle geometry and candlestick positioning.

## Strategy 1: Ascending Triangle Breakout Entry (Classic Method)

### Indicators and settings
- No technical indicators; pure pattern recognition
- Triangle formation requires: flat resistance line + rising support line (higher lows) [01:30]

### Context / bias filter
1. Ascending triangle must form "in the middle of a bullish trend" for more weight [04:00]
2. Prior uptrend context is needed: "we need to have a previous uptrend to support the breakout" [02:00]
3. Triangle should NOT be forming inside a larger trading range [04:00]
4. Clear resistance level with "3 or more touches" at or near the same price [05:00]

### Entry trigger
"The classic method is to buy the breakout once you have had 3 or more touches with volume." [06:00]

Entry option 1 (Aggressive): "Buy on the highest high after three or more tops." [06:00]
Entry option 2 (Conservative): "Wait for a key pivot point resistance level to be breached and then place a buy order slightly above this level." [06:30]

Volume confirmation: "Once the triangle breakout happens you need to see a pick-up in volume that will result in a nice long trade." [05:00]
Also: "you need to see some acceleration to the upside" [05:30]

### Stop loss
Multiple options:
1. "Beneath the uptrend line" [09:30]
2. "Use the low of the breakout candle. If the price is able to break out, you can place your stop below the low of the candlestick." [09:30]
Speaker notes: Using breakout candle low "keeps more of your gains" than waiting for uptrend line breach [09:30]

### Take profit / exit
"Profit target can be estimated based on the height of the triangle added from the breakout price." [09:00]
Specifically: "distance of the triangle added to the top" [09:00]
"Typically a 1:1 measured move" [09:00]

Caveat: "That target is just an estimate. The price may far exceed that target, or fail to reach it." [10:00]

### Invalidation / skip conditions
1. False breakouts common: "The price may move out of the pattern only to move back into it, or the price may even proceed to break out the other side." [06:30]
2. Pattern may need redrawing: "A pattern may need to be redrawn several times as the price edges past the trend lines but fails to generate any momentum in the breakout direction." [06:30]
3. Triangle inside larger trading range: Less significant breakout likely [04:00]
4. Insufficient resistance line clarity: Need at least 3 touches, not just 1-2 peaks [05:00]

### Claimed performance
"Should be prepared to catch a bullish price move equal to at least the size of the triangle" [09:00]
