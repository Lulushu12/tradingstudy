## Summary
A smart money supply and demand trading strategy that identifies zones where institutional traders concentrate orders, then waits for price to return to these zones with price action confirmation. Demand zones are support areas created by sharp upward impulse moves; supply zones are resistance areas created by sharp downward moves. These zones gain additional confluence when they align with psychological levels (round numbers). Trades are entered conservatively using price action signals (pin bars, engulfing candles) rather than blindly placing limit orders, and are aligned with the main market trend for higher probability setups.

## Strategy 1: Supply and Demand Trading with Psychological Levels

### Indicators and settings
- Indicator 1: Supply and Demand Zones
  - Demand zone: support area where banks place large buy positions [00:30]; identified by sharp upward impulse move with big green candles (long bodies, little to no wicks) [02:00]
  - Supply zone: resistance area where banks place large sell positions [00:30]; identified by sharp downward decline with big red candles (long bodies, little to no wicks) [03:00]
  - Zone boundaries: from most recent opposite-direction candle high/low to the most recent swing low (demand) or swing high (supply) [02:30], [03:30]

- Indicator 2: Psychological Levels
  - Round numbers where traders commonly place orders [04:00]
  - Plotted using horizontal grid lines in TradingView [04:00]
  - Grid spacing depends on market: $1 grid for crude oil [05:00], $10 grid for stocks [09:30]
  - Best zones contain 2-3 psychological levels [05:30], [09:30]

### Context / bias filter
Best zones combine supply/demand area with psychological levels for "high probability area" [04:30]. 

**Zone quality factors:**
- Strength of price move away from zone [07:30]
- Distance price moved away before returning [07:30]
- First-time retrace to zone is strongest entry opportunity [08:00]
- Zone must be "fresh" - price has not revisited yet after initial creation [08:00]
- Each time price revisits a zone, previously unfilled orders are filled and level weakens [08:00]

**Trend alignment:** 
- In downtrend: trade supply zones (sell side) [13:00]
- In uptrend: trade demand zones (buy side) [13:00]

### Entry trigger
**Conservative approach (recommended):** Find zone on higher timeframe, wait for price to return to zone, then confirm with price action signal on lower timeframe [12:00].

**Bullish entry (Long at demand zone):**
1. Identify demand zone on higher timeframe from fast impulse move [02:00]
2. Zone includes psychological levels [05:30]
3. Trend is up [13:00]
4. Price retraces back to demand zone [05:00]
5. Wait for price action rejection signal on lower timeframe [06:00]:
   - Pin bar candlestick rejecting psychological level inside zone [06:00]
   - Or engulfing candle [06:00]
   - Or first reaction on round number showing strength pushing price away [10:00]
   - Or momentum loss with lower wicks inside zone [10:00]
6. Enter long on confirmation signal

**Bearish entry (Short at supply zone):**
1. Identify supply zone on higher timeframe from fast sharp decline [03:00]
2. Zone includes psychological levels [08:30]
3. Trend is down [13:00]
4. Price retraces back to supply zone [07:00]
5. Wait for price action rejection signal on lower timeframe [12:00]:
   - Pin bar candlestick rejecting psychological level [08:30], [13:30]
   - Evening star candlestick formation [08:30]
   - First reaction on round number showing strength [10:00]
6. Enter short on confirmation signal

**Aggressive approach (not recommended):** Set limit order at psychological level inside zone [05:30], [11:00]; but speaker explicitly warns this has "NO confirmation" and "you will blindly place the order...and hope that price reverses" [11:30].

### Stop loss
"Just beyond the extreme of the zone" [10:30]. 
- For longs: below the demand zone [06:30]
- For shorts: above the supply zone [08:30]

### Take profit / exit
Target the next major zone in the direction of trade:
- For longs: next major supply zone [06:30]
- For shorts: next major demand zone [08:30]
- Place take profit "a few cents below" (for longs) or "a few points below" (for shorts) the target zone [06:30], [14:00]

Expected risk-reward ratios: 4 to 6 [10:30], [14:00].

### Invalidation / skip conditions
1. Limit order entry without price action confirmation [11:30]
2. Zone that has been revisited multiple times (weakened) [08:00]
3. Trading against main trend [13:00]
4. Zone not aligned with psychological level [04:30]
5. Not a "fresh" zone - price has already revisited [08:00]
