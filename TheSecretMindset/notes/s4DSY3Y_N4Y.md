## Summary

This video presents a mechanical swing trading strategy designed for small accounts with limited time (e.g., 30 minutes per day). The strategy uses two exponential moving averages (20 and 50) to define tradable trends, enters on pullbacks into the zone between them using an outside bar trigger (a candle that dominates the prior candle's body and closes in the trend direction), and exits using a tiered system: 50% position at 1R profit, move stop to breakeven on remaining 50%, then trail the stop using recent swing structure. The strategy is designed for the daily timeframe only to reduce noise and transaction costs.

## Strategy 1: Mechanical Swing Trading (Daily Outside Bar)

### Indicators and settings

- **EMA 20:** Period 20, applied to daily close [01:30-02:00]
- **EMA 50:** Period 50, applied to daily close [01:30-02:00]

### Context / bias filter

#### Trend Filter - Three Checks in Order [02:00-03:30]

**Check 1: Alignment [02:00-02:30]**
"The 20 must sit above the 50 for longs, below for shorts" [02:00-02:30].

**Check 2: Slope [02:00-02:30]**
"Both moving averages must point in the same direction" [02:00-02:30]. "Both pointing up at a clean angle" for uptrend [02:30]. "Both pointing down" for downtrend [02:30]. "Flat" slope fails this check [03:00].

**Check 3: Price Position [02:00-02:30]**
"Price has to be on the right side of both. Above for longs, below for shorts" [02:00-02:30]. "Price stacked on top of both" [02:30] for uptrend. "Price below both" [02:30] for downtrend.

**Trend Test Failure [03:00-03:30]:**
"The lines are tangled together, crossing back and forth. No clear order. You close the chart when you see this" [03:00]. "The lines are aligned but flat. No slope. Price just drifting sideways. Again, close the chart" [03:00-03:30].

**Rule [03:30-04:00]:**
"Hard rule, if the EMAs are aligned and sloping clean, you have a tradable context. If lines are tangled, flat, or crossing, skip the chart and don't reopen it that day" [03:30-04:00].

#### Pullback Zone [04:00-05:30]

"In an uptrend, a pullback happens when early buyers take profit, short-term traders get scared, and price drifts back down" [04:00-04:30].

**Definition [04:30-05:00]:**
"The pullback zone is the area between the 20 and the 50. That is where price must pull back before we even care about an entry" [04:30-05:00].

"Why a zone? Because price rarely respects one perfect line. Sometimes it touches the 20 line and reacts. Sometimes it pushes deeper toward the 50...A zone matches how markets actually move" [04:30-05:00].

**Zone Invalidation [05:30-06:00]:**
"If price closes beyond the 50 EMA on a daily candle, the setup is dead. No entry, no second chance, no waiting for it to come back. Move to the next chart" [05:30-06:00].

"Binary rule, price inside the band with the trend intact. Setup is alive. Price closed beyond the 50. Setup is dead" [05:30-06:00].

### Entry trigger

#### Outside Bar Definition [06:00-07:30]

"The entry trigger is the outside bar. An outside bar is a candle whose full body covers the full body of the candle before it. It must dominate the body of the prior candle" [06:00-06:30].

**Valid Outside Bar Conditions [06:00-06:30]:**
1. Candle's full body covers the prior candle's full body
2. Must dominate the body (the body, not the wicks)
3. **For long entry:** "You want the outside bar to close bullish. That means it closes in the direction of the trend" [06:30]
4. **For short entry:** "You want it to close bearish again in the direction of the trend" [06:30]

**Visual Examples [06:30-07:30]:**
Uptrend example: "Price pulls into the zone. This candle breaks the high and low of the previous candle and closes green. Valid outside bar" [06:30-07:00].

Downtrend example: "Price pulls back into the zone. The candle breaks above and below the previous candle and closes red. Valid short trigger" [07:00].

**Invalid Outside Bar Locations [07:00-07:30]:**
"This outside bar forms far above the pullback zone. It is tempting, but you skip this. The candle is valid, but the location is wrong" [07:00-07:30].

**Not an Outside Bar (Common Mistake) [07:30]:**
"This candle looks strong, but it does not break both the previous high and the previous low. So it is not an outside bar. You skip this, too" [07:30].

**Entry Timing [07:30-08:00]:**
"Entry is always on the close of the outside bar. You wait for the daily candle to close. If the candle meets the rule, you enter. If it does not, you do nothing" [07:30-08:00].

### Stop loss

**Placement [08:00-08:30]:**
"The stop goes just beyond the opposite extreme of the outside bar. This candle defines your entry and it also defines your risk. If price breaks the opposite side of that candle, the trigger failed" [08:00-08:30].

**Example [09:30-10:00]:**
"The stop goes under the outside bar low" [09:30] (for a long trade).

For a short trade, stop would go above the outside bar high (implied mirror logic).

### Take profit / exit

#### Tiered Exit System [08:30-10:00]

**Tier 1 - The 1R Partial (50% Position) [08:30-09:00]:**
"Once price moves in your favor by the same distance as your risk, you take off 50% of the position. This is mechanical and the reason is simple. Small accounts need emotional protection. When you take 50% off at 1 R, you lock in a real win early" [08:30-09:00].

**Tier 2 - Break Even Stop [09:00-09:30]:**
"After 1 R is hit, move the stop on the remaining 50% to your entry price. Now the trade can no longer lose money. The worst case is that the second half comes back and stops at break even" [09:00-09:30].

**Tier 3 - Trailing Stop [09:00-09:30]:**
"Third tier is the trailing stop. We trail it using swing structure. For a long trade, trail the stop under the most recent swing low. For a short above the most recent swing high and only update it after confirmed daily candle closes, not during the candle. This keeps you in the move until structure actually breaks" [09:00-09:30].

**Update Timing [09:00-09:30]:**
"Only update it after confirmed daily candle closes, not during the candle" [09:00-09:30].

**Exit Philosophy [09:30-10:00]:**
"This exit is asymmetric on purpose. Tier one creates a high feel-good rate. Tier two removes downside. Tier three gives the trade a chance to become large" [09:30-10:00].

### Invalidation / skip conditions

1. **Failed Trend Filter:** If EMAs are tangled, flat, or crossing, skip the chart [03:00-03:30].
2. **Price Beyond 50 EMA:** If price closes beyond the 50 EMA on a daily candle, the setup is dead [05:30-06:00].
3. **Outside Bar Not in Zone:** If an outside bar forms outside the pullback zone, it is invalid [07:00-07:30].
4. **Not a True Outside Bar:** If a candle does not break both the prior candle's high and low, it is not an outside bar [07:30].
5. **Stops Triggered:** If the stop is hit (price breaks the opposite side of the outside bar), the trade is over [08:00-08:30].
