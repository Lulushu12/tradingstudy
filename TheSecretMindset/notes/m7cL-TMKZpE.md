## Mechanizability
DISCRETIONARY - While Anchored VWAP calculation is mechanical and support/resistance bounces are identifiable, entry decisions depend on subjective judgment about:
- When price is "overextended"
- Which anchor points are "important"
- When pullbacks constitute tradeable setups
- How far price must retract to VWAP before reversal
No trader could code this without inventing entry parameters.
