## Summary

This video presents a CCI-based trading strategy with three conditions that must be met before entry. The strategy involves waiting for CCI to reach extreme levels (-200) twice, then confirming a regular divergence between CCI and price, and finally confirming a trend line breakout. The speaker emphasizes conservative entry waiting and advocates for using the strategy on stocks with good fundamentals. The strategy is designed for long entries (buying oversold conditions).

## Strategy 1: CCI Triple Condition with Trend Line Breakout

### Indicators and settings

- Commodity Channel Index (CCI): period = 21 (standard) [00:30]; also 14 or 50 period are popular [00:30]
- Oversold threshold: -200 (not standard -100) [01:30]
- Overbought threshold: 200 (not standard 100) [01:30]
- Trend line: drawn on price action connecting significant lows or highs

### Context / bias filter

The strategy focuses on long entries only, requiring CCI to reach oversold (-200) conditions [01:30]. Do not trade the first oversold signal [01:30-02:00]; wait for the CCI to reach -200 level a second time before considering entry [01:30-02:00]. For maximum edge, use stocks with strong fundamentals (dividend stocks, growth stocks) [03:30] and/or use a stock screener to filter candidates [04:00]. Do not trade overbought signals (CCI above 200); focus only on oversold setups [04:30-05:00].

### Entry trigger

All three conditions must be met before entry:

1. **CCI reaches -200 level twice** [01:00-01:30]: The CCI indicator must reach the -200 oversold level on at least two separate occasions. Do not enter on the first occurrence; wait for the second move into oversold territory to avoid false signals [01:30-02:00].

2. **Regular divergence forms** [02:00-02:30]: A regular divergence must appear between CCI and price. For a downtrend: price makes lower lows but CCI makes higher lows (bullish divergence). This indicates weakening downside momentum [02:00-02:30].

3. **Trend line breakout** [05:00-05:30]: After the above conditions, wait for a trend line break. The speaker draws a trend line connecting significant price lows during the downtrend, and entry occurs when price breaks above this trend line [05:30]. This confirms "a true momentum shift" [07:30].

The speaker explicitly states: "we have to wait for the breakout to enter the market" [07:00-07:30] to protect capital and avoid early entry leading to further losses [07:30].

### Invalidation / skip conditions

If all three conditions are not met, do not enter [07:30-08:00]. Specifically, if CCI reaches -200 twice and a divergence forms but NO trend line breakout occurs, skip the trade entirely [07:30-08:00]. If the first two conditions are met but price breaks the trend line downward (not upward), the setup is invalidated.
