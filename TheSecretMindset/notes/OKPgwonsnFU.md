## Summary
The video teaches a price action strategy using Heiken-Ashi pressure zones. The core concept is identifying 3+ consecutive HA candles with overlapping wicks (lower wicks for bullish pressure, upper wicks for bearish pressure) as reversal zones. Entry occurs when an opposite-color HA candle forms after these pressure zones, preferably at support/resistance confluence levels. Variations include adding weekly VWAP confirmation, Fibonacci retracements, and psychological round number levels. Trade management suggests targeting a 2:1 risk/reward ratio and possibly moving stops to breakeven after color change.
