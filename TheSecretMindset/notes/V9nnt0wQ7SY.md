## Strategy 2: RSI Divergence and Double Tap Reversal System (System Two)

### Indicators and settings

### Context / bias filter
Identify when trends are exhausting by detecting divergences [05:00-06:00]:
- Bearish divergence: price makes higher high, but RSI makes lower high [05:00-05:30]
- Bullish divergence: price makes lower low, but RSI makes higher low [05:30]

Divergence must develop over at least 20 candles; quick divergences over 5 candles are typically fake [06:00]

### Entry trigger

**Divergence Entry [05:00-06:00]**
When divergence pattern forms:
- Draw line connecting price peaks (for bearish divergence)
- Draw line connecting RSI peaks (for bearish divergence)
- When price goes up but RSI goes down, "the reversal is loading" [05:30-06:00]
- Opposite for bullish divergence: price down but RSI up [06:00]

**Double Tap Failure Entry [06:30-08:00]**
The critical pattern: "the double tap"

For shorts (at overbought extreme):
- RSI hits 80 [06:30-07:00]
- Pulls back to approximately 70 [07:00]
- Tries for 80 again but fails (does not reach 80) [07:00]
- Entry: short right there [07:00]
- Example: "RSI hits 80, pulls back to around 70, pushes up but only reaches 78. That's the double tap failure. I shorted right there with a stop above the high. 50 pips winner" [07:00-07:30]

For longs (at oversold extreme):
- RSI hits approximately 18-20 [07:30]
- Bounces to around 30 [07:30]
- Drops again but does not reach 20 level [07:30]
- Entry: "That's your reversal buy signal" [07:30]

### Stop loss
"Short right there with a stop above the high" [07:00] for double tap shorts at 80
