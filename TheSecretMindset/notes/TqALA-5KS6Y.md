## Summary

This video presents a day trading/scalping strategy combining Weekly VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) as a trend and dynamic support/resistance indicator with RSI (period 50) displayed as colored candles. Green candles indicate RSI above 50 (bullish momentum); red candles indicate RSI below 50 (bearish momentum). Entry signals occur at VWAP breakouts or pullbacks when price action and momentum align. The strategy emphasizes avoiding choppy range-bound markets and entering only when trend and momentum are confirmed. Stop losses are placed beyond VWAP and key price levels, with a target risk-reward ratio of 2:1.

## Strategy 1: Weekly VWAP + RSI Colored Candles - Uptrend Breakout

### Indicators and settings

- **Weekly VWAP:** Calculated from weekly data, applied to lower timeframe chart [01:00]; "this line reacts to price movements based on the volume during that period" [00:00-00:30]
- **RSI Colored Candles:** RSI period 50 [03:00], overbought/oversold levels NOT USED; candles are green when RSI > 50, red when RSI < 50 [03:00-03:30]

### Context / bias filter

**Uptrend Confirmation [03:30-04:00]:**
"Price above the view up bullish Trend green RSI candles bullish momentum in this situation you'll look for long signals" [03:30-04:00].

**Strongest Market Conditions [04:00-04:30]:**
"If the trend is strong and there's also momentum you should see something like this price breaks the weekly if you are blind RSI maintains above its 50 line so the candles remain green and the price moves in a healthy uptrend" [04:00-04:30].

### Entry trigger

#### Type 1: Breakout Entry - Uptrend [05:00-06:00]

"Price moves above the view up this can often signal that sellers are exiting their positions or buyers are gaining strength which moves price above view up" [05:00-05:30].

**Optimal Conditions [05:30-06:00]:**
"Ideally the RSI is already green at the time of the Breakout so there's polish momentum before the weekly viewable Breakout" [05:30-06:00]. "This is not a must-have condition but there's something I've observed in my back tests" [05:30-06:00].

**Price Action Confirmation [05:30-06:00]:**
"To filter signals is also good to have a price action confirmation. The weekly view of RSI signal is stronger and more reliable when price moves above a previously resistance level or breaks a trend line" [05:30-06:00].

Entry occurs when: (1) Price breaks above weekly VWAP, (2) RSI candles are green (or turning green), (3) Price action confirms (resistance break, trend line break).

#### Type 2: Pullback Entry - Uptrend [07:00-08:00]

"When price is on an extended move upward or downward and then makes a movement towards the weekly view of line if the RSI doesn't change its color meaning the overall momentum is still on this is a chance to buy or sell at the weekly average price" [07:00-07:30].

**Conditions [07:00-07:30]:**
Price pulls back to weekly VWAP without momentum reversal (RSI candles stay green). "If the RSI changes its color during the pullback let's say price was below view up RSI was red and comes back to the weekly view up but the candles are now green you have to rely on a price action trigger to enter after the RSI crosses once again below 50" [07:00-07:30].

**Price Action Confirmation [07:30-08:00]:**
"Trend line breakouts pin bars with big Wicks or support and resistance breakouts are valid signals" [07:30-08:00].

Example trade [08:00-08:30]: "we have this important support level which was broken so this is our price action trigger to initiate a short stop loss goes above the weekly view upline and above this previous resistance level and we set a risk reward ratio of 2" [08:00-08:30].

### Stop loss

**Uptrend/Long Stop [08:00-08:30, 09:30]:**
"Stop loss goes above the view upline and below the swing low" [09:30]. More generally, "stop goes above the view upline" [08:00-08:30].

**Downtrend/Short Stop [08:00-08:30, 13:00]:**
"Stop loss goes above the view upline" [08:00-08:30] (for a short trade, this means above the most recent resistance).

### Take profit / exit

**Target Identification [09:30]:**
"We'll Target this swing High" [09:30] (for long trades). For short trades, target "the previous swing low" [13:00].

**Risk-Reward Ratio [08:30, 13:00]:**
"We set a risk reward ratio of 2" [08:30], stated again at [13:00]: "will Target the previous swing low which offers a risk to reward ratio of 2" [13:00].

**Manual Exit in Choppy Markets [13:30]:**
"Price moved above the view upline so we'll close the trade manually with a small loss" [13:30].

### Invalidation / skip conditions

1. **Range-Bound Market (Frequent RSI Color Changes) [11:00-12:00]:**
"When the RSI changes its color four times in a short amount of time you mark the range and trade when price breaks it" [11:00-12:00]. "Avoid trading inside of it" [11:00-12:00]. "When you find yourself analyzing a market...with price moving above and below the weekly view up multiple times again don't trade draw the channel and look for opportunities when the trend is more obvious" [14:30-15:00].

2. **Price Above VWAP but RSI Bearish [12:30]:**
"Price is above the view up but the candles are red so this is not a good time to initiate any trade" [12:30].

3. **Price Below VWAP but RSI Bullish [08:00-09:00]:**
Conversely, if price is below VWAP but RSI candles are green, do not enter (though not explicitly stated, implied by the "price-momentum alignment" logic).

### Mechanizability

PARTIAL. Weekly VWAP is mechanically computable from volume data. RSI colored candles (period 50, green/red based on 50-level) are mechanically computable from price. However, the strategy heavily depends on visual price action confirmation (trend line breaks, support/resistance breaks, pin bars with "big wicks"), which cannot be fully automated without additional rules. The rules for identifying "extended moves," "important" levels, and "big wicks" are subjective and require either discretionary trader judgment or parameterized threshold definitions.
