## Summary
The video teaches three market breadth indicators used to time market entries and exits by measuring participation across a stock index. The Advance/Decline Line tracks the ratio of advancing versus declining stocks; the McClellan Oscillator adds smoothing to detect overbought/oversold conditions; the Arms Index (TRIN) incorporates volume to identify whether volume is flowing into advancing or declining stocks. Each indicator is presented with specific buy and sell signals and divergence rules to predict reversals.

## Strategy 1: Advance/Decline Line Trading

### Indicators and settings
- Indicator: Advance/Decline (A/D) Line
- Calculation: Number of advancing stocks minus number of declining stocks, cumulative from fixed starting point (0) [02:00]
- Does not include unchanged stocks [02:30]
- Period: daily or weekly basis [02:00]
- Applied: plotted against relevant index for comparison [02:30]

### Context / bias filter
Use by comparing A/D Line trend against index trend to identify convergence or divergence [09:00]. Four main interpretations [03:00]:

1. **Bullish confirmation:** Both A/D Line and index trending upward. Rise in index is driven by rise in majority of stocks [03:00].

2. **Bearish confirmation:** Both A/D Line and index trending downward. Decline in index is driven by decline in majority of stocks [03:30].

3. **Bullish divergence:** A/D Line trending up while index trending down. Decline in index driven by minority of stocks; sellers losing conviction [03:30].

4. **Bearish divergence:** A/D Line trending down while index trending up. Increase in index driven by minority of stocks; buyers losing conviction [04:00].

### Entry trigger
Entry signals depend on divergence interpretation:
- **Long entry:** When A/D Line is trending up with index up (confirmation) [03:00], or A/D Line up while index down (bullish divergence predicting reversal upward) [03:30].
- **Short entry:** When A/D Line trending down with index down (confirmation) [03:30], or A/D Line down while index up (bearish divergence predicting downward reversal) [04:00].

### Take profit / exit
NOT STATED. Implied: hold position as long as trend/divergence structure remains intact.

### Invalidation / skip conditions
Divergence invalidated if index reverses to align with A/D Line trend, negating the divergence signal [04:00].

### Claimed performance
Investors "tend to believe that the market will continue its uptrend in the near future" with bullish confirmation [03:00]. Bullish divergence signals market "will show a reversal and trend upwards" [03:30]. Bearish divergence signals market "will show a reversal and trend downwards" [04:00].

## Strategy 2: McClellan Oscillator Trading

### Indicators and settings
- Indicator: McClellan Oscillator
- Calculation: Smoothed difference between number of advancing and declining stocks on NYSE [04:30]
- Overbought zone: +70 to +100 [05:00]
- Oversold zone: -70 to -100 [05:00]
- Extreme levels: above +100 or below -100 indicate extremely overbought/oversold market [05:30]

### Context / bias filter
Applicable to equities markets with substantial number of actively-trading individual stocks [04:30]. Extreme readings (above +100 or below -100) associated with powerful bull or bear market trends with "staying power for only another couple of weeks or somewhat longer" [05:30].

### Entry trigger
**Buy signal:** McClellan oscillator falls into oversold area (-70 to -100) and then turns up [05:00]. Best to look for upside crossovers from within oversold area [05:30].

**Sell signal:** McClellan oscillator rises into overbought zone (+70 to +100) and then turns down [05:00]. Best to look for downside crossovers from within overbought area [05:30].

### Stop loss
NOT STATED

### Take profit / exit
NOT STATED. Implied: exit when oscillator reaches opposite extreme or turns in opposite direction.

### Invalidation / skip conditions
Ignore crossovers that occur in middle ranges of oscillator without "compelling other evidence of a technical nature" [06:00]. These mid-range signals are unreliable without confirmation.

### Claimed performance
"Buy indications are given when the McClellan oscillator falls into an oversold area" [05:00]. Crossovers from extreme zones indicate directional changes. Extreme readings indicate "powerful bull or bear market trends" [05:30].

## Strategy 3: Arms Index (TRIN) Trading

### Indicators and settings
- Indicator: Arms Index (TRIN - Total Return Index)
- Calculation: Hybrid indicator using price and volume; indicates whether volume is flowing into advancing or declining stocks [06:30]
- Measuring: Ratio of advancing volume to declining volume [06:30]
- Calculation timing: calculated throughout trading day, but close is primarily of interest [07:00]
- Buy signal threshold: below 1.0 [07:30]
- Sell signal threshold: above 1.0 [07:30]
- Neutral level: 1.0 [07:30]
- Extreme reversal signals: above 3.0 or below 0.5 indicate imminent reversal [08:00]

### Context / bias filter
Arms Index above 1: more volume in declining stocks; indicates heavy selling pressure [07:00]. If above 1 persists over multiple days, associated with market being oversold near bottom [07:00]. Below 1: more volume in advancing stocks [06:30].

Functions as a contrary indicator - extreme readings suggest market may soon reverse [07:00].

### Entry trigger
**Buy signals:** TRIN closes below 1.0 [07:30]. Stronger buy when TRIN drops below 0.5, indicating imminent reversal [08:00]. High readings (well above 1) that persist suggest market oversold and ready to turn up [07:00].

**Sell signals:** TRIN closes above 1.0 [07:30]. Stronger sell when TRIN exceeds 3.0, indicating imminent reversal [08:00].

### Stop loss
NOT STATED

### Take profit / exit
NOT STATED. Implied: exit when TRIN approaches 1.0 (neutral) or reverses direction.

### Invalidation / skip conditions
Contrarian signals may not work if "circumstances" differ [07:30]. Readings too far from 1.0 "might be a sign that the market may soon reverse" rather than continue [08:00], so extreme readings paradoxically suggest shorter holding periods.

### Claimed performance
Traders "generally take anything under 1.0 to be a buy signal" [07:30]. "The further away you get from neutrality, the stronger the bulls or bears are on that day" [08:00]. Extreme readings (above 3.0 or below 0.5) are "a sign of an imminent reversal" [08:00].
