## Summary
The "Triple Screen System" for day trading using three aligned timeframes (4-hour/1-hour/15-minute or 1-hour/15-minute/5-minute). Screen 1 (highest timeframe) determines bias (uptrend=longs only, downtrend=shorts only), Screen 2 (middle timeframe) identifies pullback zones with confluence, Screen 3 (fastest timeframe) waits for rejection confirmation patterns (pin bar, engulfing candle, momentum shift). All three screens must align before entry. The strategy emphasizes patience, strict timeframe adherence, and never trading against the boss (highest) timeframe, with specific stop loss (below rejection pattern) and profit targets (next resistance level).

### Screen 1: Boss Timeframe (Bias Filter) [02:30 - 04:30]

**Timeframe selection:**
- Day trading: 4-hour chart [01:30 - 02:00]
- Faster scalping: 1-hour chart [01:30 - 02:00]

**Context / bias filter** [03:00 - 03:30]:
Look at last two major swing points:
- Higher highs and higher lows = uptrend. "You're only taking long trades today. No exceptions" [03:00]
- Lower highs and lower lows = downtrend. "You're only taking short trades" [03:00]
- No clear trend (choppy sideways) = no trades [04:30]

**Critical rule** [03:30 - 04:00]: "Never trade against the boss timeframe, ever, no matter how perfect the setup looks" [11:30]. "82% of losing trades happen when you're fighting the bigger trend" [01:00].

**Mark major swings** [04:00 - 04:30]: "Mark the major swings on this timeframe. These are the zones that will control everything on your lower timeframes. If the 4-hour has resistance here, this level will affect your 15-minute trades whether you see it or not" [04:00 - 04:30].

**Time required**: "This 5-second check will save you from 80% of losing trades" [02:30]. "This took me exactly 5 seconds to determine" [09:30].

### Screen 2: Middle Timeframe (Setup Zone Identification) [05:00 - 07:00]

**Timeframe selection:**
- Day trading: 1-hour chart [01:30 - 02:00, 05:00]
- Faster scalping: 15-minute chart [01:30 - 02:00]

**Context / bias filter** [05:30 - 06:30]:
"You're hunting for pullbacks in trends. While screen one told you the direction, screen two shows you exactly where to enter" [05:00 - 05:30].

**Confluence zone identification** [05:30 - 06:00]: "You want confluence zones where multiple factors align. Here's what I'm scanning for: Previous resistance that should now act as support, the 20-period moving average, previous swing lows, or even round numbers. When two or more of these line up, that's your kill zone" [05:30 - 06:00].

**Quality of pullback** [06:30 - 07:00]: "The setup shape matters, too. You want to see a controlled pullback, not a violent crash. Think of it like a rubber band, a gentle stretch that's ready to snap back, not one that's about to break. If price crashes through multiple support levels in one candle, that's not a pullback. That's a reversal starting" [06:30 - 07:00].

Pattern observation: "Does it drift down slowly over several candles? Perfect. That's a healthy pullback. Does it spike down in one massive red candle? Danger. That's emotional selling, and it might continue" [07:00].

**Patience rule** [06:00 - 06:30]: "If there's no pullback, there's no trade. If price is extended way above support, you wait" [06:00 - 06:30].

### Screen 3: Fastest Timeframe (Trigger Confirmation) [07:30 - 09:30]

**Timeframe selection:**
- Day trading: 15-minute chart [01:30 - 02:00, 07:30]
- Faster scalping: 5-minute chart [01:30 - 02:00]

**Context / bias filter** [08:00 - 08:30]: "You've already done the heavy lifting. The 4-hour said buy only, the 1-hour showed you where. Now the 15-minute tells you when" [08:00].

**Trigger confirmation patterns** [08:30 - 09:00]:

2. A bullish engulfing candle (closes above previous candle)

"Here's what amateurs do. They see price touch their support zone and immediately market buy. Then they watch it drop another 20 pips before reversing. They entered too early. The level was right, but the timing was wrong" [08:00 - 08:30].

**Example trade** [09:00 - 09:30]: "Price hits my value area, then I see a massive bullish engulfing that closes above the previous candle. That's my trigger. Entry execution has to be immediate once you get confirmation" [09:00].

### Entry trigger

All three screens must align [09:30 - 10:30]:
1. Screen 1 (4-hour): Clear trend direction (higher highs/lows for long, lower highs/lows for short)
2. Screen 2 (1-hour): Price has pulled back to confluence zone (2+ factors aligned)
3. Screen 3 (15-minute): Rejection pattern forms (pin bar, engulfing, momentum shift)

"The power of this strategy comes from confirmation across all three timeframes. Skip one and you're gambling, not trading" [12:30].

### Stop loss

**Primary rule** [09:00]: "Your stop goes below the lowest point of the rejection pattern, not some arbitrary number" [09:00].

**From Screen 2** [07:00 - 07:30]: "Your stop goes below the setup structure" [07:00 - 07:30].

Screen 2 also "controls your stop loss and targets" - keep "risk reward clean and logical" [07:00 - 07:30].

### Take profit / exit

**Primary target** [07:00 - 07:30]: "Your target aims for the next resistance level visible on this time frame" [07:00 - 07:30]. Example: "Target just below the previous 1-hour resistance" [10:30].

**Trade management** [10:30 - 11:00]:
"The 15-minute controls my entry and initial stop. The 1-hour controls my targets and trailing stops. If we break above a major swing, I'll trail my stop to break even. The 4-hour keeps me patient. As long as the uptrend is intact, I hold" [10:30 - 11:00].

**Trailing stop rule** [10:30 - 11:00]: Trail stop to breakeven when price breaks above major swing level on 1-hour.

**Skip if no pullback on Screen 2** [06:00]: "If there's no pullback, there's no trade" [06:00].
