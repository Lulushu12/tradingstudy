## The Three Laws of Wyckoff

### Law 1: Supply and Demand [01:00]
"When demand is greater than supply, prices rise, and when supply is greater than demand, prices fall" [01:00]. Trader studies balance by "comparing price and volume bars over time" [01:00].

### Law 2: Cause and Effect [01:00 - 01:30]
"The differences between supply and demand are not random. Instead, they come after periods of preparation, as a result of specific events" [01:00 - 01:30]. "A period of accumulation (cause) eventually leads to an uptrend (effect). In contrast, a period of distribution (cause) eventually results in a downtrend (effect)" [01:00 - 01:30].

### Law 3: Effort vs Result [01:30 - 02:00]
"Changes in an asset's price are a result of an effort, which is represented by the trading volume. If the price action is in harmony with the volume, there is a good chance the trend will continue. But, if the volume and price diverge, the market trend is likely to stop or change direction" [01:30 - 02:00].

Example: "Market consolidates with a very high volume after a long bearish trend. The high volume indicates a big effort, but the sideways movement suggests a small result. So, there is a lot of activity, but no more significant price drops. Such a situation could indicate that the downtrend may be over, and a reversal is near" [02:00].

## Wyckoff Price Cycle (4 Stages)

### Stage 1: Accumulation [03:30 - 04:00]

"Process of accumulation is the first stage of the Wyckoff price cycle. The Composite Man accumulates assets before most investors. The accumulation is done gradually to avoid the price from changing significantly. Bulls are gaining power slowly" [03:30 - 04:00].

**Chart appearance**: "Price action on the chart is flat. In other words, the process of accumulation is illustrated by a ranging price structure" [03:30 - 04:00].

**Identification signal**: "Higher lows within the range is usually considered a signal that the price action is currently in an Accumulation phase" [04:00].

### Stage 2: Markup (Uptrend) [04:00 - 04:30]

"When the Composite Man is holding enough power, and the selling force is depleted, he starts pushing the market up. The emerging trend attracts more investors, causing demand to increase" [04:00 - 04:30].

"There may be multiple phases of accumulation during an uptrend. We may call them re-accumulation phases, where the bigger trend stops and consolidates for a while, before continuing its upward movement" [04:00 - 04:30].

"As the market moves up, other traders are encouraged to buy. At this point, demand is higher than supply" [04:30].

### Stage 3: Distribution [04:30 - 05:00]

"The Composite Man starts distributing his holdings. He sells his profitable positions to those entering the market at a late stage. The distribution phase is marked by a sideways movement that absorbs demand until it gets exhausted" [04:30 - 05:00].

**Chart appearance**: "Price action on the chart at this stage is flat" [05:00].

**Identification signal**: "One indication that the market is in a Distribution stage will be the sustained failure of price to create higher lows on the chart. The price action creates lower highs which is an indication that the market is currently experiencing a selloff" [05:00].

### Stage 4: Markdown (Downtrend) [05:00 - 05:30]

"Soon after the distribution phase, the market starts reverting to the downside. After the Composite Man is done selling a good amount of his shares, he starts pushing the market down. The supply becomes much greater than demand, and the downtrend is established" [05:00 - 05:30].

"When the bearish trend is finally over, a new accumulation phase begins" [05:30].

## Wyckoff Volume Spread Analysis [05:30 - 06:00]

"Volume is of a great importance for the Wyckoff trader, because it can provide valuable information into what is really going on 'behind the scenes'" [05:30 - 06:00].

"Wyckoff Volume Analysis provides confirmation of progressing events during the Wyckoff Price Cycle. High volumes can lead to sustained price moves on the chart. Wyckoff Volume Spread Analysis also helps you identify periods when the price is transitioning between the different stages of the Wyckoff Price Cycle" [05:30 - 06:00].

**Breakout validation** [06:00]:
"When the price moves through a key level during the Wyckoff Price Cycle, you should consider the move valid if the trading volumes are relatively high during the breakout. If the volumes are decreasing, then you are probably looking at a spring (which is a false breakout) rather than a real breakout" [06:00].

**Spring pattern** [07:30 - 08:00]: "Transitional price action behavior that often occurs between the cycle stages" [07:30 - 08:00]. Example: "Price breaks upper level with decreasing volume = possible spring forming" [08:30 - 09:00].

## Basic Trading Rules

### Entry Signals [07:00 - 08:30]

"Ideally, you should enter a trade when the price action is transiting from Accumulation to Markup and from Distribution to Markdown" [07:30].

**Long entry** [08:00 - 08:30]:
- "End of Accumulation stage is the beginning of a Markup, which could be traded to the long side" [07:00]
- "Buy when the price breaks the flat range through the upper level" (of Accumulation) [08:00 - 08:30]
- Must confirm with volume [08:30]

**Short entry** [08:00 - 08:30]:
- "End of Distribution stage is the beginning of a Markdown, which could be traded to the short side" [07:00]
- "Sell when the price action breaks the lower support level of the Distribution area" [08:00 - 08:30]
- Must confirm with volume [08:30]

### Confirmation Requirements [07:30 - 08:00]

"You would need to confirm the current stage when the price is ranging. It would help to identify increasing bottoms for an Accumulation and decreasing tops for Distribution" [07:30 - 08:00].

"You could also analyze the previous price moves for additional clues" [07:30 - 08:00].

"Chart patterns can also be helpful in identifying Accumulation and Distribution processes on the chart" [08:00].

### Stop Loss Placement [08:30]

"If you are trading a Markup, your stop loss should be located below the lowest point of the Accumulation stage" [08:30].

"If you are trading a Markdown, then your stop loss order should be positioned above the highest point during the Distribution stage" [08:30].

### Take Profit [09:00 - 09:30]

"If you haven't reached your profit target, you would close your trade when the price action begins to create increasing tops on the chart" (during Markdown) [09:00 - 09:30].

Implied: Hold through Markdown until Accumulation phase begins (identified by increasing lows) [09:30 - 10:00].
