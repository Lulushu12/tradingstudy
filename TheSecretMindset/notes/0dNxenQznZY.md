## Summary
This video teaches a Pressure Zone price action strategy based on candlestick shadow analysis. Traders identify zones where three or more consecutive bars have overlapping shadows, indicating trapped traders and likely reversal pressure. The strategy enters when price returns to the zone and forms a confirmation candle closing beyond it. Confluence filters include moving averages, higher timeframe levels, trendlines, and pivot points to avoid false signals in ranging markets.

## Strategy 1: Pressure Zone Trading

### Indicators and settings
- Moving Averages for filtering: 200, 100, 50 period [09:30]; VWAP also mentioned [09:30]
- Pivot Points: central pivot point (CPP), S1 and R1 levels [12:30]
- Trendlines: drawn on obvious levels with most touches [11:30]

### Context / bias filter

**Market condition filter [09:00-09:30]**
- Pressure zones work better at key price levels, not in congested/ranging markets [09:00]
- Apply moving average filter: buy bullish pressure zones ABOVE moving average, sell bearish zones BELOW [09:30]
- Higher timeframe support and resistance levels add confirmation [10:30]
- "The better trades usually happen at key price levels and when you take volume into account" [09:00]

**Confluence factors [10:30-12:30]**
- High probability when pressure zone located at higher timeframe S/R [10:30]
- Strong impulsive turning points where price previously reacted strongly [10:30]
- Trendlines and channels; draw obvious ones with most touches [11:00-11:30]
- Pivot points: CPP and S1/R1 levels especially [12:30]

### Pressure Zone Identification [04:00-05:30]

**Bullish Pressure Zone [04:30-05:00]**
1. Find three consecutive bars with overlapping lower shadows [04:30]
2. Mark out lowest low among the three bars [04:30]
3. Mark out lowest closing price of three candle bodies (exclude shadows) [04:30]
4. Area between these two price levels = bullish pressure zone [04:30]

**Bearish Pressure Zone [05:00-05:30]**
1. Find three consecutive bars with overlapping upper shadows [05:00]
2. Mark out highest high among three bars [05:00]
3. Mark out highest close of three candle bodies (exclude shadows) [05:00]
4. Area between these two price levels = bearish pressure zone [05:00]

**Caveat [05:30]**: "Small overlaps of shadows can create unreliable Pressure Zones. To avoid them, ignore overlaps below certain threshold. For higher timeframes (1 hour and above), consider increasing threshold as necessary" [05:30]

### Entry trigger

**Long Pressure Zone Entry [06:00-06:30]**
1. Find bullish pressure zone
2. Buy above next bullish bar that overlaps with zone and closes above it [06:00]
   - The third bar of the pressure zone is acceptable if bullish [06:00]
3. If price returns to bullish pressure zone and forms another signal candle, can re-enter [06:00]

**Short Pressure Zone Entry [06:30-07:00]**
1. Find bearish pressure zone
2. Sell below next bearish bar that overlaps with zone and closes below it [06:30]
   - The third bar of pressure zone acceptable if bearish [06:30]
3. If price returns to bearish pressure zone and forms another signal candle, can re-enter [06:30]

**Psychology of entry [01:30-03:30]**
Three consecutive bars with overlapping shadows trap traders (those on wrong side of move), causing emotional cycle that repeats three times. This creates reliable pressure in that compact price range.

### Stop loss
"The low of this extended wick provides prudent level for stop placement" [07:30]
- Place stop just below the wick that sellers couldn't push beyond [07:30]
- Rationale: "Sellers pushed price to low of that wick but could not push it beyond that point. Level has lower likelihood of getting hit" [07:30]

### Take profit / exit

**Discretionary approach [08:00-08:30]**
"Probably most difficult question to answer; depends on risk tolerance" [08:00]
- Sometimes best to take profit quickly and walk away [08:00]
- Other times: take some money off table, move stop to breakeven, let trade run [08:00]
- "This discretion will be up to you" [08:00]

**Using pivot points for exits [12:30]**
"Exit part of position at next pivot levels" [12:30]

### Invalidation / skip conditions
- For long: "If price breaks below Pressure Zone, setup becomes invalid" [06:00]
- For short: "If price breaks above Pressure Zone, setup becomes invalid" [06:30]
- Skip signals in congested/ranging markets [09:00]
- Skip small shadow overlaps below threshold [05:30]
