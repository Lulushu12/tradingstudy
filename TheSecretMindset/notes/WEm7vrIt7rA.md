### Mechanizability
DISCRETIONARY
The core framework is analytical rather than mechanical. While the flip concept is logically sound (level breakout + retrace to test from other side), execution requires extensive visual and subjective judgment on: market phase identification, what constitutes "hard bounce" vs "drift through," volume adequacy, momentum strength, candle narrative interpretation, and exact entry timing on lower timeframe. Cannot be coded from OHLCV alone without inventing multiple subjective thresholds.
