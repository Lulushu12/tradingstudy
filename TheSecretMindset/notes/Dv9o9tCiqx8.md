## Wyckoff Volume Principles (Framework, not a strategy)

### Core interpretive rules stated

1. **Buying vs Selling Volume** [01:00 - 01:30]: Rising price + rising volume = buying volume, strong trend [01:00]. Rising price + falling volume = weak trend, buyers leaving [01:30].

2. **Volume Climax** [02:00 - 03:00]: Ultra-high volume bar appearing signals potential trend reversal, especially if volume is extreme but price spread is narrow (effort without result) [02:30 - 03:00]. According to Wyckoff's third law: if market fails to rise with significant volume, the volume must be selling volume [02:30 - 03:00].

3. **Volume and Support/Resistance** [03:30 - 04:00]: Points where market trades on high volume are points of strong support and resistance, first signs of trend change or breakout [03:30].

4. **Breakout Validation** [04:00 - 04:30]: High volume at bottom of reversals suggests buying/accumulation [04:00]. Low volume breakout is false [12:30]. High volume validating breakout = low-risk trade [04:00 - 04:30].

5. **Trend Health** [09:30 - 10:30]: Uptrend is strong if volume increases as price rises and decreases on pullbacks [09:30]. Downtrend is strong if volume increases as price falls and decreases on uptrend retracements [10:00 - 10:30].

6. **Reversals** [10:30 - 11:30]: Volume climax (extreme spike larger than historical spikes) crowns point of market reversal [11:30]. Higher-than-average volume sustained for several days creates volume climax [11:30].

7. **Low Volume Signals** [06:00 - 06:30]: Market moving higher with falling/low volume is not genuine [06:00]. Market falling with falling volume is not genuine [06:00].
