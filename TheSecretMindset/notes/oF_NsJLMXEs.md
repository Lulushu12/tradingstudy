## Strategy 1: Trend Trading Pullbacks

### Indicators and settings

- Market structure: Track highs and lows to identify trend direction
- Trend direction: Read as series of higher highs and higher lows (uptrend) or lower lows and lower highs (downtrend) [01:00-01:30]

### Context / bias filter

**Essential rule:** Trade in the direction of the dominant trend, not against it [00:00-00:30].

**Uptrend conditions:**
- Price above previous highs (Break of Structure = bullish confirmation)
- Lows respect previous lows (buyers in control)
- Break below previous low = shift in market structure, warning that sellers taking over [00:30]

**Downtrend conditions:**
- Price below previous lows
- Highs can't reach previous highs (sellers in control)
- Break above previous high = shift in market structure, buyers may take control [01:00]

**Consolidation:** Price stuck in sideways range, bouncing between supply and demand [01:00]. Can align with previous trend for bias [01:30].

### Entry trigger

**Trending days:**
Enter pullback trades in direction of trend. Look for:
- Series of bullish trend candles with decent-sized bodies and high closes (uptrend) [02:30]
- Series of bearish bars with average-sized bodies and lower closes (downtrend) [03:30]
- More bullish bars than bearish bars in uptrend, both in quality and quantity [03:00]
- More bearish bars than bullish in downtrend

**Pullback bars in uptrend:**
- Short-lived narrow bearish bars with wicks at bottom [03:00]
- Each follow-up bullish bar should have higher high, higher low, and higher close than previous [04:00]

**Range days:**
- Look for buys only at lower channel of range (in previous uptrend consolidation) [05:30]
- Look for sells only at upper channel of range (in previous downtrend consolidation) [05:30]
- Avoid middle of range (can be very choppy) [01:30]

## Strategy 2: Supply and Demand Zone Trading

### Indicators and settings

Four main zone types [08:00-09:00]:

1. **Rally-base-Rally** (demand zone in uptrend):
   - Rally, consolidation (base), another rally [08:00]
   - Forms magnet for buyers

2. **Drop-base-Drop** (supply zone in downtrend):
   - Drop, consolidation (base), another drop [08:30]
   - Forms magnet for sellers

3. **Drop-base-Rally** (demand zone, reversal):
   - Drop, base, rally [08:30]
   - Marks shift from falling to rising

4. **Rally-base-Drop** (supply zone, reversal):
   - Rally, base, drop [09:00]
   - Marks shift from rising to falling

### Context / bias filter

Identify zones from price action: look for sharp rises and declines - footprints of smart money [06:30]. Look for big, bold candles with little to no wicks [06:30].

Two golden rules [07:30-08:00]:
1. Recent zones are far more powerful than old ones [07:30]
2. Supply and demand zones are one-time use only; once reversed, lose power [07:30]

### Entry trigger

**Demand zones:** Look for fast upward move; place line at high of most recent red candle before price increase and another at most recent swing low [07:00]

**Supply zones:** Look for sharp decline; place lines at low of most recent green candle and most recent swing high [07:00]

When price revisits these areas, has potential to reverse [07:00].

## Strategy 3: Volume Spread Analysis (VSA)

### Indicators and settings

Two main components [09:30-10:30]:

1. **Spread:** Difference between opening and closing price [10:00]. 
   - Wide spread = lot of price movement
   - Narrow spread = less activity

2. **Volume:** Number of transactions during time period [10:00]
   - High volume = lot of participation
   - Low volume = lack of interest

Concept: Changes in market price result from effort (volume) vs. result (price) [11:00]. If price and volume in sync, trend continues; if significant difference, trend may change [11:00].

### Context / bias filter

**Sign of Strength:** Buyers control, demand overpowers supply; imbalance causes market to rise [10:30]

**Sign of Weakness:** Sellers control, supply overpowers demand; imbalance leads to market decline [11:00]

### Entry trigger

**Sign of Strength patterns [11:30-13:30]:**

1. **Down Thrust:** Bullish pin bar or Doji with ultra-high/above-average volume [11:30]
   - Low spread but very high volume (mismatch)
   - Demand overpowers supply; price likely to rise [11:30]

2. **Selling Climax:** High-spread bearish candle with big downward rejection wick + ultra-high volume [12:00]
   - Signals market could rise (more demand than supply) [12:00]

3. **Bearish Effort lower than Bearish Result:** High-spread bearish candle, larger spread than previous, but lower volume [12:30]
   - Sellers trying to push down but can't gain strength
   - Disagreement between price and volume = bullish breakout potential [12:30]

4. **Bearish Effort higher than Bearish Result:** Low-spread bearish candle, smaller spread than previous, but higher volume [13:00]
   - Sellers pushing hard but price doesn't move much
   - Could sign buyers about to take over [13:00]

5. **No Supply Bar:** Low-spread bearish candle with downward wick and lower volume than previous two candles [13:00]
   - Supply dried up; buyers run the show
   - Prices tend to rise [13:30]

**Sign of Weakness patterns [13:30-15:30]:**

1. **Up Thrust:** Bearish pin bar or Doji with ultra-high volume [13:30]
   - Low spread but high volume (mismatch)
   - Potential fall in near future [14:00]

2. **Buying Climax:** High-spread bullish candle with big upward rejection wick + ultra-high volume [14:00]
   - Supply overpowers demand; watch for potential drop [14:00]

3. **Bullish Effort lower than Bullish Result:** High-spread bullish candle, larger spread than previous, but lower volume [14:30]
   - No effort shouldn't produce result
   - Market might decrease in future [14:30]

4. **Bullish Effort higher than Bullish Result:** Low-spread bullish candle, smaller spread than previous, but higher volume [15:00]
   - Volume and price don't align; price could fall [15:00]

5. **No Demand Bar:** Low-spread bullish candle with upward wick and lower volume than previous two candles [15:00]
   - Buyers left; sellers run the show
   - Prices tend to fall [15:30]
