## Summary

This video explains Fibonacci extensions as a tool to identify hidden support and resistance levels beyond the 100% retracement level. It covers how to draw extensions using three points (swing high, swing low, and pullback low), the common extension levels (161.8%, 261.8%, 423.6%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%, 100%), and how to use price reactions at these levels as entry points. The speaker demonstrates practical examples on crude oil and stock charts, emphasizing that Fibonacci extensions should be combined with other technical confirmation.
