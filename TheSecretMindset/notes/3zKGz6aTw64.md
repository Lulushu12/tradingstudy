## Summary

This video teaches a volume-based strategy centered on "stopping volume" - extreme volume spikes that halt or reverse trends. The strategy identifies high-volume bars at potential turning points (demand after downtrends, supply after uptrends) and either fades the immediate move (expecting reversal) or trades continuation (if momentum persists). A box is drawn around the signal candle, and trades are entered on breakouts with targets based on swing levels or multiples of risk.

### Entry trigger

**Scenario 1: Fade the Move (Reversal)**
- Price has been falling or rising
- Sudden massive volume spike appears [07:30]
- Small range candle with huge volume (effort ≠ result) [04:30]
- Draw box around high/low of volume candle
- Enter when price breaks above (for fade of down move) or below (for fade of up move) the box [07:30]
- Wait for retest of the box in some cases before entering (not always immediate) [08:30]

**Scenario 2: Continue the Move**
- Price has been falling or rising
- High volume spike with large range candle (effort = result, strong move) [11:30]
- Volume matches the directional move
- Draw box around volume candle
- Enter after price consolidates and breaks above/below box in direction of initial move [08:30]
- Wait for price to retest box (do not enter right after initial breakout) [08:30]
