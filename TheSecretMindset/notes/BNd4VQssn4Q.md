## Strategy 1: 3-Candle Safety Check

### Context / bias filter

This system rejects single-candle thinking entirely [00:00-01:00]:
"The problem isn't finding better patterns. The problem is thinking in single candles at all. I don't trade off one candle. I trade off sequences that tell a complete story" [00:30-01:00]

### Entry framework: Three non-negotiable steps

**Step 1: Exhaustion Signal [01:30-02:00]**

This is the warning signal that trend energy is dying. Must occur first in sequence.

For uptrends - Shooting Star [01:30]:
- Small body near bottom
- Massive upper wick
- Interpretation: "Buyers tried to push higher, got rejected hard, sellers slammed price back down" [01:30]
- Longer the upper wick, stronger the rejection [02:00]

For downtrends - Hammer [02:00]:
- Small body near top
- Huge lower wick extending down
- Interpretation: "Sellers tried to push lower, but buyers stepped in aggressively and pushed price back up by close" [02:00]

Alternative exhaustion signals - Spinning Tops [02:00-02:30]:
- Small bodies in middle with long wicks on both sides
- Interpretation: "Market is confused. Neither buyers nor sellers can maintain control" [02:30]

**Step 2: Indecision Candle [02:30-03:30]**

After exhaustion, confusion. Market doesn't know which way to go next [02:30]

Doji [02:30-03:00]:
- Opens and closes at almost exact same price
- Body basically flat line
- Long wicks on both sides possible, but neither side wins [03:00]

Inside Bar [03:00]:
- Entire candle (body and wicks) fits completely inside previous candle's range [03:00]
- "Market tried to move but couldn't break out of previous candle's boundaries. It's stuck" [03:00]

Small-bodied candles with average wicks [03:00-03:30]:
- Not dramatic like dojis
- Reveal neither side has conviction; moves weak and halfhearted [03:30]

**Step 3: Confirmation Breakthrough [03:30-04:30]**

One side takes control and proves change is real [03:30]

Large-bodied candles with small wicks [03:30-04:00]:
- Sellers take control: big red candle that opens near high, closes near low [04:00]
- Buyers take control: big green candle that opens near low, closes near high [04:00]
- Shows dominance and conviction [03:30]

Engulfing patterns [04:00-04:30]:
- New candle's body completely covers previous candle's body [04:00]
- Bearish engulfing after exhaustion/indecision = sellers overwhelmed buyers [04:00]
- Massive confirmation [04:00]

Gap patterns [04:30]:
- Confirming candle gaps in new direction
- "Shows such strong conviction that price can't even open at previous close" [04:30]

## Context Filter System (Advanced Filters) [08:00-11:30]

Speaker claims: "Imagine if you could filter out 80% of false signals before risking any money. That's exactly what proper context analysis does" [08:30]

**Filter 1: Market Structure Analysis [08:30-09:00]**

Same combo behaves differently in trending vs ranging markets:
- Reversal combo in strong trend = often just brief pause before continuation [09:00]
- Same combo in range-bound market = might actually reverse direction [09:00]
- Trap: "You see perfect bearish reversal combo, but trading against massive daily uptrend. You're trying to catch falling knife" [09:00]
- Higher timeframe trend will crush your position [09:00]

**Filter 2: Level Significance [09:30-10:00]**

Timeframe hierarchy matters; higher timeframe levels carry more weight:
- Reversal combo at major weekly support = massive weight [09:30]
- Same combo at random 5-minute resistance = meaningless [09:30]
- "Higher timeframe levels where institutions place biggest bets. When combo aligns with these levels, you're trading alongside smart money" [09:30-10:00]

**Filter 3: Momentum Alignment [10:00-10:30]**

Always trade WITH higher timeframe momentum, never against [10:00]
- Daily bullish + 5-minute bearish combo = losing battle [10:00]
- Perfect reversal on 5-minute might be "just noise in massive institutional uptrend" [10:30]

**Filter 4: Session Timing [10:30-11:00]**

Volume and session matter:
- Combos during London or New York open = high-volume sessions, real institutional participation, more reliable [10:30-11:00]
- Combos during Asian sessions = low-volume noise that gets reversed when big traders return [11:00]

**Filter 5: Volume Confirmation [11:00-11:30]**

Volume patterns must match combo type:
- Reversal combos: should form on INCREASING volume as new direction takes control [11:00]
- Continuation combos: should show DECREASING volume during pullback, then EXPLOSIVE volume on confirmation [11:00]
- "Volume reveals institutional activity. When combo aligns with smart money volume patterns, your odds skyrocket" [11:00-11:30]
