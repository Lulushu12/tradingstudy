## Candlestick Formations Explained

### Long Bullish Candlestick (Trend Candle)
- Closing price significantly higher than opening price
- Little to no upper wick
- Psychology: Buyer optimism and aggression; strong bullish sentiment
- Signals: Start of new uptrend or continuation
- Indicates: Buyers in complete control, aggressively absorbing supply [00:30-01:30]

### Long Bearish Candlestick (Trend Candle)
- Closing price significantly lower than opening price
- Little to no lower wick
- Psychology: Seller pessimism and aggression
- Signals: Start of new downtrend or continuation
- Indicates: Sellers in control, aggressively pushing lower [01:30-02:00]

### Short Candlestick
- Small body regardless of color
- Represents relative balance between buyers and sellers
- Psychology: Uncertainty and caution
- Signals: Indecision, consolidation, or declining momentum
- When multiple short candles appear: "declining momentum in the current trend or be a symptom of sideways movement" [03:00]

### Doji Candle
- Opening and closing prices virtually equal
- Psychology: Market at crossroads; previous trend losing momentum
- Indicates: Indecision and balance between buyers/sellers
- Signals: "Precursor to significant market moves" [04:00-04:30]
- Represents: "Back-and-forth results in the price oscillating around the open" [04:00]

### Candlesticks with Long Upper and Lower Wicks
- Long wicks on both sides, small real body
- Psychology: High uncertainty and indecision; significant volatility
- Indicates: Both bullish and bearish sentiment present but no clear winner
- Signals: "Key turning points in the market, or after a prolonged trend" [05:30-06:00]
- Shows: Previous trend losing momentum; shift in market sentiment may be underway [06:00]

### Candlestick with Long Upper Wick and Little/No Lower Wick
- High upper wick, price closes lower than high
- Psychology: "Buying exhaustion" - bullish momentum decreasing
- Indicates: Exhaustion point in uptrend; supply outweighs demand at higher prices
- Signals: Potential change in market sentiment and "selling pressure that drives prices back down" [06:30-07:00]

### Candlestick with Long Lower Wick and Little/No Upper Wick
- Low lower wick, price closes higher than low
- Psychology: "Selling climax" - downward trend losing steam
- Indicates: Exhaustion point in downtrend; demand outweighs supply at lower prices
- Signals: Potential change in market sentiment and "buying pressure that drives prices back up" [08:00-08:30]

### Up Gap Candle
- Lowest price of candle higher than highest price of previous candle
- Psychology: "Significant shift in sentiment in favor of the buyers"
- Indicates: Buying pressure intensified; demand has outpaced supply
- Signals: "Demand has suddenly outpaced supply, and that the balance of power has shifted decisively in favor of the buyers" [09:30]
- Note: Can signal buying opportunities or exhaustion depending on context [09:30-10:00]

### Down Gap Candle
- Highest price of candle lower than lowest price of previous candle
- Psychology: "Significant shift in sentiment in favor of the sellers"
- Indicates: Selling pressure intensified; supply has outpaced demand
- Signals: Can lead to further downside momentum or signal exhaustion [10:00-10:30]
- Note: Gap fills (price returning to fill blank space) are worth monitoring [10:30-11:00]
