## Summary

A comprehensive first-retest trading system based on the speaker's analysis of 1,000 failed retests. The strategy capitalizes on smart money liquidity grabs at support/resistance zones by waiting for the first retest after a clean breakout, then entering only on clear price action confirmation (engulfing candles or pin bars). Key insight: most traders fail because they enter on the touch rather than waiting for rejection. The system emphasizes treating support/resistance as zones (not lines), confirming breakouts with volume spike, recognizing decreasing volume on retests, and understanding that first retests are psychologically strongest while subsequent retests weaken the level.

## Strategy 1: First Retest Trading System

### Indicators and settings

- Volume: High volume on initial breakout [06:00-06:30]; decreasing/low volume on retest/pullback [06:30-07:00]
- Price action patterns: Engulfing candle or pin bar at retest zone [08:30-11:00]
- Support/Resistance: Zone-based, not line-based [04:00-05:30]
- No technical indicators; price action and volume only

### Context / bias filter

- Breakout condition [07:00-07:30]:
  - "Clean, decisive move through the level, not a slow grind, not a hesitant push"
  - "Strong breakout will gap through the level"
  - "Close well beyond the break point"
  - Gap indicates strength behind move [07:30]

- Retest condition [06:30-07:00]:
  - Must occur on "low volume because now you're developing a trend"
  - In uptrend: "rising volume on the moves higher and falling volume on the pullbacks"
  - Decreasing/low volume indicates institutions are NOT participating in pullback [14:00-14:30]

- Level strength [12:00-13:30]:
  - First retest is most important; level has "maximum psychological power" [12:00-12:30]
  - "All the breakout traders are still in their positions. All the stops are still there"
  - Level gets "weaker with each touch" [12:30-13:00]
  - "Focus on the first retest. That's where the real opportunity lies" [13:00-13:30]

### Entry trigger

Five-step systematic process [13:30-15:00]:

1. Identify clean breakout from well-defined consolidation zone [14:00]
2. Confirm breakout with "clear spike in volume. No high volume, no trade" [14:00-14:30]
3. Wait patiently for "first retest of that zone" on "low decreasing volume" [14:30]
4. Wait for "clear price action confirmation signal to form within or at the edge of your zone" [14:30-15:00]

Confirmation candle options [08:30-11:00]:

**Engulfing Candle** [09:30-10:00]:
- "Second candle completely engulfed the first one"
- "Telling us that the sellers [or buyers in uptrend] are in control"
- High volume on engulfing candle is textbook institutional behavior [10:00]
- Entry on close of engulfing candle [11:00]

**Pin Bar** [10:00-11:00]:
- "Pin bar with real conviction"
- Criteria:
  - Wick should be "at least twice the size of the body" [10:30]
  - "Clearly rejecting from the retest zone" [10:30]
  - Volume "above average" [11:00]
  - "Pin bar that sticks out. The rejection should be obvious" [10:30]
  - "Most reliable" setup when "rejects violently" with "dramatic wick" [11:00-12:00]
- Entry on close of pin bar [11:00]

Additional criteria [08:00-09:00]:
- Don't enter on touch; "wait for price action confirmation. Don't enter on the touch. Enter on the rejection" [08:00-08:30]
- Must use lower timeframe to "watch for my price action signal" when price approaches retest zone [09:00]
- "Wait for the market to show you that the level is working" [08:30]

### Stop loss

- Placed "at the edge of the zone, not just the candle" [09:00-09:30]
- "On the other side of the zone, not just the candle to give the trade room to breathe" [15:00]
- Rationale: "If the market breaks the signal candle but stays within the zone, I might still be right. The zone is what matters, not the individual candle" [09:00-09:30]

### Take profit / exit

- "Profit target should be the next clear area of market structure" [15:00]
- Target is "the next major level in the direction of the breakout" [11:30]

### Invalidation / skip conditions

- Breakout without high volume; "No high volume, no trade" [14:00-14:30]
- Retest on high volume indicates institution participation; skip [14:00-14:30]
- Weak breakout (barely pushes through, closes only few pips beyond); skip [07:30]
- After second retest: level power diminishes; subsequent retests weaker [12:30-13:00]
- Avoid trading third or fourth retest; "they keep trying to trade the third or fourth retest and get run over when the level finally breaks" [13:00]
- Not every pin bar; "Be picky. Don't trade every pin bar you see. Wait for the ones that are obvious" [10:30-11:00]
- Pin bar must have "real conviction" with obvious rejection [10:30]
