## No strategy content

This video does not present a discrete, mechanically tradeable strategy with defined entry triggers and exit rules. Instead, it teaches market structure concepts and cycle analysis tools:

1. **Trend identification** [00:30-01:00]: Higher highs/higher lows (bullish), lower lows/lower highs (bearish)
2. **Consolidation phases** [01:00-01:30]: Equal highs and equal lows
3. **Structure break confirmation** [02:00-02:30]: New high/low must be followed by several closes beyond the level
4. **Higher timeframe confirmation** [03:00-03:30]: Verify structure on multiple timeframes
5. **50% retracement rule** [03:30-04:00]: Disregard swings that don't retrace at least 50% of prior swing
6. **Trend-to-range transitions** [04:30-05:30]: Failed test of high + failed test of low = consolidation phase
7. **Cycle magnitude** [06:00-06:30]: Are new legs increasing or decreasing in size?
8. **Cycle velocity** [07:00-07:30]: Count approximate candles to measure speed of new leg formation
9. **Fibonacci projection targets** [07:30-08:30]: Use Trend-based FIB Extension tool from low to high to retracement low for 100% projection
10. **Support/resistance clustering** [09:00-09:30]: Identify 2+ levels on multiple timeframes to anticipate reversals

**General trading approach mentioned** [11:00-12:30]:
- Trade off hourly chart aligned with 4-hour and daily structure
- Confirm structure is trending with several closes beyond swing high/low
- Avoid levels congested with higher timeframe resistance or economic data
- Look for entries at 50% fib retracement, 50-61.8% "golden zone," or 78.6% fib retracement
- Use "confirmation trigger such as the break of a trendline, pattern (flag, triangle), or price action formation such as engulfing bars, pin bars" [12:00]

None of these constitute a specific, complete strategy with defined triggers.
