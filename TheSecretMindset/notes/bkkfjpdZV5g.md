## Concepts taught (not mechanizable as-is)

### Break of Structure
- Definition: price moves through a prior swing point aligned with the existing trend, closes convincingly beyond it [01:30-02:00]
- In uptrend: price flips above a prior high [01:30]
- In downtrend: price breaks under a last critical low [01:00]
- Used as: confirmation that buyers/sellers remain in control

### Change of Character
- Definition: failure to form a new higher high followed by a decisive break of a key higher low [02:00-02:30]
- Signals: potential shift in balance from buyers to sellers or vice versa [02:00]
- More telling than break of structure because it signals a potential reversal [02:00]

### Supply and Demand Zones
- Supply zone: where price consolidated briefly before a big drop [06:00]
- Demand zone: where price consolidated before a rapid climb [06:00]
- Specifics of zone definition: "some traders label with the highest close or open within consolidation, others take the entire range of the basing candles" [07:00]
- Zone potency fades over time as orders are absorbed or executed [06:30]
- Retest zones are "prime locations for structured trades" [07:00]

### Footprint Chart Analysis
Absorption vs. Imbalance distinction [11:00-14:30]:
- **Absorption**: large traders soak up orders, stopping price from moving [11:00]
- **Imbalance**: much stronger buying or selling at a particular price range, driving price in a direction [11:00]
- How to read: bid column (sellers) vs. ask column (buyers) at each price level [11:30]
- Bullish imbalance: large numbers on ask side, small on bid [14:00]
- Bearish imbalance: large numbers on bid side [14:30]

### Pattern Recognition
- Wedges and pullbacks [04:00-04:30]
- Fake breakouts [04:30]
- Liquidity sweeps above old highs or below old lows [05:00-05:30]
- Micro channels and pivot flips [08:30]
- Support/resistance flipping from supply to demand based on breakout strength [08:00-09:30]
