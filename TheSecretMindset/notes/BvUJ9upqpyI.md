## Strategy 1: Opening Channel Breakout (European Session)

### Indicators and settings
- No indicators required; price-based only
- Optional: 200 EMA for trend confirmation [13:00]

### Context / bias filter
Identify the "opening channel" formed from 7:00 GMT (Frankfurt open) to 8:00 GMT (London open) [04:30-05:30]. This hour creates the high and low that forms the neutral zone or "no-man's land" [05:30]. Price must remain inside channel before breakout can occur.

### Entry trigger
After 8:00 GMT London open [06:30]:
- **LONG**: Price breaks ABOVE the opening channel high [06:30-07:00]
- **SHORT**: Price breaks BELOW the opening channel low [06:30-07:00]

Entries occur only when price exits the channel boundaries after London open.

### Stop loss
- For LONG: Placed a few pips BELOW the opening channel low [07:30]
- For SHORT: Placed a few pips ABOVE the opening channel high [07:30]
Stop is positioned this way because if price returns to channel, the original market sentiment is invalidated [08:00]

### Take profit / exit
Project the opening channel range multiple times from the breakout point [08:30]:
- First target: Channel range projected once from breakout price
- Second target: Channel range projected twice from breakout price
- Third target: Channel range projected three times from breakout price

Close 50% of position at first target, trail remaining position to next targets or use discretionary exit [09:00]
