## Summary
A swing trading strategy that combines support/resistance levels with supply/demand zones to time reversals and find high-probability entries. The method involves: marking support/resistance lines where multiple historical reversals occurred (aligned to round numbers), identifying supply/demand zones, overlaying S/R levels inside zones to pinpoint confluence areas, then entering on price action signals (engulfing candles, pin bars) when price tests these levels within the zone. Stop loss is placed at the zone edge, and profits are taken after price creates new extremes out of the zone.

- markets: Forex (examples show currency pairs); strategy applicable to any market
- timeframes: 1-hour minimum for zone/level identification and marking [03:00]; 15-minute chart for price action signal monitoring and entry [06:00-09:00]
- sessions/hours: NOT STATED

## Strategy 1: Confluence Supply & Demand with Support & Resistance

### Indicators and settings
- No technical indicators used; pure price structure and price action
- Support/resistance levels: Identified manually from historical reversals aligned to round numbers
- Supply/demand zones: Identified manually from recent swing structures

### Context / bias filter
**Strategy Framework** [00:00-05:00]: Combine support/resistance levels (precise lines) with supply/demand zones (area-based) to gain advantages of both: "The aim here is to combine the preciseness of support and resistance levels with the area aspect of supply and demand. In this way, we will be able to pinpoint entries into supply and demand zones." [01:00]

**Zone-based flexibility** [01:00-02:00]: "Having a zone you can use to watch for entries is much easier than having a line... You don't really have to worry about a reversal signal forming at an exact point, like you do when trading support and resistance levels, because you've got a whole zone you can use to watch for entries into trades." [01:00-01:30]

**Confluence concept** [05:00-05:30]: "If you combine support and resistance levels with supply and demand zones you can get the best of both worlds. You can eliminate the issue not knowing if the market failing to touch the level or just moving beyond the level is still a reversal signal, whilst also having a better idea of where a reversal out of the supply or demand zone is likely to take place from." [05:00-05:30]

### Entry trigger
**Step 1: Identify Support and Resistance Levels** [02:00-04:00]
- Find points where market reversed multiple times from similar prices [02:30]
- Identify round numbers closest to these reversal points (order clustering phenomenon) [03:00-03:30]
- Adjust reversal lines to sit on top of round number prices [03:30-04:00]
- Use minimum 1-hour timeframe for marking [03:00]
- Result: S/R lines that show where reversals are likely in future [04:00]

**Step 2: Identify Supply and Demand Zones** [04:30-05:00]
- **Supply zones**: "Located by looking for a major drop away from either a single candle or small consolidation structure. You mark the supply zone from the open of last BULLISH candle before the drop and the most recent high before the drop." [04:30-05:00]
- **Demand zones**: "Found when the market makes a large move up from a single candle or a consolidation structure. You mark the demand zone from the open of last BEARISH candle before the drop and the most recent low before the up move." [04:30-05:00]

**Step 3: Find Confluence** [05:00-06:00]
- Overlay support/resistance levels inside supply/demand zones
- "The market reversed out of the zone after hitting the lower support level found at this round number" [05:30-06:00]
- Identify which S/R levels lie within the S/D zones [05:30-06:00]

**Step 4: Switch to Lower Timeframe and Wait for Price Action Signal** [07:00-09:00]
- Move to 15-minute chart when price approaches the S/D zone [06:00-07:00]
- "The only other option is to watch for a price action signal to form when the market hits one of the support or resistance levels" [06:30-07:00]
- Monitor for price action patterns:
  - **Bullish Engulfing Candles** [07:30-08:00]: "When the market hit the first support level a bullish engulfing candle formed" [07:30]
  - **Pin Bars** [09:00]: "You can also look for other price action patterns to get your signal, pin bars representing other great price action signals" [09:00]

**Entry Execution** [08:00-09:00]:
- "Your entry into this trade would be on one of the bullish engulfing candles that formed after the market hit the support level" [09:00]
- Enter immediately after price action pattern closes/confirms
- Can enter on either first or subsequent engulfing candles [08:00-08:30]

### Stop loss
[08:00-09:00]: "Your stop loss on either trade would be placed at the support level which makes up the edge of the demand zone. Or you could have entered a trade with a stop loss placed at the edge of the demand zone itself. Usually having the stop placed at the edge of the zone is the better option, because it will save you from any spikes which may occur after getting your trade." [08:00-08:30]

For sell trades (shorting out of supply zone), stop loss would be at the upper edge of the supply zone.

### Take profit / exit
[09:30-10:00]: "The best time to take profits is after you've seen the market makes a new higher high, if you've got a buy trade placed into the market, or after a new lower low has formed, if you've got a sell trade placed. You either move you stop loss higher or you partially close half of your position." [09:30-10:00]

Specific exit levels: NOT STATED. Video mentions "new higher high" or "new lower low" as profit signals but does not specify target price or distance. Profit taking appears discretionary based on market continuation after zone reversal.

### Invalidation / skip conditions
- Price fails to reverse at any S/R level within zone [05:30-06:00]: "In real time, you wouldn't know which of these levels the market was going to reverse at before the price dropped into it, you would just know that it's almost certain for it to reverse at one of these levels" [06:00]
- Reversal from S/D zone is incomplete: If price reverses at one S/R level but then drops back into zone, wait for next signal [07:30-08:00]
