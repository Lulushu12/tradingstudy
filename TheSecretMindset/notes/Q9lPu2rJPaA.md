## Summary
A Wyckoff candle-volume analysis strategy using doji candlestick patterns combined with volume analysis to predict reversals at market tops and bottoms. The strategy identifies two setups: (1) "doji test bar" at bottoms with declining volume indicating no supply and bullish reversal; (2) "doji demand drying up" at tops with declining volume indicating no demand and bearish reversal. Entry occurs after the doji confirmation with specific profit targets based on doji range size and volume confirmation of smart money abandonment.

- markets: Stocks, S&P futures, Bitcoin mentioned as examples; NOT STATED as universal
- timeframes: 1-hour, 15-minute, 30-minute examples shown; NOT RECOMMENDED: 1-minute and 5-minute charts [13:30]
- sessions/hours: NOT STATED

## Strategy 1: Doji Test Bar (Bottom Reversal)

### Indicators and settings
- Candlestick pattern: Doji (open and close at or very close to same price) [02:00 - 02:30]
- Volume: Measured against prior candles

### Context / bias filter

Market must be in a downtrend with price "starting to make higher highs and higher lows" or "in a slight consolidation" [04:30 - 05:00]. Background: prior high established (used as reference for how far price dropped to test).

**Doji test bar validation criteria** [04:00 - 04:30]:
1. Must have a low that is lower than the previous candles' low (makes new recent low) [04:00]
2. Must display lower volume than at least several of the prior candles [04:00 - 04:30]
3. "The lower the volume, the stronger the indication of no supply being present" [04:30]

**Interpretation** [05:30 - 06:30]: "There are a few sellers at this lower price, and the price comes back to close near the open" [05:30]. "The smart money, which represents a large percentage of the overall trading volume, is not selling as lower price levels are explored. This is clearly evident and is shown by the reduction in the total volume" [06:00 - 06:30].

### Entry trigger

Price must confirm the test bar. Examples show two phases:
1. Price tests lower (doji bar forms on low volume)
2. "Within the next bars, this market started making higher highs and higher lows" [06:00]

**Explicit entry rule stated for demand drying up setup applies here inversely** [11:00 - 11:30]: When you see a doji test bar with low volume at a bottom, enter long as price rises above the doji low, OR enter after price closes above the doji.

Timing note: "Several candles later" the predicted move occurs [06:30]; not immediate at doji close.

### Stop loss

NOT EXPLICITLY STATED for bottom reversal. Implied: place below the low of the doji (the test level itself).

### Take profit / exit

NOT EXPLICITLY STATED for this scenario. The video focuses on entry signals only for the test bar; profit targets are specified only for the demand drying up strategy.

### Invalidation / skip conditions

- Doji must make new recent low (not just any low) [04:00]
- Volume must be lower than several prior candles [04:00 - 04:30]
- If volume is high on doji, the pattern fails (indicates supply is present) [04:30]

## Strategy 2: Doji Demand Drying Up (Top Reversal)

### Indicators and settings
- Candlestick pattern: Doji (open and close at or very close to same price) [02:00 - 02:30]
- Volume: Measured against prior candles

### Context / bias filter

Market must be in an uptrend "making higher highs and higher lows" [09:00]. Background: As price moves up, trading volume decreases, "telling sign that there is no interest in higher prices from the smart money" [08:00].

**Doji demand drying up validation criteria** [08:00 - 08:30]:
1. This candle's close must be higher than the previous candle's close [08:00 - 08:30]
2. Must display lower volume than at least several of the prior candles [08:00 - 08:30]
3. "The lower the volume, the stronger the indication that the demand has dried up" [08:30]

**Interpretation** [09:00 - 10:00]: "As the market moves up to the highest point, the amount of interested buyers is drying up. We know this because the volume is greatly reduced compared to the previous candles. Even though the S&P is making a new recent high, and strong markets don't behave this way. This occurs at the top of the market... the doji indicates that at least this phase of the up move is either close to or at the end" [09:00 - 09:00].

"The smart money is not interested in supporting higher prices. This is evidenced by the reduction in the overall volume" [09:30 - 10:00].

### Entry trigger

"A simple trigger is the low of the doji. Once the candle closes below this level, you can open a short position" [11:00 - 11:30].

"The aggressive entry would be immediately after the candle closes" [11:00 - 11:30].

Timing: "Whenever you see a doji on low volume that appears in the context of a bullish uptrend, this should give you some warnings that a trend correction could come at any time" [10:30 - 11:00].

### Stop loss

"The proper location of a stop loss is above the high of the doji" [11:30].

### Take profit / exit

**Two profit targets:**
1. "A first profit target equals the size or the range of the doji candle. This would give you a roughly 1:1 reward-to-risk ratio" [11:30 - 12:00]
2. "The second profit target is double the size of the doji. This will get you closer to a 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio" [12:30]

**Calculation method**: Measure the range (high-low) of the doji candle. 
- First target = current price minus doji range
- Second target = current price minus (2 × doji range)

**Risk-reward note** [11:30 - 12:30]: "Ideally, you want to have your reward, your take profit, at least double your risk. A first profit target equals the size or the range of the doji candle. This would give you a roughly 1:1 reward-to-risk ratio. Not ideal, but at least you can lock in some profits. The second profit target is double the size of the doji. This will get you closer to a 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio" [11:30 - 12:30].

### Invalidation / skip conditions

- Doji must make new recent high (not just any high) [09:00 - 09:30]
- Volume must be lower than several prior candles [08:00 - 08:30]
- If volume is high on doji, the pattern fails (indicates demand is present) [08:30]
- Avoid 1-minute and 5-minute timeframes: "I advise you, however, to avoid small time frames like the 1-minute or the 5-minute charts because you'll witness many false signals and it will be hard to see the big picture with all that market noise" [13:30]

### Claimed performance

If the smart money is not interested in higher prices, you as a retail trader can take this knowledge and refrain from buying at market tops. Instead, you will establish a short trade into the path of least resistance" [09:30 - 10:00].

"Wyckoff candle volume analysis works in all markets and time frames and precisely reveals the true psychology and the sentiment of the smart money" [13:00 - 13:00].
