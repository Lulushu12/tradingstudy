## Summary
This video teaches trading with Bill Williams fractals, a price action indicator that identifies potential reversal points using a 5-bar pattern. The strategy uses fractals as support (bullish fractals) and resistance (bearish fractals) levels and emphasizes that fractals alone produce many false signals and must be combined with confluence factors such as moving averages, VWAP, trend analysis, or multiple timeframe confirmation. The video covers fractal identification, breaking fractals as trend signals, using fractals for stop loss and take profit placement, and the importance of higher timeframes for reliability.

## Strategy 1: Fractal-Based Support and Resistance Trading

### Indicators and settings
- Fractal pattern (Bill Williams): 5-bar pattern where:
  - Up fractal: middle bar (3rd bar) has higher high than bars 1, 2, 4, 5; creates support level [02:30]
  - Down fractal: middle bar (3rd bar) has lower low than bars 1, 2, 4, 5; creates resistance level [02:30]
- Moving average (optional filter): 50-period exponential moving average mentioned [08:00-08:30]
- Anchored VWAP (optional filter): NOT STATED (mentioned but not detailed) [08:00]

### Context / bias filter
1. Wait for the 5th candle to CLOSE completely before making trading decisions [04:00-04:30]. "Price can move during the time the fifth candle is forming. If price moves either above the higher high or below the lower low, while the pattern is still forming, the fractal indicator will disappear from your price chart." [04:00-04:30]
2. Use trend filtering: Only take fractal signals in the direction of the overall trend [06:30-07:00, 07:30-08:00]
   - In uptrend: Look for up fractals being broken [08:00-08:30]
   - In downtrend: Look for down fractals being broken [06:30-07:00]
3. Combine with moving average: If 50-period EMA is moving upside, ignore down fractals until moving average changes direction [08:00-08:30]
4. Look for confluence: Fractal high/low should coincide with supply/demand areas, pivot points, Fibonacci levels, or round numbers [06:00-06:30]
5. Multiple timeframe approach: Base support/resistance on higher timeframes (H4, daily), but look for entries on lower timeframes (30M, hourly) [11:00-11:30]
6. Multiple fractals: If multiple fractals cluster around same area, that zone is "important" [11:30-12:00]

### Entry trigger
Entry conditions depend on market context:

1. **Broken fractal entry** [05:30-06:00]: Price breaks through a confirmed up or down fractal in direction of trend:
   - Up fractal broken to upside = market is up, expect continuation higher [05:30-06:00]
   - Down fractal broken to downside = market is down, expect continuation lower [05:30-06:00]

2. **Reaction at fractal support/resistance** [11:00-11:30]: Price approaches fractal level from higher timeframe and shows rejection:
   - If in uptrend and price bounces at down fractal support [08:30]
   - If in downtrend and price bounces at up fractal resistance

3. **Entry on lower timeframe after higher timeframe confirmation** [11:00-11:30]: Day trading example uses H4/daily fractal levels as support/resistance zones, then enters on 30M chart when price shows reaction at those levels [11:00-11:30]

4. NOT clearly specified: How to enter after fractal appears; speaker notes signal lags 2 bars [09:30-10:00]

### Stop loss
- For short position: "Set your stop loss level...above the highest fractal" [09:00-09:30]
- General guidance: "Leave a bigger margin, in case there's some sort of liquidity hunt" [09:30]
- General placement: Do not place stop loss "right above or below a fractal" [09:00-09:30]
- NOT PRECISELY SPECIFIED: Exact distance or pip amount for the margin/buffer

### Take profit / exit
- Exit when price hits opposite fractal: "If going long on a fractal, you could exit the position once a price hits a bearish fractal" [09:30-10:00]
- General guidance: "A better approach to take profits is a few points above or below previous fractals" [09:30-10:00]
- NOT clearly specified: Exact point/pip distance for "a few points"
- Avoid exiting on fractal appearance: "I heard some traders close their positions when an opposite fractal appears, but this is a bad money management approach, because the fractal signal will lag by a couple of bars" [09:30-10:00]

### Invalidation / skip conditions
1. Fractal on a single timeframe only (no confluence on higher timeframe): Unreliable [02:30-03:00, 12:00-13:00]
2. Fractal signal lags: "At least two closed bars are needed for a fractal to be completed, meaning that when it provides a signal, the price may be at another level compared to the signal" [09:30-10:00]
3. Fractal below hourly timeframe: "Fractals has practical meaning only as part of a comprehensive strategy, and only for periods of more than one hour" [13:00]
4. Fractal in sideways/range market without trend context: Higher chance of false signals [06:00-06:30]
5. No confluence with other levels: "I wouldn't base my entries on the appearance of a single fractal" [13:00]
6. Fractal where "the market is getting ready to consolidate" - inability to break through previous fractal can signal trend change [07:00-07:30]

## Notable claims and caveats
- "Fractals do not predict the price. They offer lagging signals." [12:30]
- "The arrows for the pattern are typically drawn over the high or low point, which is the middle of the fractal, not where the fractal completes. Therefore, the arrows can be visually deceiving." [12:30-13:00]
- "The pattern is actually completing two bars to the right of the arrow" [12:30-13:00]
- "On their own, fractals will offer many false signals. As a standalone price action tool, fractals are quite unreliable." [05:30-06:00]
- "The main problem with fractals is that there are so many of them." [05:30-06:00]
- "Few traders rely only on the fractal indicator for trading signals, instead they are using it in conjunction with other technical indicators." [13:00-13:30]
- "The longer the time period of the chart, the more reliable the pattern." [13:30]
- "Fractals has practical meaning only as part of a comprehensive strategy, and only for periods of more than one hour." [13:00]
