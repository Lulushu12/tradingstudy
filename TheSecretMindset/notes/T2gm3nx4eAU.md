## Summary

An educational video explaining how institutional "smart money" (market makers, dealers, banks) manipulate markets through stop-loss hunting, false breakouts, and psychological tactics. Covers smart money advantages (seeing all orders, stop-losses, information), their operational strategies (shake-outs, testing, up-thrusts, gap moves), the importance of volume over price, and how they exploit retail trader psychology (fear, greed, herd mentality). The speaker demonstrates common manipulation scenarios (price breaking support then reversing, consolidation before drop to trigger early entries). Recommends Volume Spread Analysis (VSA) as the counter-strategy but does not teach it. Emphasizes aligning with smart money rather than fighting against them.

## Notable smart money tactics and concepts

**Smart Money Advantages** [01:00-02:00]:
- Can "see all the buy and sell orders as they arrive"
- Can see "all the stop-loss orders on their screens"
- Aware of "inside information" (despite illegality) [01:30]
- Fill "large blocks of buy or sell orders" with "special trading techniques to prevent putting the price up"
- Can "see the balance of supply and demand far better than anyone else"

**Smart Money Operational Strategies** [02:00-02:30]:
- Gapping up or gapping down stocks
- Shake-outs
- Testing
- Up-thrusts
- "Moneymaking strategies helping the smart money to trade successfully, at your expense"

**Secrecy Requirement** [03:00-03:30]:
- "Last thing smart money want is for you or anybody else to know that a stock is under accumulation or distribution"
- "Known to go to the extremes, producing false rumors"
- "Actively selling a stock in the open, but secretly buying it all back, and more, through other ways"
- Use news to mask intentions [03:00-03:30]

**Stop-Loss Hunting Tactic** [03:30-04:00]:
- When expecting higher prices: "They want to catch all the stops before moving the price up"
- "Want to buy at the best possible price"
- Method: Produce price drop that triggers stops, then push price up
- Alternative: When expecting drops, use good earnings/fundamentals, then drop price

**Volume and Price Interrelationship** [04:00-04:30]:
- Professional traders create "mass of trades" that change market trend
- "Takes time"
- "Lack of participation as important as active participation"
- "Low volume = 'no demand'" when professionals not interested [04:00-04:30]
- "No demand is a sure sign that the rally will not last long"
- "Activity of professional traders causes noticeable changes in volume—not retail traders"

**News Exploitation** [05:00-05:30]:
- Smart money "use good or bad news to enhance their trading position"
- "Capitalize on known human weaknesses"
- Bad news + smart money interest = rapid markdown that "shakes out weak buyers at lower prices"
- Stops get triggered, allowing purchase at lower prices
- Traders who shorted on bad news get "locked-in by rapid recovery" and forced to cover

**Volume vs. Price** [06:00-06:30]:
- "Main thing smart money want to hide from you is not the price, but the VOLUME"
- "Seeing price gives either fear or hope; knowing volume gives facts"
- More liquid markets = harder to manipulate [06:30]
- Thinly traded markets more susceptible to manipulation
- Smart money needs "windows of opportunity" like "temporary thinning out of trading orders" or "taking advantage of news" [06:30]

**Herd Psychology Exploitation** [07:00-07:30]:
- "Human beings are conditioned to act as a herd"
- Media play "key role in helping investors and traders form opinions"
- "When media-induced opinions are wrong, and 'uninformed Herd' believe them, it will cost them dearly"
- Smart money "know how you will act as an investor or trader"
- "Know how to steer you"
- "Two most powerful human emotions are greed and fear, and those emotions are our worst enemies"

**Smart Money Psychology** [07:30-08:00]:
- "Not evil beings intent on stealing your money"
- "Clever institutions who understand human nature and market structure"
- "View their activity as a game"
- "Have acquired knowledge and skills required to win"
- "Simply take advantage of opportunities"
- "Use financial supremacy and understanding to make profits"

**False Breakout/Support Trick** [10:30-12:00]:
- Scenario: Support level at $50, price currently at $58
- Retail traders have impulsive buy orders at support
- Smart money strategy:
  1. Drive price through support level (appears to break)
  2. Triggers stops of early buyers (sell orders into smart money)
  3. Other retail traders sell the "breakout," believing it's real
  4. Retail traders unknowingly selling TO smart money at discount below support
  5. Smart money then pushes price back up
  6. Stops from fake breakout sellers get triggered, accelerating bullish move
- Result: "Our initial idea of buying support was correct. But at end of day, we lost money" [12:00]

**Greed/Fear Trigger Example** [12:30-13:30]:
- Scenario: Price at $58 falling toward $50 support
- Smart money sees retail impatience during consolidation at $52
- Retail traders "fear that price has already found support and will take off without them"
- Retail traders "greedy" and "hate when price misses orders"
- Smart money knows this psychology and "seduce amateur traders to pull trigger early"
- Smart money allows early longs to form, then drives price further down
- Generates panic selling among long-positioned retail traders
- Smart money covers the shorts at retail's forced exit prices

**Stop-Loss as Liquidity** [09:30]:
- "Stop loss orders represent liquidity in the markets"
- "Big players need liquidity"
- "Cannot just enter trade at once"
- "Slowly build position by hunting for liquidity"
- "Stop loss orders in the markets are the best way to get liquidity"

**Trading Myths Rejected** [10:00-10:30]:
- Myth 1: "More obvious a support or level is, the stronger and more reliable the signals around it"
- Myth 2: "Put your buy order right at support level and your stop right below it"
- Reality: Smart money needs sellers to buy; if everyone buys at support, who sells?
- Smart money must "seduce traders to take trades in the wrong direction"
