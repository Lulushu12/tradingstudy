## Concept: Supply and Demand Zone Identification (Wyckoff Market Structure)

### How to Identify Supply and Demand Zones [00:00-00:30]

**Supply Zone** [00:00-00:30]: "Found when the market makes a large move down from either a single candle or small consolidation structure, also known as a base." Mark the area from the OPEN of the LAST bullish candle before the drop to the most recent high before the drop.

**Demand Zone** [00:00-00:30]: "Found when the market makes a large move up, again, from a single candle or a base." Mark the area from the OPEN of the LAST bearish candle before the up move to the most recent low before the up move.

### Four Types of Market Structures [01:00-06:00]

**Continuation Patterns:**

1. **Rally-Base-Rally** [01:00-01:30]: Market rallies, consolidates (base), then rallies again. Forms a demand zone. "A rally-base-rally will always form a demand zone; it can never create a supply zone." [01:30].

2. **Drop-Base-Drop** [01:30-02:00]: Market drops, consolidates (base), then drops again. Forms a supply zone. "A drop-base-drop will always form a supply zone, a zone which if the market returns to, should push the price back in the direction of the movement that created the zone." [01:30-02:00].

**Reversal Patterns:**

3. **Drop-Base-Rally** [05:00-05:30]: Market drops, consolidates, then rallies upward. Forms a demand zone. "It's a reversal pattern." [05:00-05:30].

4. **Rally-Base-Drop** [05:30-06:00]: Market rallies, consolidates, then drops. Forms a supply zone. "Again, a reversal structure." [05:30-06:00].

### Mechanics of Zone Creation [02:00-04:30]

**Rally-Base-Rally and Drop-Base-Drop** [02:00-04:00]: These form during trending markets due to bank traders taking profits. In a rally-base-rally: banks place long trades, then when profitable, consume buy orders from retail traders, causing a small pullback (base). Retail traders exit long trades at a loss (creating sell orders), and new retail traders enter shorts thinking it's a reversal. Banks then buy again, forcing shorts to cover at a loss and continuing the rally. The inverse happens in drop-base-drop.

**Drop-Base-Rally and Rally-Base-Drop** [05:30-08:00]: These form either because banks are placing trades to force reversals OR taking profits off existing positions. "Very important: The rally-base-drop/drop-base-rally zones which form when bank traders taking profits have a lower probability of resulting in successful trades than zones that are created because of bank traders placing trades, wanting the market to reverse." [06:00].

### Quality Indicators for Reversal Zones [08:00-11:00]

**Distinguishing reversal-intent zones from profit-taking zones** [08:00-09:30]: Analyze the rally/drop that creates the zone by looking at candle size and wicks. "The size of the candlesticks which form the rally/drop along with understanding where the wicks are on the candles, can give us the majority of the information needed to find out why the zone has been created." [08:30]. 

- Reversal-intent zones: rally/drop candles are large and bold with few or no wicks on the opposite side [09:00-09:30].
- Profit-taking zones: rally/drop candles are small or contain wicks showing resistance/selling [08:30-09:00].

**Importance of trend length** [09:30]: "The banks will only cause a reversal when there are enough retail traders for them to make a lot of money off... the banks will not be able to make a lot of money if they cause the market to reverse, because only a small amount of traders will be liquidating their losing positions." [09:30].

**Swing high/low break filter** [10:30-11:00]: "If you plan to trade rally-base-drop/drop-base-rally zones the best piece of advice I can give you is make sure the zone your trading breaks a recent swing high for drop-base-rally zones or a recent swing low for rally-base-drop zones. If the rally in a drop-base-rally breaks an old swing high there is a much higher chance the zone will result in you having a successful trade than if no swing high was broken." [10:30-11:00].
