## Divergence Pattern Recognition (Conceptual)

### Regular Divergence Patterns (Reversal Signals)

**Regular Bearish Divergence** [02:00-03:00]:
- Occurs during uptrend
- Price makes higher highs
- Indicator records lower highs
- Interpretation: Market exhaustion, possible reversal or short-term correction

**Regular Bullish Divergence** [03:00-03:30]:
- Occurs during downtrend
- Price makes lower lows
- Indicator records higher lows
- Interpretation: Possible reversal or short-term bounce

### Hidden Divergence Patterns (Continuation Signals)

**Bullish Hidden Divergence** [04:00-05:00]:
- Occurs during uptrend/collection
- Price makes higher low or double-bottom low
- Indicator makes lower low
- Interpretation: Upward continuation expected

**Bearish Hidden Divergence** [05:00-05:30]:
- Occurs during downtrend/correction
- Price makes lower high or double-top high
- Indicator makes higher high
- Interpretation: Downward continuation expected
