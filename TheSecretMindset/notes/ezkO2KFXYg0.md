### Wyckoff Accumulation Phase Overview [01:00-07:00]

**Accumulation Events** (in order):
1. **PS (Preliminary Support)**: Substantial buying begins to provide support after prolonged downtrend; volume increases, price spread widens [01:30]
2. **SC (Selling Climax)**: Widening spread and selling pressure climaxes; panic selling by public absorbed by smart money at bottom; price often closes well off the low [02:00]
3. **AR (Automatic Rally)**: Intense selling pressure diminished; wave of buying easily pushes prices up; high helps define upper boundary of accumulation range [02:00]
4. **ST (Secondary Test)**: Price revisits area of selling climax; if bottom confirmed, volume and spread should be significantly diminished vs. the climax [02:30]
5. **SOS (Sign of Strength)**: Price advance on increasing spread and relatively higher volume; often occurs after a spring [03:00]
6. **LPS (Last Point of Support)**: Low point of reaction/pullback after SOS; makes higher low then turns upward; final drive to bottom before uptrend begins [03:00]
7. **BU (Back-up)**: Common structural element before substantial price markup; can take form of simple pullback or new trading range at higher level [03:30]

**Accumulation Phases**:
- **Phase A** [03:30-05:00]: PS, SC, AR, ST events set boundaries of trading range; widening spread and heavy volume on climax and decline; narrowing spread and decreased volume on secondary test
- **Phase B** [05:00-05:30]: Smart money accumulating at low prices; multiple secondary tests; upthrust actions at upper end of range; wide price swings, high volume initially, then volume diminishes on downswings
- **Phase C** [05:30-06:00]: Decisive test of remaining supply; spring occurs (price drops below support then quickly reverses - bear trap); spring is high-probability trading opportunity
- **Phase D** [06:30-07:00]: Advances (SOS) on widening spreads and increasing volume; LPS on smaller spreads and diminished volumes = excellent place to initiate longs; price moves to top of trading range minimum
- **Phase E** [07:30]: Price leaves trading range; demand in full control; markup obvious

### Wyckoff Distribution Phase Overview [07:30-13:00]

**Distribution Events** (in order):
1. **PSY (Preliminary Supply)**: Smart money begins to unload shares after pronounced up-move; volume expands, spread widens [07:30]
2. **BC (Buying Climax)**: Increases in volume and spread; force of buying reaches climax; heavy/urgent buying by public filled by smart money near top; often coincides with good news [08:00]
3. **AR (Automatic Reaction)**: With intense buying diminished and heavy supply continuing; selloff occurs; low helps define lower boundary of distribution range [08:30]
4. **ST (Secondary Test)**: Price revisits area of buying climax to test demand/supply balance; for top to be confirmed, volume and spread should decrease as price approaches resistance [09:00]
5. **UT (Upthrust)**: Price moves above resistance (buying climax), possibly above secondary tests, then quickly reverses to close below resistance [09:00]
6. **SOW (Sign of Weakness)**: Down-move to lower boundary of trading range, usually on increased spread and volume [09:30]
7. **LPSY (Last Point of Supply)**: After testing support on SOW, rally on narrow spread shows difficulty advancing; exhaustion of demand and last waves of smart money distribution before markdown begins [10:00]
8. **UTAD (Upthrust After Distribution)**: In latter stages of range; definitive test of new demand after breakout above range resistance; NOT required element [10:30]

**Distribution Phases**:
- **Phase A** [10:30-11:00]: Stopping of prior uptrend; PSY and BC followed by AR and ST; may terminate without climactic action, showing exhaustion with decreasing spread/volume
- **Phase B** [11:30]: Smart money reviews portfolios and initiates shorts; tilting of supply/demand balance toward supply; SOW followed by significantly increased spread and volume to downside
- **Phase C** [11:30-12:00]: Most interesting phase; retail traders go long (trap); aggressive traders open shorts after upthrust (good risk/reward); smart money repeatedly triggers upthrusts to hunt stops; conservative traders wait for Phase D
- **Phase D** [12:30]: Last signs of demand; price goes to or through trading range support; supply clearly dominant on break of support or decline below mid-point after upthrust/UTAD; **best strategy: short at LPS** [12:30]
- **Phase E** [13:00]: Supply in control; price leaves trading range; rally that fails at or near support = high-probability short entry [13:00]
