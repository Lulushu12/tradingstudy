## Summary
This video discusses market structure and smart money tactics rather than a specific mechanizable strategy. It argues that traditional support/resistance levels are often manipulation traps and that multiple retests of a level weaken it. The speaker teaches concepts like liquidity clear-outs, the difference between professional and retail entry points, consolidation traps, and the importance of trading with market momentum rather than against it. No complete trading strategy is presented.

## Key concepts and observations

### Traditional Support/Resistance Myth [00:30-02:30]
Dispels the belief: "The more times a level is tested, the more valid it is" [00:30]

**Reality [01:00-01:30]**:
- Really strong support/resistance levels immediately push price away (strong buying/selling pressure)
- If price can hang around level or quickly comes back, suggests lack of conviction [01:00]
- Multiple retests indicate buyers/sellers are getting exhausted [01:30-02:00]

**When levels break [01:30-02:00]**:
- Orders at support level absorbed each retest until buyers lose power
- Price then plunges through zone with little difficulty
- Sellers' orders gradually filled until less sellers remain; price breaks through

**Speaker's preference [02:00-03:00]**:
"I avoid buying a support which has been tested multiple times in the past" [02:00]
"The greater the number of times a zone is tested, the more likely it is to break" [02:30]

### Liquidity Clear-Out Entry [02:30-03:30]

**Preferred entry pattern [03:00-03:30]**:
Price drops to support and quickly moves up, leaving a long shadow on the candle that dropped through support [03:00]
"I would much rather buy a broken support, after a liquidity clear-out, than clean support… that trade is 10 times better (to me)" [03:00]

This suggests a wick/tail below support that gets quickly rejected back up.

### Strong Imbalance Between Buyers and Sellers [03:30-04:30]

When price leaves support/resistance and starts trending [03:30]:
- Strong imbalance = strong and explosive price movements
- Rule of thumb: "The stronger the movement away from a level, the more interest will usually still exist – especially when time spent at level was relatively short" [03:30-04:00]
- Next time price revisits area, "I would be very interested to buy that support" [04:00]
- Look for extremely strong turning points; these are high probability levels [04:00]

### Smart Money Manipulation Tactics [04:30-05:30]

**Why large traders manipulate [05:00-05:30]**:
- Smart money uses massive position sizes; cannot simply buy/sell like retail
- Must enter over period of time to avoid spiking price and revealing position
- Must seek areas with sufficient liquidity
- Large orders reside around support and resistance zones [05:30]

### Retail vs Smart Money Entry Timing [05:30-07:00]

**Retail trader pattern [05:30-06:30]**:
- Buy when price breaks previous resistance at new high
- Soon after entry, price moves lower from high
- Retail trader gets stopped out
- At exact same time, professionals entering

**Key differences [06:30-07:00]**:
- Retail: buy at support
- Smart money: buy below support (after breakdown)
- Professionals cover longs and take profit when price moves higher
- Retail traders just starting to enter long at that point [06:30]

**Consequence [07:00]**:
"Entering market when smart money getting out is not a smart plan" [07:00]

### Value Area Entry Strategy [07:00-08:00]

Trading mantra: "Buy any weakness and sell any strength" [07:00]
- If price moves lower from extreme high: look to buy from value area [07:00]
- If price moves higher from extreme low: sell from value area [07:00]

Example: "If you entered long trade from the high, you would have been stopped out. But if instead entered when price showed weakness and moved back lower, you would have entered at much better area" [07:30-08:00]

### The Consolidation Trap [08:00-09:30]

**How it works [08:00-08:30]**:
- Price consolidates for extended period
- Masses place support/resistance at extremes of consolidation zone
- Any sudden breakout tempts traders to enter
- These are traps set by market makers [08:30]

**Indicator trap [09:00-09:30]**:
- In consolidation, moving averages get close together
- Common entry signal: when 2 moving averages cross
- Breakout + crossover signal retail traders that trend changed (false signal) [09:30]
- Smart money uses this to fill massive sell positions [09:30]
- "By time indicators signal change to downtrend, market has already made most of its move" [10:00]

### Retail Patterns Exploited for Liquidity [10:30-11:30]

**How smart money creates liquidity [10:30-11:00]**:
- Create well-known retail chart patterns to involve retail traders
- These patterns induce retail traders to take positions
- Liquidity is just fancy word for trader taking position [10:00]
- More people taking positions = more liquidity for big players [10:30]

**Example [11:00-11:30]**:
Triangle patterns: retail sees as continuation, smart money sees as reversal
"How many times have you traded a triangle pattern, just for price to do exact opposite?" [11:00]

### Smart Money Strategy Principles [11:30-12:30]

- Always searching for pockets of liquidity [11:30-12:00]
- Always looking to shake out weak hands around support/resistance [12:00]
- Think like hunter, not prey [11:30]
- Start building strategy to join smart money after liquidity clear-out [12:00-12:30]

### Trading Direction of Recent Momentum [12:30-13:30]

**Mistake to avoid [12:30-13:00]**:
Most S/R traders try to predict where movement will end instead of trading in direction of movement itself. Mark levels market hasn't encountered, wait for price to enter zones.

**Better approach [13:00-13:30]**:
"Start taking trades at levels which formed in direction of most recent high or low" [13:00-13:30]
New high or low signals current momentum is up or down. Trading against this momentum "unnecessarily puts yourself at risk of losing money because you're trying to trade against trend" [13:30]

### Big Round Numbers [13:30-14:30]

"One of the best places to find high probability support and resistance levels is around big round number levels" [13:30-14:00]
Levels around round numbers "have high probability of causing large reversal" [14:00]
"Many big reversals will begin once price has either almost hit big round number, or just moved beyond it" [14:00-14:30]
