### Take profit / exit

**Method 1: Scaling at Previous Swing Highs/Lows**
- Take partial profits when price reaches a previous swing high or low [07:00]
- Reduce exposure and re-evaluate trend continuation [07:30]
- Re-evaluate if price fails to break to new extremes [07:30]

**Method 2: Scaling at Supply/Demand**
- Sell portion of position at supply level when price has risen to it [08:30]
- Increase account efficiency by freeing capital for new trades [09:00]

**Method 3: Using ATR/ADR**
- Use average daily range or ATR to estimate how much profit potential exists [09:30]
- Example: if ADR is 60 pips on Forex pair, don't expect 100 pips profit [09:30]

**Method 4: Fibonacci Extensions**
- Exit portions at 100% extension level (some profit locked), then 161% (more profit) [10:30]
- Move stop up after hitting extensions [10:30]

**Method 5: VWAP/Bollinger Bands**
- Take profits when price approaches upper VWAP band or Bollinger Band [11:30]
- More relevant in ranging markets than trending markets [14:00]

**Method 6: Pivot Points**
- Exit at resistance pivot point levels [12:30]
- More useful in ranging markets [14:00]

**Method 7: End-of-Day/Week**
- Scale out before end of session to reduce weekend/overnight gap risk [13:00-13:30]
- Day traders scale if in profit at session end [13:00]
- Swing traders reduce risk before weekend [13:30]
