### The Five Ichimoku Components

**Component 1: Conversion Line (Tenkan-Sen)** [00:30-01:30]
- Calculation: Midpoint of high and low over last 9 periods (default value) [00:30]
- Represents: Short-term price momentum [00:30]
- Signals minor support/resistance [00:30-01:00]
- Interpretation:
  - Price above Conversion Line = short-term upward momentum, focus on buy signals [01:00-01:30]
  - Price below Conversion Line = short-term downward momentum, search for sell signals [01:00-01:30]
  - Upward slope = upward trend [01:00-01:30]
  - Downward slope = downward trend [01:00-01:30]
- Note: "Not typically used on its own but rather used in conjunction with the other elements" [01:30]

**Component 2: Base Line (Kijun-Sen / Confirmation Line)** [01:30-02:30]
- Calculation: Midpoint of high and low over last 26 periods [01:30]
- Represents: Medium-term support/resistance levels [01:30]
- Interpretation:
  - Price above Base Line = upward bias [02:00]
  - Price below Base Line = downward bias [02:00]
  - Upward slope = upward trend [02:00]
  - Downward slope = downward trend [02:00]
- Caveat: "Unless there is a very strong trend, the base line will often appear near the price. When the base Line is often intersecting or near the price, it is not as useful for helping to evaluate the trend direction." [02:30]

**Component 3: Lagging Span (Chikou Span)** [02:30-04:00]
- Calculation: Closing prices plotted 26 periods behind (26 days in the past) [02:30-03:00]
- Purpose: Allow traders to visualize relationship between current and prior trends; spot potential trend reversals [02:30-03:00]
- Interpretation:
  - Price below Lagging Span = usually indication of strength, price moving higher [03:00-03:30]
  - Price above Lagging Span = usually indication of weakness [03:00-03:30]
  - Lagging Span crossing above prior price line = bullish confirmation [03:00-03:30]
  - Lagging Span crossing below prior price line = bearish confirmation [03:00-03:30]
- Signal strength:
  - Strongest signals when Lagging Span doesn't touch or cross prior candles [03:30-04:00]
  - Touching/crossing prior candles = choppy or sideways market [03:30-04:00]
  - Lagging Span descending quickly into past price line = sign of exhaustion [03:30-04:00]

**Component 4: Kumo Cloud (Leading Span A and Leading Span B)** [04:00-05:30]
- Composition: Cloud formed from two lines
  - Leading Span A (green): Average of Conversion Line and Base Line [04:00-04:30]
  - Leading Span B (red): Midpoint of high and low over 52 periods [04:00-04:30]
- Forward shift: Cloud is plotted 26 periods ahead (future-looking support/resistance) [04:30-05:00]
- Trend indication:
  - Uptrend: Leading Span A (green) is rising and above Leading Span B (red) = green cloud [04:00-04:30]
  - Downtrend: Leading Span A (green) is falling and below Leading Span B (red) = red cloud [04:00-04:30]
- Cloud thickness:
  - Thick cloud = strong support/resistance levels [04:30-05:00]
  - Thin cloud = weak S/R levels [04:30-05:00]
- Price position interpretation:
  - Price above Kumo = uptrend in place, more buying opportunities [05:00-05:30]
  - Price below Kumo = under resistance, look for shorts instead of longs [05:00-05:30]
  - Longer price stays above/below Kumo = stronger the trend and more support/resistance the Kumo offers [05:00-05:30]
- Avoid trading inside Kumo: "Signals a period of indecision" [05:30]

### Trading Signals

**Signal 1: Conversion Line / Base Line Crossover** [06:00-07:30]
- Buy Signal: Conversion Line crosses above Base Line [06:00]
- Sell Signal: Conversion Line crosses below Base Line [06:00]
- Signal strength depends on Kumo cloud position:
  - Strongest buy signal: upside crossover while prices are above the cloud [06:30-07:00]
  - Weak buy signal: upside crossover while prices are below the cloud [06:30-07:00]
  - Strongest sell signal: downside crossover while prices are below the cloud [06:30]
  - Weak sell signal: downside crossover while prices are above the cloud [06:30]

**Enhancing Crossover Signal Strength with Lagging Span** [07:30-08:00]:
- Stronger sell signal: downside crossover + Lagging Span below price candles [07:30-08:00]
- Higher probability buy signal: upside crossover + Lagging Span above prices [07:30-08:00]
- Principle: "Crossover in the direction of the overall trend is considered more powerful than one that is against the trend" [08:00]

**Signal 2: Kumo Cloud Span Line Crossover** [08:30-09:00]
- Leading Span A crosses Leading Span B (cloud crossover):
  - Strong buy signal: Span A crosses above Span B + prices positioned above Kumo cloud [08:30-09:00]
  - Strong sell signal: Span A crosses below Span B + prices positioned below Kumo cloud [08:30-09:00]
  - Weak buy signal: Span A crosses above Span B + prices positioned below Kumo cloud [08:30-09:00]
  - Weak sell signal: Span A crosses below Span B + prices positioned above Kumo cloud [09:00]

**Signal 3: Kumo Cloud Breakout** [09:00-09:30]
- Bullish signal: Price enters Kumo cloud and breaks upper line [09:00-09:30]
- Bearish signal: Price enters Kumo and breaks lower line [09:00-09:30]
- Reversal probability increases when:
  - Price is clearly above or below cloud before breakout [09:00-09:30]
  - Thickness of Kumo when broken [09:00-09:30]
  - Amount of time price action has been on one side of cloud [09:00-09:30]
  - Distance price has moved before touching/breaking Kumo [09:00-09:30]
  - Timeframe being analyzed [09:00-09:30]

### Ichimoku Parameters

**Default Settings** [10:00-10:30]:
- Period 1: 9 (represents one week of trading) [10:00-10:30]
- Period 2: 26 (represents one month of trading days) [10:00-10:30]
- Period 3: 52 (represents two months of trading days) [10:00-10:30]
- Historical context: Based on when Japanese markets traded 6 days/week; now trade 5 days/week [10:00-10:30]
- Alternative suggestion: Some practitioners use 7, 22, 44 [10:00-10:30]
- Speaker's note: "In my experience, however, the traditional default settings of nine, 26 and 52 have performed the best" [10:00-10:30]

### Limitations and Usage Tips

**Indicator Limitations** [11:00-11:30]:
- "The indicator can make a chart look busy with all the lines" [11:00]
- Based on historical data only; "nothing in the formula that is predictive. Averages are simply being plotted in the future" [11:00-11:30]
- "Cloud can also become irrelevant for long periods of time, as the price remains way above or below it" [11:00-11:30]
- In such cases, "conversion line, base line, and their crossovers become more important, as they generally stick closer to the price" [11:00-11:30]

**Customization** [11:00]:
- "Depending on your trading needs, certain lines can be hidden" [11:00]
- Example: Hide all lines except Leading Span A and B (keep cloud only) to reduce visual clutter [11:00]
- "Focus on which lines provide the most information for you, and then consider hiding the rest if all the lines are distracting" [11:00]
