## Summary
A framework for reading price action using High-Low charts (TradingView feature showing only high/low of each bar, removing open/close and color bias). The system simplifies candlestick analysis to four types (bullish, bearish, inside, engulfing) and applies six rules to identify swings and trend direction. The video demonstrates two trading applications: (1) support/resistance breakout + retest, entering on new upswing formation, and (2) liquidity clear-out plays, entering after failed breakouts in the direction of the main trend. Emphasis on reducing market noise and identifying true swings for real-time trading.

## High-Low Chart Foundation [00:30 - 02:00]

**Chart type**: "Bars are built based on the value of the maximum and minimum prices for a given timeframe. The price values for which the chart was built are displayed on each bar, below and above the bar" [00:30].

**Benefits** [01:00 - 02:00]:
"This type of chart basically removes the open and close price, and instead, you see the range in which the value changed, on your timeframe" [01:00].

"By having the same color on up and down candles, you'll actually trade in a neutral/unbiased state of mind" [01:00 - 01:30].

"If you are analyzing highs and lows in price, this is very helpful... With the High Low chart, reading price action of individual candlesticks is super easy" [01:30 - 02:00].

**Momentum identification** [02:00 - 02:30]:
"When the high and the low of the recent candlestick is higher than the high and low of the previous candlestick, market is in a bullish momentum and buyers are controlling the market" [02:00].

"When the low and high of the recent candlestick is lower than the low and high of the previous candlestick, market is in a bearish momentum and sellers are controlling the market" [02:00].

## Four Candlestick Formations [02:30 - 04:00]

### Formation 1: Bullish Candle [03:00]
"A bullish candle has a higher high and a higher low compared to the previous bar" [03:00].

### Formation 2: Bearish Candle [03:00 - 03:30]
"Bearish candlesticks have a lower high and a lower low, again, compared to the previous bar" [03:00 - 03:30].

### Formation 3: Inside Candlestick [03:30]
"Has a lower high and higher low relative to the previous bar. This bar is contained within the range of the previous candlestick. Even if the high or the low are equal to the previous candlestick, it's still an inside bar" [03:30].

### Formation 4: Engulfing Candlestick [03:30 - 04:00]
"Has higher high and lower low relative to the previous bar. And even if either the high or the low are equal to that of the previous bar, it's still an engulfing bar" [03:30 - 04:00].

## Six Price Action Rules [06:00 - 07:00]

**Rule 1: Consecutive Bullish Bars** [06:00 - 06:30]
"If you have consecutive bullish bars, you are in a minor uptrend" [06:00 - 06:30].

**Rule 2: Consecutive Bearish Bars** [06:00 - 06:30]
"Consecutive bearish bars indicate a minor downtrend" [06:00 - 06:30].

**Rule 3: Bullish Candle Starts/Confirms** [06:30]
"A bullish candle starts an upswing and confirms the end of a downswing" [06:30].

**Rule 4: Bearish Candle Starts/Confirms** [06:30]
"A bearish candle starts a downswing and confirms the end of an upswing" [06:30].

**Rule 5: Inside Candlesticks (Neutral)** [06:30 - 07:00]
"Inside candlesticks are neutral bars. They don't affect the direction of the current swing, because they do not break the previous high and low. So an inside bar after a bullish or bearish candlestick don't change the current trend" [06:30 - 07:00].

**Rule 6: Engulfing Candlesticks** [07:00]
"Engulfing candlesticks introduce uncertainty into the market structure, because they break both the previous high and low. But, we'll give the benefit of the doubt to the current swing. So, engulfing candlesticks in an upswing continue the upswing. Engulfing candlesticks in a downswing continue the downswing" [07:00].

## Strategy 2: Liquidity Clear-Out + Trend Direction Entry [11:30 - 13:30]

### Context / bias filter

"Trade liquidity clear-outs in the direction of the main trend. Markets hunt for stop orders and activity beyond significant price levels. If this breakout occurs and fails, there will be many trapped traders, which can add momentum to the opposite direction from that level" [11:30 - 12:00].

"The best trades of this type occur in direction of the main trend" [12:00].

### Example 1: Bearish Clear-Out (Downtrend Context) [12:00 - 12:30]

**Setup**: "Clear downtrend, with price currently in consolidation" [12:00 - 12:30].

**False breakout/liquidity grab**: "For a short entry, the market trades above this resistance area, and immediately reverses on the following bars and closes back under the resistance. There no real continuation beyond the level" [12:00 - 12:30].

**Entry trigger**: "When the price forms a bearish candlestick, which ends the previous upswing, we can take a short trade, after this liquidity clear-out, in the direction of the main trend" [12:00 - 12:30].

**Stop loss**: "Stop goes above the previous swing high" [12:30].

**Take profit**: "Target this next swing low in the price structure" [12:30].

### Example 2: Bullish Clear-Out (Uptrend Context) [12:30 - 13:30]

**Setup**: "Clear support, in an obvious uptrend, with the price being unable to continue lower" [12:30].

**False breakout/liquidity grab**: "Small breach below support, but price immediately closed back above the support level" [13:00].

**Entry trigger**: "Once the movement was confirmed by a bullish candle, the wiser trade would be a long trade, instead of chasing the failed breakout" [13:00].

**Stop loss**: "Stop loss is placed below the previous swing low" [13:00 - 13:30].

**Take profit**: "Target this next swing high in the price structure" [13:00 - 13:30].

## Mechanizability

PARTIAL - The High-Low chart formation detection (bullish, bearish, inside, engulfing based on high/low comparison) is fully mechanizable. The six swing rules are mechanical (consecutive bars of same type, single bar confirmation). Support/resistance level identification can be automated (recent highs/lows). However, both trading strategies lack specificity: (1) S/R strategy requires defining "small consolidation" before "valid breakout," "most recent swing" for stop, and "next resistance" for target; (2) liquidity clear-out requires precise detection of "immediately reverses" and "closes back within range." Entry signals are defined (bullish/bearish candle at specific location) but risk-reward targets rely on undefined "next swing" measurements. At least 8-10 parameters must be assumed before backtesting.
