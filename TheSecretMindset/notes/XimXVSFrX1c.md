- Engulfing gap: candle opens lower than prior close but closes higher than prior open (bullish) or opens higher than prior close but closes lower than prior open (bearish) [00:30]

- Context: gap alignment with trend (engulfing gaps with trend are stronger than gaps against trend) [01:30]
- Gap type classification: breakaway gaps (start of new trend), runaway gaps (during strong trend), exhaustion gaps (end of trend) [02:00-02:30]

## Strategy 4: Exhaustion Wick Illusion

### Indicators and settings

- Pin bar: candle with long wick, representing price that tried to move one direction but changed its mind [15:00-15:30]

### Context / bias filter

- "When they show up after big price moves, they might not be as reliable as you think" [15:30]
- Filter 1: "Look at the bigger picture. Pay attention to where the pin bar forms. Is it at an important level? Maybe a support or resistance level. Or at a fresh, unmitigated supply or demand area that's been important in the past?" [16:30-17:00]
- Filter 2: "Does the price quickly move in the direction the pin bar suggests? Or does it just sit there, not doing much? A real reversal signal should lead to some follow-through." [17:00-17:30]
- Filter 3: "Volume can be a helpful clue too. If you see a pin bar with a lot more trading volume than usual, it might be more significant." [17:30]
- Filter 4: "Sometimes, what looks like a reversal is just the market taking a breather." [17:30-18:00]
- Filter 5: Session timing: "pin bars that form at the start of a new trading session might be more meaningful" [18:00-18:30]
  - London opening: "major Forex hub... there's often a burst of activity" [18:30-19:00]
  - New York opening: "these are times when big players enter the market. Their trades can really move the market." [19:00]
  - Session overlap (London and New York both open): "very active time... might be worth paying extra attention to" [19:30]
  - End of sessions: traders closing positions, can lead to price moves [19:30-20:00]

## Strategy 5: Buying/Selling Climaxes

- Selling climax signs [25:00-25:30]:

  - Volume is super high [22:30]
  - Price bars are narrow [22:30]

### Context / bias filter
