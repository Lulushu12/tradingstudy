## Summary

This video covers position sizing and risk management principles rather than a trading strategy. It explains why position size is more important than entry/exit selection and presents three position-sizing approaches: fixed percentage per trade (e.g., 1% per trade), fixed-dollar amount per trade, and volatility-based position sizing using standard deviation. The speaker emphasizes maintaining maximum drawdown limits (e.g., 25% total from 10 consecutive losses = 2% per trade) and the principle of adjusting position size to match predetermined risk levels rather than adjusting stops to fit a desired position size.
